# Protective characteristics of an arrester (§2.39)

In [[iec-99-4]] the protective characteristics of a gapless
[[metal-oxide-surge-arrester]] are the combination of the following **residual
voltages** (§2.39) — there is no gap sparkover, so unlike the gapped arrester of
[[iec-99-1]] (see [[protective-characteristics-arrester]]) the protective levels
are residual voltages only:

- a) **Residual voltage for the [[steep-current-impulse|steep current impulse]]**
  per §7.3.1.
- b) **Residual voltage versus discharge current characteristic for
  [[lightning-current-impulse|lightning impulses]]** per §7.3.2. The **lightning
  impulse protection level** is the maximum [[residual-voltage]] at the
  [[nominal-discharge-current]] (read from the residual-voltage-versus-discharge-
  current curve).
- c) **Residual voltage for the [[switching-current-impulse|switching impulse]]**
  per §7.3.3. The **switching impulse protection level** is the maximum residual
  voltage at the specified switching impulse currents (Table 3).

These protection levels are the design's headline protective values: they set the
[[insulation-withstand-test|housing insulation withstand]] requirement (the
lightning level × 1,3, the switching level × 1,25; §5.1) and are the quantities
compared against the insulation strength of the protected equipment in insulation
co-ordination (IEC 71). They are quoted with enquiries and tenders (Annex G).
Typical maximum residual voltages are tabulated in Annex K.
