# Interconnection diagram and table (IEC 1082-3 §4)

The [[connection-documents-iec1082|connection document]] that provides the
information about the **connections *among* different constructional units** of
equipment or installations (IEC 1082-3 Section 4, §4.1). It is the counterpart of
the [[unit-connection-diagram]]: the unit diagram shows what happens *inside* a
unit, the interconnection diagram shows the cabling *between* units. Information
about the internal connections within the units need not be included, but
references may be given (to the relevant unit connection diagram/table, or to
internal components by their item designations). This is the IEC 1082 successor of
the *interconnection diagram* of the superseded **IEC 113-5** (compare the older,
purpose-tree [[interconnection-diagram|IEC 113-1 interconnection diagram]]).

**Layout (§4.2).** All devices and connections should be shown **as though they
were all in one plane**.

**Worked examples (§4.3).**
- **fig. 8** — an interconnection diagram in **[[multi-line-representation]]**; the
  cable end of `-W109` is supplemented with an **item designation for the remote
  end**.
- **fig. 9** — the *same* equipment using **partly [[single-line-representation]]**.
- **fig. 10** — an interconnection diagram in which the **prefabricated cable `-W3`
  is terminated by a connector at each end**.
- **fig. 11** — the cable of fig. 10 in single-line representation, the individual
  connections supplemented with the **kind of current and voltage** (e.g. `~230V`).
- **fig. 12** — a **connection-oriented interconnection table** for the same
  installation as fig. 8.

Devices and conductors are identified by their
[[reference-designation-system|item designations]] (`-W109`, `+A1`, `+A2`, …) and
remote-end designations, consistent with the rest of the document set. A
[[terminal-connection-diagram]] for a set of units carries the **same information
in the same form** as an interconnection diagram for the same connections (§5.1).
