# Adhesion (encapsulation "m")

Where parts are **not completely embedded** in the [[encapsulation-compound|compound]]
— for example a printed-circuit board that is only partially encapsulated — the
adhesion of the compound to those parts must be assured (IEC 79-18 §5.7). The bond is
what keeps a continuous, gas-tight, moisture-tight interface where the compound meets
a protruding part, so that the protection of [[type-of-protection-m|encapsulation "m"]]
is not undermined at that join.

The standard requires the adhesion to be secured **by a procedure specified by the
manufacturer of the assembly** — such as casting, glueing and/or varnishing — preceded
where necessary by an **etching of the surface**, in such a way that **no moisture can
enter between the compound and the parts**. Compliance is verified after the
[[thermal-tests|thermal tests of §8.2.1]]: **no visible separation shall be detectable**
following those tests. This complements the §8.2.1.3 acceptance criteria (no cracks,
flaking, exposure of encapsulated parts, etc.) and the entry seal of
[[external-connections-and-sealing]] (§5.5).
