# Short-circuit test procedure

Sub-clause 6.105 of [[iec-56]] governs how the [[short-circuit-test]] series is executed. Time intervals between operations follow the [[rated-operating-sequence]] (4.104); the nominal 3 min interval may extend to 10 min for plant reasons (once per series longer) but not be shorter than 2 min, with actual intervals recorded (6.105.1). For breaking tests, auxiliary opening-release power is applied after short-circuit initiation where practicable, demonstrating the breaker is not pre-tripped (6.105.2); in make-break tests it is not applied until the breaker is closed and at least one half-cycle after contact make (6.105.3). Satisfactory latching on the [[rated-short-circuit-making-current]] must be proved, repeated at reduced voltage with negligible a.c. decrement if needed (6.105.4). Invalid parts of a duty cycle may be repeated without reconditioning (6.105.5). The duties themselves and their currents are defined in [[test-duty]] ([[iec-56]] 6.106).

**Amended (1992, Amd. 1):** [[iec-56-amendment-1]] amends 6.105.1, fixing the time intervals between operations of a test-sequence to those of the [[rated-operating-sequence]] (4.104), and rewrites the short-arcing-time procedure 6.102.9 (three valid breaking operations independent of the rated sequence; reconditioning per 6.102.8.5 allowed afterwards).
