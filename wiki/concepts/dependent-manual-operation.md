# Dependent manual operation

An operation solely by means of directly applied manual energy, such that the speed and force of the operation are dependent upon the action of the operator ([[iec-56]] 3.104.9, IEV 441-16-13). The closing or opening of the [[circuit-breaker]] is carried out directly by the hand of the operator, with no stored or auxiliary energy interposed; consequently the making speed cannot be guaranteed. For this reason [[iec-56]] explicitly excludes from its scope any circuit-breaker fitted with a closing mechanism for dependent manual operation, because a rated short-circuit making current cannot be specified and such operation may be objectionable on safety grounds (scope, [[iec-56]] clause 1). Contrast with [[independent-manual-operation]], where stored energy de-couples the result from operator effort, and with [[stored-energy-operation]].
