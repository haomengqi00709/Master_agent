# IP first numeral 2 — finger / objects ≥ 12,5 mm

First characteristic numeral **2** of the [[ip-code-structure|IP Code]]
([[iec-529]], 1st ed. Table I; 2nd ed. clause 5, Table V). The familiar
"finger-safe" level:

- **Access (persons):** protection against access with a **finger** — verified
  with the [[ip-access-probe|jointed test finger]], **12 mm diameter × 80 mm
  long** (corresponds to [[ip-additional-letter|additional letter B]]). 1976
  wording: "fingers or similar objects not exceeding 80 mm in length".
- **Solid objects (equipment):** excludes solid foreign objects of **12,5 mm
  diameter and greater** (1976: "greater than 12 mm").

**Test ([[ip-first-numeral-tests]], 1st ed. §7.2; 2nd ed. Table V):** two parts —
**(a) finger test** with the metallic jointed test finger (both joints bend 90° in
one direction only) pushed with not more than **10 N ± 10%** into every opening
and placed in every possible position; adequate clearance must be kept to live or
moving parts (smooth shafts may be touched). For LV gear a ≥ 40 V lamp circuit
between finger and live parts must not light. **(b) Sphere test** with a rigid
sphere of **12,5 mm ± 0,2 diameter** applied at **30 N ± 10%**, which must not
pass through any opening. Per Table V the finger may penetrate up to its 80 mm
length provided adequate clearance is kept, but the 12,5 mm sphere shall not
fully penetrate.

Subsumes [[ip-first-numeral-1]]; finer level is [[ip-first-numeral-3]].
