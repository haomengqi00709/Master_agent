# Choice of colours

**IEC 73 §3.2.** The colour of an [[indicator-light]] or a [[push-button]] shall
be chosen **with regard to the information to be given to the operator** by the
light, or **with regard to the operation initiated by the push-button** when it is
pressed. This is the governing principle of [[iec-73]]: meaning drives colour, not
the hardware. It is reinforced in Appendix A
([[indicator-light-colour-choice-guide]]), which stresses that the colour shall be
based on the most important information to be given to personnel — usually some
mechanical effect or movement — rather than, as in older practice, the position of
the immediately associated switchgear.

An important corollary (§3.2 note): where lights connected **in parallel** are
mounted at **different locations** and may have to give different information,
**different colours may have to be used** for those lights even though they are
electrically the same circuit (illustrated in Appendix A Table IV, lines 9–10 and
12–13). Once chosen, the significance is preserved by [[permanence-of-colours]]
(§3.3), and the palette from which the choice is made is fixed by
[[use-of-colours]] (§3.1).
