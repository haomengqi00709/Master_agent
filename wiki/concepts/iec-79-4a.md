# IEC 79-4A — First supplement (1970): list of established ignition temperatures

The **first supplement to IEC Publication 79-4**, 1st edition 1970, prepared by
IEC TC No. 31. It does **not change the [[iec-79-4|test method]]**; it
**publishes a list of ignition temperatures determined by that method** and adds
two interpretive notes. Its changes are folded into the relevant concept pages
and marked **Amended (Amd.)**.

**What the amendment adds (vs. the 79-4 base text):**

1. **A reference table of established ignition temperatures** (~28 substances) —
   see [[ignition-temperature-data-list]]. Inclusion rule (Note 1): values were
   **measured by the IEC 79-4 method in at least two countries and retained only
   within the 5 % limit of §7.2** (the [[repeatability-reproducibility|reproducibility]]
   criterion). This operationalises §7.2 as a data-curation rule.

2. **An impurity / ageing caution (Note 2)** — *a small amount of impurity may
   appreciably alter the ignition temperature.* Worked example: **trichlorosilane
   gives 230 °C with a freshly prepared sample, but after ageing and possible
   moisture contamination the value was found to fall to 185 °C.** This is folded
   into [[factors-affecting-ignition-temperature]] and flagged against the
   [[temperature-classes-t1-t6|T-class]] consequences.

The supplement notes the intent to **publish further lists from time to time** as
results from several countries accumulate — consistent with the §7 note that the
tolerances are "tentative pending more information."

(Bibliographic note: the 79-4 back-matter lists "79-4A (1970) First supplement to
Publication 79-4 (1966)"; the supplement was approved under the Six Months' Rule
in September 1967 after a 1966 Ottawa meeting, and references the 1966 1st edition
of 79-4. The substantive method now lives in the [[iec-79-4|1975 2nd edition]].)
