# Restrike-free circuit-breaker

A restrike-free circuit-breaker is a [[circuit-breaker]] that interrupts without restrike during the capacitive current-breaking test duties specified in this standard ([[iec-56]] 3.102.12). Unlike the preceding entries this clause is defined verbatim in the body rather than by IEV reference. A "restrike" is a resumption of current between the contacts a quarter-cycle or more after interruption, which is especially troublesome when switching capacitive loads such as no-load lines, cables and capacitor banks because it can cause damaging voltage escalation. The term is a performance classification, not a construction type: any of the medium-based designs, the [[sf6-circuit-breaker]], [[vacuum-circuit-breaker]], [[air-blast-circuit-breaker]] or [[oil-circuit-breaker]], may qualify if it passes the capacitive switching tests without restriking. Performance still depends on each [[pole]] and its [[arcing-contact]] behaviour.
