# Watt-hour meter (active energy)

An **instrument intended to measure active energy by integrating active power
with respect to time** (IEC 387 §3.3, after IEV 301-04-17). It is the most
common a.c. electricity meter and the reference case throughout IEC 387: the
measuring-element symbols of Table 1 are introduced as "**watt-hour or var-hour
meter**" arrangements, distinguished only by which energy they integrate — see
[[measuring-element-symbols]].

The corresponding registered unit is the **watt-hour (Wh)** / kilowatt-hour
(§5.4, [[meter-unit-symbols]]), and for marking the measured quantity the meter's
dial carries the unit symbol per [[measured-quantity-marking]] (§6). When the
meter measures in one direction only or both directions, the
[[bidirectional-meter]] arrow markings apply. Accuracy classes and the
[[meter-constant]] (rev/kWh or imp/kWh) are marked per
[[accuracy-class-and-protective-class]].

Contrast with the reactive [[var-hour-meter-def]] and apparent
[[volt-ampere-hour-meter-def]] meters, which integrate reactive and apparent
power respectively. Related TC 13 product standards: IEC 514, IEC 521, IEC 687,
IEC 1036. General graphical equivalent: [[08-04-03]] (watt-hour meter),
[[08-04-04]] (one direction only).

See also: [[iec-387]] §3.3.
