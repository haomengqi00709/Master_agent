# Test duty

The basic short-circuit test series consists of five standardised Test-duties Nos. 1-5 per [[iec-56]] 6.106, each based on the [[rated-operating-sequence]] and expressed as percentages of the [[rated-short-circuit-breaking-current]]. Test-duty No. 1 is opening-only at 10% (d.c. below 20%); No. 2 at 30%; No. 3 at 60%; No. 4 the full rated operating sequence at 100% breaking and 100% making current with applied voltage per 6.104.1 (and may be split into making duty 4a and breaking duty 4b); No. 5 (applied only where the opening time t0 is below 80 ms) is opening-only at 100% breaking with the rated asymmetrical d.c. component. Each carries the prospective [[transient-recovery-voltage]] and power-frequency recovery voltage of 6.104.5 and 6.104.7. Breaking current may depart up to 20% (duties 1-2) or 10% (duty 3); peak making current up to 110% rated (duties 4, 4b, 5) ([[iec-56]] 6.106). See [[short-circuit-test-procedure]], [[short-circuit-test-quantities]].

**Amended (1992, Amd. 1):** [[iec-56-amendment-1]] restates 6.106 and 6.106.1–6.106.5 (the 110%-of-rated-making-current cap now applies to duties 4, 4b and 5; making operations may be omitted before breaking in duties 1–3; revised TRV table references) and adds **Test-duty No. 4a** (making tests, 6.106.4.1): C-t'-C for sequence O-t-CO-t'-CO or C-t"-C for CO-t"-CO, one close against rated making current and one against a symmetrical current.
