# IP second numeral 2 — dripping water when tilted 15°

Second characteristic numeral **2** of the [[ip-code-structure|IP Code]]
([[iec-529]], 1st ed. Table II; 2nd ed. clause 6). Protection against
**vertically dripping water when the enclosure is tilted**: *"vertically dripping
water shall have no harmful effect when the enclosure is tilted at any angle up
to 15° from its normal position."*

**Test ([[ip-second-numeral-tests]], 1st ed. §8.2):** the **same drip apparatus**
as numeral 1 (Figure 3), adjusted to the same **3–5 mm/min** discharge rate. The
equipment is tested for **2,5 min in each of four fixed tilt positions** — 15° to
either side of the vertical in two mutually perpendicular planes — for a **total
duration of 10 min**. Inspection per §8.9 applies (water that entered must not
impair operation, reach unprotected live parts, or accumulate at cable ends).

Subsumes [[ip-second-numeral-1]]; next is [[ip-second-numeral-3]] (spraying).
