# Indicator light — YELLOW (caution)

**IEC 73 §4.2, Table I.** A **YELLOW** [[indicator-light]] means **caution**: a
*change or impending change of conditions*. YELLOW (which may shade towards orange
and is sometimes called *amber* — §3.1.2 note 1) is one of the three **reserved**
colours (with RED and GREEN) whose meaning *shall not be used with any other
meaning*. It sits between RED (danger, immediate action) and GREEN (safety): a
yellow light flags a developing or marginal situation that the operator should
watch or correct before it becomes a danger.

Typical applications listed in Table I, column 4:
- temperature (or pressure) different from normal level;
- overload, the duration of which is permitted for a limited time only.

YELLOW is frequently used in the *indication* mode
([[indicator-light-modes-of-use]], §4.1). Its push-button counterpart is the
YELLOW "intervention" button (Table II) — *intervention to suppress abnormal
conditions or to avoid unwanted changes* ([[push-button-colour-table]]). Contrast
with [[indicator-light-red]] (danger), [[indicator-light-green]] (safety), and the
free/neutral [[indicator-light-blue]] and [[indicator-light-white]]. The
general signal-lamp symbol is [[08-10-01]] (colour code **YE**).
