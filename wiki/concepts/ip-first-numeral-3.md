# IP first numeral 3 — tool / objects ≥ 2,5 mm

First characteristic numeral **3** of the [[ip-code-structure|IP Code]]
([[iec-529]], 1st ed. Table I; 2nd ed. clause 5, Table V):

- **Access (persons):** protection against access with a **tool** — verified with
  the [[ip-access-probe|test rod]] of **2,5 mm diameter × 100 mm long**
  (corresponds to [[ip-additional-letter|additional letter C]]). 1976 wording:
  "tools, wires, etc., of diameter or thickness greater than 2,5 mm".
- **Solid objects (equipment):** excludes solid foreign objects of **2,5 mm
  diameter and greater**.

**Test ([[ip-first-numeral-tests]], 1st ed. §7.3; 2nd ed. Table V):** a straight
rigid steel rod/wire of **2,5 mm diameter** (end free from burrs, at right angles
to its length) is applied with a force of **3 N ± 10%**. The protection is
satisfactory if the rod **cannot enter** the enclosure and adequate clearance is
kept. For equipment with ventilating openings or drain holes, the manner of
interpreting this requirement is the responsibility of the relevant Technical
Committee (1st ed. §7.3, Table I Note 2).

Subsumes [[ip-first-numeral-2]]; finer level is [[ip-first-numeral-4]].
