# Validity of results (§7) & data record (§8)

The acceptance criteria that decide whether a measured [[ignition-temperature]]
may be recorded ([[iec-79-4]] §7), plus the permanent record required (§8). See
[[repeatability-reproducibility]] for the two precision statistics; this page
ties them to the test and the data record.

**7.1 Repeatability.** Duplicate results obtained by the **same operator** shall
be considered **suspect if they differ by more than 2 %**.

**7.2 Reproducibility.** The averages of duplicate results obtained in
**different laboratories** shall be considered **suspect if they differ by more
than 5 %**.

> **Note (§7):** These repeatability and reproducibility tolerances are
> **tentative values pending the accumulation of more information.**

The §7.2 reproducibility limit is also the **inclusion rule for the reference
table**: amendment [[iec-79-4a|79-4A]] lists only values measured in at least two
countries that agreed to within the **5 %** of §7.2 (see
[[ignition-temperature-data-list]]). The §7 tolerances also define the §4.2
furnace-acceptance check against the Table I reference substances.

**8 Data.** A record shall be kept of: the **name, source and physical properties
of the combustible**, **test number**, **date of test**, **ambient temperature**,
**pressure**, **quantity of sample used**, **[[ignition-temperature|ignition
temperature]]** and **[[ignition-lag|ignition lag]]**. Recording ambient
temperature and pressure is necessary because the result is defined "at
atmospheric pressure" and pressure is one of the
[[factors-affecting-ignition-temperature|factors affecting the result]].
