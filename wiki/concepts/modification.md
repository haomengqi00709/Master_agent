# Modification

A change to the design of the apparatus which affects material, fit, form or function ([[iec-79-19]] 2.2.7). Because it alters the certified design, a modification may invalidate the explosion-protection [[certification]] and must therefore be carried out only under the modification rules of the relevant type-of-protection clause — [[type-of-protection-d|"d"]] 3.4, [[type-of-protection-i|"i"]] 4.4, [[type-of-protection-p|"p"]] 5.4, [[type-of-protection-e|"e"]] 6.4, [[type-of-protection-n|"n"]] 7.4 — which typically require reference to the [[manufacturer]] or certifying authority. It is distinct from [[repair]], [[overhaul]] and [[reclamation]], which restore the original design rather than change it. Where the certificate carries the suffix "X" ([[certificate-references]]), the certification documents must be studied before any modification is undertaken.
