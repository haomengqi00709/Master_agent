# Sampling (encapsulation "m")

The number of samples to be submitted to the national or other appropriate authority
for the type tests of [[type-of-protection-m|encapsulation "m"]] (IEC 79-18 clause 7).
The quantity depends on the apparatus **group**:

- **Group I** (mining / firedamp): **1 sample non-filled** with compound + **4 samples
  filled** with compound.
- **Group II** (other explosive-gas atmospheres): **1 sample non-filled** with compound
  + **2 samples filled** with compound.

The "non-filled" sample lets the authority inspect the construction before
encapsulation; the filled samples carry the test sequence. Those sequences are set out
in **annex B** — B.1 for group I, B.2 for group II — which thread the samples through
the [[thermal-tests|thermal]], [[cable-pull-test|cable-pull]] and
[[apparatus-electric-strength-test|electric-strength]] tests together with separate
compound specimens (discs of 50 mm diameter × 3 mm thick, and for group II also
50 × 6 × 4 mm test bars) for the [[compound-electric-strength-test]],
[[water-absorption-test]] and [[non-specific-tests-encapsulation|annex C non-specific tests]].
