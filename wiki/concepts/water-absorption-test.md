# Water absorption test (encapsulation "m")

A type test on the [[encapsulation-compound|compound]] material that bounds how much
water it takes up, since absorbed moisture can degrade the dielectric and mechanical
properties on which [[type-of-protection-m|encapsulation "m"]] depends (IEC 79-18
§8.1.2). It is carried out **only on compounds intended to be used in a moist
environment** during operation.

**Three dry samples** (dried per ISO 62), each **50 mm ± 2 mm diameter × 3 mm ± 0.2 mm
thick**, are weighed; immersed for **24 h in tap water at 23 °C ± 2 K**; then removed,
wiped off and reweighed. **The increase in mass shall not exceed 1 %.** Where the test
has **not** been performed, the apparatus is marked **"X"** and the associated
documentation must state the restrictions of use.

It is one of the two compound-specimen tests (with the
[[compound-electric-strength-test]] of §8.1.1) and appears in both annex B sequences on
50 mm discs alongside the [[non-specific-tests-encapsulation|annex C non-specific tests]].
