# 08-01-03 — Integrating instrument

![08-01-03](../assets/60617-8/p05.png)
<sub>This symbol appears on Publication No.617-8.pdf, printed p.7 (full page shown).</sub>

**Standard:** [[60617-8]] · **Section:** [[60617-8-01-general]]

## Description
Integrating instrument. The asterisk shall be replaced in accordance with the rules given in Clause 1.1.

## Names
- **EN:** Integrating instrument
- **FR:** Appareil intégrateur

## Notes
- The symbol may also be used for a remote instrument which repeats a reading transmitted from an integrating meter (e.g. 08-04-11).
- The symbol may be combined with that for a recording instrument to represent a contact-recording instrument (e.g. 08-04-14).
- Symbols from Section 5 of IEC Publication 617-2 may be used to specify the direction of energy flow (e.g. 08-04-04 to 08-04-07).
- The number of rectangles at the top indicates the number of different summations by a multi-rate meter (e.g. 08-04-08).
