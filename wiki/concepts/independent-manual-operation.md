# Independent manual operation

A stored-energy operation in which the energy originates from manual power, stored and released in one continuous operation, such that the speed and force of the operation are independent of the action of the operator ([[iec-56]] 3.104.12, IEV 441-16-16). The operator's hand movement (typically charging a spring) accumulates the energy, which is then released by a mechanism so that the closing speed of the [[circuit-breaker]] is fixed and cannot be slowed by hesitation. It is a special case of [[stored-energy-operation]] using human rather than auxiliary power, and is distinguished from [[dependent-manual-operation]], where the result depends directly on the operator's effort. The charging and release functions reside in the [[operating-mechanism]].
