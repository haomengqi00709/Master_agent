# IEC 750 — Item designation in electrotechnology

*Repérage d'identification du matériel en électrotechnique. Publication 750,
1st edition, 1983, © CEI/IEC 1983.* This standard lays down a **uniform,
international code for labelling every "item" of electrotechnical equipment** —
the alphanumeric reference (e.g. `-K1`, `+C-A2-S1`, `=SSP2`) that appears beside
each symbol on a circuit diagram, on the equipment itself, in parts lists and in
documentation. The code is built from up to four **[[designation-block|designation
blocks]]**, each introduced by a distinctive **prefix sign**, so that any item can
be identified by *what it is*, *what function it serves*, *where it is located*
and *which terminal* is meant. IEC 750 is the standard cited whenever an IEC
document refers to "item designation"; it underpins the reference letters used in
the [[60617-1|IEC 60617]] symbol family and the designations carried on the
diagrams governed by [[iec-1082]].

## Scope (§1)
IEC 750 establishes a method of designating items so that, on diagrams and in
associated documents, each item — and each of its terminals — can be uniquely and
unambiguously identified, irrespective of the type of diagram or the country of
origin. It applies to all branches of electrotechnology. The designation is
**independent of the graphical symbol**: the symbol shows *what kind of thing* it
is graphically, the IEC 750 designation says *which particular one* and *where it
belongs* in the plant hierarchy.

## The four designation blocks (§3)
An item designation is assembled from up to four blocks, written in a fixed order,
each marked by its own **prefix sign** so blocks can be combined or omitted freely:

| Block | Name | Prefix sign | Page |
|-------|------|-------------|------|
| **Block 1** | Higher-level (function) designation | `=` (equals) | [[higher-level-designation]] |
| **Block 2** | Location designation | `+` (plus) | [[location-designation]] |
| **Block 3** | Identification of item (kind + number) | `-` (hyphen) | [[item-identification-block]] |
| **Block 4** | Terminal designation | `:` (colon) | [[terminal-designation]] |

A full designation is read in the sequence `=…+…-…:…`. See
**[[item-designation]]** for the assembly rules, omission of prefixes, and the
[[simplified-designation|simplified form]] that drops repeated hyphens.

## How it relates to the rest of the wiki
- The **kind-of-item letters** (R resistor, K relay, Q power switch, etc.) are the
  same reference letters the [[60617-1|general index of IEC 60617-1]] attaches to
  each graphical symbol, so a reader can move from symbol → letter code → IEC 750
  designation.
- IEC 750 designations are written onto the diagrams whose *preparation* is
  governed by **[[iec-1082]]** (Preparation of documents used in
  electrotechnology) — item designation is the labelling layer, 1082 is the
  document-structure layer.
- Later renumbered/expanded as **IEC 61346** "Industrial systems, installations
  and equipment and industrial products — Structuring principles and reference
  designations" (in turn → IEC 81346); treat those as the successor editions.
