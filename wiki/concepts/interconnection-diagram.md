# Interconnection diagram

The second subtype of [[wiring-diagram]] in [[iec-113-1]] (§5.2). A **diagram
representing the connections between the different units of an installation**
(§5.2). Its scope is *between* units, making it the complement of the
[[unit-wiring-diagram]] (§5.1), which represents all connections *within* a single
unit.

Together with the [[terminal-diagram]] (§5.3) — which shows terminals and the
conductors connected to them — these three subtypes partition the wiring-diagram
job by scope: inside a unit, between units, and at the terminals. As a
[[wiring-diagram]] it serves to guide the making and checking of connections (§5),
and the §5 note permits it to be replaced or supplemented by a [[table-document]]
(a wiring table). Because the relative placement of units is often relevant, it is
a natural candidate for [[topographical-representation]] (§9).
