# Rated peak withstand current

The rated peak withstand current is the peak value of the first major loop of current the circuit-breaker can withstand in the closed position during a short-circuit, representing the electrodynamic (mechanical) stress. Sub-clause 4.6 of IEC Publication 694 applies, with the addition that the rated peak withstand current is equal to the [[rated-short-circuit-making-current]] ([[iec-56]] 4.6, see 4.103). It is therefore derived as 2.5x (50 Hz) or 2.6x (60 Hz) the r.m.s. a.c. component of the [[rated-short-circuit-breaking-current]]. It complements the [[rated-short-time-withstand-current]] (4.5), which expresses the thermal stress carried for the [[rated-duration-of-short-circuit]] (4.7). Both are demonstrated together in the short-time and peak withstand current tests (6.5).
