# Verification tests for the second characteristic numeral

The **type tests** that establish a [[ip-code-structure|second characteristic
numeral]] — protection against harmful ingress of water ([[iec-529]], 1st ed.
clause 8; 2nd ed. clause 14). **All tests use fresh water.** Samples are tested
clean and new, assembled and mounted as the manufacturer states; the relevant
product standard specifies sample count, mounting (artificial roof/wall),
pre-conditioning, energized-or-not, and (per the [[ip-supplementary-letter|S/M
letters]]) whether parts are in motion. Summary of method by level:

| Numeral | Protection | Apparatus | Key parameters |
|---------|-----------|-----------|----------------|
| [[ip-second-numeral-0|0]] | none | — | no test required |
| [[ip-second-numeral-1|1]] | vertical drip | drip box (Fig. 3) | 3–5 mm/min rainfall, 10 min |
| [[ip-second-numeral-2|2]] | drip, tilted 15° | drip box (Fig. 3) | 4 positions × 2,5 min = 10 min |
| [[ip-second-numeral-3|3]] | spray ≤ 60° | oscillating tube (Fig. 4) / hand spray (Fig. 5) | 60° arc, 0,8 bar, ≥ 10 min |
| [[ip-second-numeral-4|4]] | splash, any direction | oscillating tube / hand spray | full 180° arc, all directions |
| [[ip-second-numeral-5|5]] | water jets | nozzle 6,3 mm (Fig. 6) | 12,5 l/min, 0,3 bar, ~3 m, 1 min/m² (≥3 min) |
| [[ip-second-numeral-6|6]] | powerful jets | nozzle 12,5 mm (Fig. 6) | 100 l/min, 1 bar, ~3 m, 1 min/m² (≥3 min) |
| [[ip-second-numeral-7|7]] | temporary immersion | water tank | ≥150 mm over top, ≥1 m deep, ≥30 min, ΔT ≤ 5 °C |
| [[ip-second-numeral-8|8]] | continuous submersion | per agreement | not less severe than numeral 7 |

**State after the tests (1st ed. §8.9):** the enclosure is inspected for water
ingress. The relevant Technical Committee specifies, if possible, how much water
may enter; in general any water that entered shall not (a) be enough to impair
satisfactory operation, (b) reach live parts or windings not designed to operate
when wet, or (c) accumulate near or enter the cable end. Drain-holed enclosures
must be shown to drain without harm; non-drained enclosures are assessed for
water build-up.

For levels 1–4 a **visual inspection** may, in obvious cases, show the protection
is met (then no test need be made if the product standard permits); in doubt the
prescribed tests are performed. Interpretation of results is the relevant
Technical Committee's responsibility.
