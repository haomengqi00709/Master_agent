# Type of protection "p" — pressurization

Protection by which entry of the surrounding atmosphere into the enclosure is prevented by maintaining a **protective gas at a pressure above** the external atmosphere (with or without continuous flow). Repair must not increase gas leakage or create stagnant volumes.

Defined in [[iec-79-19]] 2.2.16. Repair, overhaul, reclamation and modification requirements are in **[[iec-79-19]] clause 5**.
