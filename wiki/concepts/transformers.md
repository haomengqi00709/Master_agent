# Transformers — Ex repair

Transformers in Ex apparatus; a faulty unit is replaced via the manufacturer and no embedded (encapsulated) thermal trip is ever repaired or replaced in the field.

In [[iec-79-19]], repair/overhaul/reclamation of this component is specified per type of protection:

- **[[type-of-protection-i|"i" — intrinsic safety]]** (clause 4): If a transformer is found faulty, the replacement shall be obtained from the equipment manufacturer, and no attempt shall be made to repair or replace any embedded (encapsulated) thermal trip device (4.2.13).
- **[[type-of-protection-p|"p" — pressurization]]** (clause 5): After winding repair, the transformer should preferably be energized at rated supply voltage, the supply current and secondary voltage/current measured and compared with the manufacturer's data, and balanced across all phases in three-phase systems (5.2.6.3.1 d).
- **[[type-of-protection-e|"e" — increased safety]]** (clause 6): After winding repair, the transformer should preferably be energized at rated supply voltage, measurements compared with the manufacturer's data, and balanced across all phases (6.2.6.3.1 d).
- **[[type-of-protection-n|"n" — non-sparking]]** (clause 7): After winding repair, the transformer should preferably be energized at rated supply voltage, measurements compared with the manufacturer's data, and balanced across all phases (7.2.6.3.1 d).
