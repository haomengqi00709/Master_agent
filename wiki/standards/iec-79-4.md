# IEC 79-4 — Method of test for ignition temperature

*Electrical apparatus for explosive gas atmospheres, Part 4: Method of test for
ignition temperature.* 2nd edition, 1975, prepared by IEC Technical Committee
No. 31 (Electrical Apparatus for Explosive Atmospheres). The 2nd edition
superseded the 1966 1st edition; the **1st supplement [[iec-79-4a|79-4A]]
(1970)** publishes a table of ignition temperatures determined by this method
and is folded into the relevant concept pages below.

Like its sibling [[iec-79-19]], this is a **test-method / procedure standard**,
not a [[index#standards|60617 symbol dictionary]]. Decomposed here per the
LLM-Wiki method — each definition (§2), each piece of apparatus (§4), each
procedure step (§5), the validity rules (§7) and the cross-cutting links to the
explosion-protection framework get their own concept page.

## Scope (§1)
A method of test for determining the **ignition temperature of a chemically pure
vapour or gas in air at atmospheric pressure**. It does not cover mixtures, dusts,
or pressures other than atmospheric. The number it produces feeds the
[[temperature-classes-t1-t6|temperature classification (T1–T6)]] of Ex apparatus
and underpins [[type-of-protection-d|"d"]] / [[type-of-protection-e|"e"]] /
[[type-of-protection-n|"n"]] / [[type-of-protection-p|"p"]] maximum-surface-temperature
limits.

[[ignition-temperature]] (2.1) · [[ignition-reaction|ignition]] (2.2) ·

## Outline of method (§3)
[[ignition-test-outline]] — inject a known volume of product into a heated open
200 ml [[erlenmeyer-test-flask|Erlenmeyer flask]] of air, observe in a darkened
room until ignition; repeat over flask temperatures and sample volumes; the
lowest flask temperature giving ignition is the ignition temperature.
