# Vacuum circuit-breaker

A vacuum circuit-breaker is a [[circuit-breaker]] in which the contacts open and close within a highly evacuated envelope ([[iec-56]] 3.102.8, IEV 441-14-29). Its distinguishing feature is that arc interruption takes place in a sealed vacuum interrupter, where the very low gas density gives rapid dielectric recovery and the arc, sustained only by metal vapour from the contacts, extinguishes at the first current zero. There is no gas or liquid arc-extinguishing medium, distinguishing it from the [[oil-circuit-breaker]], [[air-circuit-breaker]], [[gas-blast-circuit-breaker]] and [[sf6-circuit-breaker]]. Vacuum technology is dominant in medium-voltage switchgear. As with any [[circuit-breaker]] [[pole]], rated current is carried continuously, the [[main-contact]] and [[arcing-contact]] functions here being combined within the single contact pair inside the vacuum bottle.
