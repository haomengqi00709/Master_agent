# Ex component (encapsulation "m")

A **part of electrical apparatus for explosive atmospheres which is not to be used
alone in such atmospheres and which requires additional certification of any
electrical apparatus with which it is used** (IEC 79-18 §3.8). The OCR'd note records
that this definition "will be transferred in due course to IEC 79-0", reflecting that
the Ex-component concept is general to the IEC 79 family rather than specific to "m".

[[iec-79-18]] consistently treats three subjects in parallel — *encapsulated
electrical apparatus*, *encapsulated parts of electrical apparatus*, and
*encapsulated Ex components* — and most clauses apply to all three. Where the rules
differ for Ex components the standard says so: several IEC 79-0 clauses are
disapplied for Ex components specifically (clause 1, e.g. 4.2 maximum surface
temperature, 22.4.3.1 impact test); the [[cable-pull-test]] (8.2.2) **shall not be
carried out on Ex components**; and marking differs — Ex components carry the minimum
marking of IEC 79-0 §25.5 (except line 4) plus, where appropriate, the mark **"U"**
(§10.2.5) in addition to the **"m"** sign and electrical data.

Sampling quantities also reference the Ex-component-bearing apparatus and its group
(I or II) — see [[sampling-encapsulation]].
