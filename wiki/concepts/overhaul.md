# Overhaul

Action to restore an apparatus that has been in service to a **fully serviceable
condition** — more thorough than routine maintenance. Defined in [[iec-79-19]]
(clause 2.2.3). For Ex (explosion-protected) apparatus, overhaul must not
invalidate the type-of-protection certification; see [[iec-79-19]] clauses 3–7
per protection type. Contrast with **maintenance** (routine, preservative) and
[[reclamation]] (restoring dimensions by adding/removing material).
