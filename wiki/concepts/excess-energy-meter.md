# Excess energy meter

An **energy meter intended to measure the excess energy when the power exceeds a
pre-determined value** (IEC 387 §3.7, after IEV 302-04-04). It records only the
energy consumed above a set power threshold, used in demand-based tariffs.

IEC 387 assigns it a dedicated **triangle symbol Δ** under the tariff-device
rules (§9 b): the register recording excess metering is marked beside the
register with that symbol, and **the value of the power over which excess-load
energy is registered shall be marked beside the symbol** in a suitable unit —
either permanently or, preferably, on a supplementary plate that can be changed
when the excess-power value is adjusted. Table 6 illustrates two forms (§9): an
excess energy meter where "the number beside the triangle indicates the power at
which the excess-energy register starts to operate" (example 800 W), and a
variant "in which the excess level is adjustable." A NOTE covers meters with two
fixed operating limits changeable by a relay — both limits are marked.

[[maximum-demand-meter]] for the related demand-measuring case.
