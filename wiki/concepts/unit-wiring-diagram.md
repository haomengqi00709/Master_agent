# Unit wiring diagram

The first subtype of [[wiring-diagram]] in [[iec-113-1]] (§5.1). A **diagram
representing all connections within a unit of an installation** (§5.1). Its scope
is therefore *internal* to a single unit: it documents every connection inside
that unit so the wiring can be made and checked.

It contrasts by scope with the other two wiring-diagram subtypes: the
[[interconnection-diagram]] (§5.2) covers the connections *between* the different
units, and the [[terminal-diagram]] (§5.3) covers the terminals and the conductors
connected to them. As a [[wiring-diagram]] it shares their practical purpose —
guiding the making and checking of connections — and the §5 note that any of these
diagrams may be replaced or supplemented by a [[table-document]] (a wiring table)
applies. It may be drawn topographically ([[topographical-representation]], §9)
where the physical layout of the unit's parts is useful.
