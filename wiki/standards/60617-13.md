# IEC 60617-13 (formerly IEC 617-13) — Analogue elements

*Éléments analogiques*

- **Standard:** IEC 60617-13 (formerly IEC 617-13)
- **Edition:** 1st edition, 1993
- **Source:** `Publication No.617-13.pdf` (scanned, 27 pp)
- **Symbols:** 54 across 14 sections

Analogue building blocks: amplifiers, operational amplifiers, converters (DAC/ADC), voltage regulators, comparators, analogue switches and function elements. Uses dependency-notation I/O qualifiers and references the binary-logic part (617-12) and [[60617-2]] qualifying symbols. (1993 edition; scans hold two printed pages per image.)
