# Indicator light — GREEN (safety)

**IEC 73 §4.2, Table I.** A **GREEN** [[indicator-light]] means **safety**: the
*indication of a safe situation*, or *authorization to proceed / clear way*.
GREEN is the third of the three **reserved** colours (with RED and YELLOW) whose
meaning *shall not be used with any other meaning*. Where RED warns of danger and
YELLOW cautions of change, GREEN positively confirms that conditions are safe and
that the operator may go ahead.

Typical applications listed in Table I, column 4:
- cooling liquid circulating;
- automatic boiler control in operation;
- machine ready to be started.

GREEN appears in both the *indication* and (in some cases) the *confirmation*
mode ([[indicator-light-modes-of-use]], §4.1). Its push-button counterpart is the
GREEN START/ON button ([[push-button-start-on]], §5.2 — the preferred colour for
buttons that close switching devices). Note that IEC 232 (nuclear instrumentation)
gives green and white *other* meanings, from which this standard deviates (preface).
Contrast with [[indicator-light-red]] (danger) and [[indicator-light-yellow]]
(caution), and the free/neutral [[indicator-light-blue]] and
[[indicator-light-white]]. The general signal-lamp symbol is [[08-10-01]] (colour
code **GN**).
