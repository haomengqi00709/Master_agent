# Module (of a pole)

A module (of a pole of a circuit-breaker) is an assembly which generally comprises making or breaking units, post-insulators and mechanical parts and which is mechanically and electrically connected to other identical assemblies to form a pole of a circuit-breaker ([[iec-56]] 3.103.22). This clause is given verbatim in the body. A module is the repeatable building block from which a high-voltage [[pole]] is constructed: identical modules are stacked and connected in series to reach the required voltage rating. Each module incorporates one or more (making or breaking) units (3.103.21), the post-insulators that support and insulate it, and the associated mechanical parts. The concept is characteristic of modular [[live-tank-circuit-breaker]] and [[sf6-circuit-breaker]] designs, where building a pole from standard modules lets one design span a range of system voltages. The complete [[circuit-breaker]] comprises one such pole per phase.
