# Certification

Certification leading to the issue of a certificate of conformity, referring primarily to assessments of apparatus carried out by a recognized testing authority. The standard may also apply to apparatus certified by other authorities or self-certified by manufacturers/users against recognized standards.

Defined in [[iec-79-19]] 2.2.11.
