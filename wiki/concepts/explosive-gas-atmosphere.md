# Explosive gas atmosphere

A **mixture with air, under atmospheric conditions** (as defined in 1.2 of
IEC 79-0), of flammable substances in the form of **gas, vapour or mist** in
which, after ignition, combustion spreads throughout the unconsumed mixture
([[iec-79-3]] §3.3). It is the hazard the whole IEC 79 family guards against and
the medium whose ignition the spark test must rule out.

For testing, the general hazard is represented by a controlled
[[explosive-test-mixture|explosive test mixture]] of defined composition (per gas
group), admitted into the [[explosion-chamber]] so that the make- and break-sparks
of the [[contact-mechanism]] act on a known, reproducible atmosphere. A circuit
is [[intrinsically-safe-circuit|intrinsically safe]] precisely when no spark or
thermal effect it produces can ignite such an atmosphere under the prescribed
conditions.
