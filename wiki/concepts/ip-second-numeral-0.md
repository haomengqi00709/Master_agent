# IP second numeral 0 — non-protected (water)

The lowest level of the [[ip-code-structure|second characteristic numeral]]
([[iec-529]], 1st ed. Table II; 2nd ed. clause 6). A second numeral **0** means
the enclosure provides **no protection against harmful ingress of water**. No
water claim is made.

Accordingly **no test is required** for second numeral 0 ([[iec-529]] 1st ed.
§8: "For second characteristic numeral 0, no test is required"). An enclosure
written `IPX0` makes no water claim; a fully unrated enclosure is `IP00`. As with
all levels, every higher water numeral subsumes 0.

This is the water counterpart of [[ip-first-numeral-0]].
