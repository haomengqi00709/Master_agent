# IP second numeral 6 — powerful water jets / heavy seas

Second characteristic numeral **6** of the [[ip-code-structure|IP Code]]
([[iec-529]], 1st ed. Table II; 2nd ed. clause 6). Protection against **powerful
water jets and heavy seas**: 1976 wording — *"water from heavy seas or water
projected in powerful jets shall not enter the enclosure in harmful
quantities."*

**Test ([[ip-second-numeral-tests]], 1st ed. §8.6):** identical method to
[[ip-second-numeral-5]] but with the **larger standard test nozzle** (Figure 6):
**12,5 mm internal diameter**; delivery rate **100 l/min ± 5%**; water pressure
≈ **100 kN/m² (1 bar)** at the nozzle (set to achieve the delivery rate); **test
duration 1 min per m²** of enclosure surface, **minimum 3 min**; distance from
nozzle to surface **≈ 3 m**. At 100 kN/m² the water rises freely ~8 m above the
nozzle. Inspection per §8.9.

This is the highest **jet** level. Beyond it the regime changes to **immersion**:
[[ip-second-numeral-7]] (temporary immersion) and [[ip-second-numeral-8]]
(continuous submersion). Subsumes [[ip-second-numeral-5]].
