# Circuit-breaker

A circuit-breaker is a mechanical **switching device** capable of making, carrying and breaking currents under normal circuit conditions and also of making, carrying for a specified time and breaking currents under specified abnormal circuit conditions such as those of short-circuit ([[iec-56]] 3.102.3, IEV 441-14-20). It is the fundamental **mechanical switching device** of this standard, distinguished from simpler switches by its short-circuit interrupting duty. Its current-carrying path runs through the [[main-circuit]] of each [[pole]], where the [[main-contact]] carries continuous current and the [[arcing-contact]] takes the arc during interruption. Circuit-breakers are classified by arc-extinguishing medium and tank construction into the [[dead-tank-circuit-breaker]], [[live-tank-circuit-breaker]], [[air-circuit-breaker]], [[oil-circuit-breaker]], [[vacuum-circuit-breaker]], [[gas-blast-circuit-breaker]], [[air-blast-circuit-breaker]] and [[sf6-circuit-breaker]] variants. A [[restrike-free-circuit-breaker]] is one that meets defined no-restrike performance.
