# IEC 60617-2 — Symbol elements, qualifying symbols and other symbols having general application

*Éléments de symboles, symboles distinctifs et autres symboles d'application générale*

- **Standard:** IEC 60617-2 (formerly IEC 617-2)
- **Edition:** 1st edition, 1983
- **Source:** `Publication No.617-2.pdf` (scanned, 35 pp)
- **Origin:** Prepared by IEC Sub-Committee 3A (Graphical Symbols) of Technical Committee No. 3.
- **Symbols:** 154 across 18 sections (3 chapters + Appendix A)

These are the **building-block** symbols (symbol elements and qualifying symbols) combined with the device symbols in the other 60617 parts. Foundational to the whole family.
