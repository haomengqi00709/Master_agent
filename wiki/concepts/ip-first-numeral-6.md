# IP first numeral 6 — dust-tight

The highest first characteristic numeral, **6**, of the
[[ip-code-structure|IP Code]] ([[iec-529]], 1st ed. Table I; 2nd ed. clause 5):

- **Solid objects (equipment):** **dust-tight** — **no ingress of dust** at all.
- **Access (persons):** complete protection against access to hazardous parts
  with the [[ip-access-probe|test wire]] (as numeral 4/5).

**Test ([[ip-first-numeral-tests]], 1st ed. §7.6):** the same **dust chamber**
apparatus and conditions as [[ip-first-numeral-5]] (talcum powder in suspension,
Figure 2), but the **acceptance criterion is stricter**: the protection is
satisfactory only if **no deposit of dust is observable inside the enclosure** at
the end of the test. For category-1 enclosures the vacuum-pump regime of §7.5
applies (up to 80 air-volume changes, ≤ 60 vol/h, ≤ 200 mm-water depression).

`IP6X` is the top solid-object/dust rating; combined with the highest water
levels it yields the most-sealed designations (e.g. `IP66`, `IP67`, `IP68`).
Subsumes all lower first-numeral degrees ([[ip-first-numeral-0]]–
[[ip-first-numeral-5]]).
