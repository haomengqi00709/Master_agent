# Out-of-phase making and breaking test

Out-of-phase making and breaking tests are required only if a [[rated-out-of-phase-breaking-current]] has been specified ([[iec-56]] 6.110.1). They verify the breaker can make and break under out-of-phase (loss-of-synchronism) conditions, which are onerous because the recovery voltage across the gap approaches twice the system voltage. The test-circuit power factor must not exceed 0.15, with roughly half the applied and recovery voltage on each side ([[iec-56]] 6.110.2). Applied and power-frequency recovery voltage equal 2.0/sqrt(3) (earthed-neutral) or 2.5/sqrt(3) (other systems) times rated voltage; [[transient-recovery-voltage]] follows 4.106 ([[iec-56]] 6.110.3). Two [[test-duty]] series (Table XVIII): Test-duty No. 1 at 20-40% and No. 2 at 100-110% of rated out-of-phase breaking current, opening-operation d.c. component below 20% ([[iec-56]] 6.110.4). Test-duty No. 1 may be omitted where [[critical-current-test]] is not required.
