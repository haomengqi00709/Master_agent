# Mixed diagram

A combined-purpose [[diagram]] recognised by the Note referring to Clauses 3 to 6 in
[[iec-113-1]]. The note states that **several types of diagrams may be combined into
a single diagram, forming a mixed diagram**, and gives the canonical case: **the
same drawing may form both an explanatory and a wiring diagram** (Note to §3–6).

The mixed diagram is the practical consequence of IEC 113-1's two orthogonal
classifications. Because *purpose* ([[explanatory-diagram]], [[wiring-diagram]],
[[location-diagram-or-table]], etc.) and *method of representation* (§7–9) are
independent axes (Note to §1), and because one drawing can simultaneously serve
more than one purpose, a real-world document is not always a pure single class. The
parallel Note referring to Clauses 7 to 9 makes the representation side just as
flexible: **several of the methods of representation may be used on the same
diagram** — for instance mixing [[single-line-representation]] and
[[multi-line-representation]], or combining [[assembled-representation]] with
[[detached-representation]]. The mixed diagram thus formalises that a single sheet
may blend both purposes and methods.
