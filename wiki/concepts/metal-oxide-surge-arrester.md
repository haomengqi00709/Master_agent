# Metal-oxide surge arrester without gaps

A metal-oxide surge arrester without gaps is an arrester having **non-linear
[[metal-oxide-resistor|metal-oxide resistors]] connected in series and/or in
parallel without any integrated series or parallel spark gaps** ([[iec-99-4]]
§2.1). It is the device that [[iec-99-4]] as a whole specifies. Because there is
no spark gap to isolate the resistor column from the system, the metal-oxide
resistors carry system [[continuous-operating-voltage|continuous operating
voltage]] continuously while in service — a fundamental difference from the
**gapped** non-linear-resistor arresters of the sibling standard [[iec-99-1]],
where a series gap normally holds off power-frequency voltage. This continuous
energization is why [[iec-99-4]] incorporates an [[accelerated-ageing-procedure]]
(§7.5.2) to simulate long-term voltage/temperature effects on the resistors.

The standard basically applies to **all** metal-oxide arresters, though
polymeric-housed, GIS, liquid-immersed and other special designs may require
special consideration in design, test and application (§1.1). An arrester may be
built up from one or more [[arrester-unit|units]] connected in series/parallel to
reach a higher voltage and/or current rating, and is represented for testing by
[[arrester-section|sections]]. Its graphical symbol is [[07-22-03]] (lightning
arrester) in [[60617-7-22-gaps-and-arresters]]. Identification is per
[[arrester-identification]] (§3.1) and classification per
[[arrester-classification]] (§3.2).
