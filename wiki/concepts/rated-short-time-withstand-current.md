# Rated short-time withstand current

The rated short-time withstand current is the r.m.s. value of current the circuit-breaker can carry, in the closed position, for the [[rated-duration-of-short-circuit]] without damage, under prescribed conditions. Sub-clause 4.5 of IEC Publication 694 applies with the addition that the rated short-time withstand current is equal to the [[rated-short-circuit-breaking-current]] ([[iec-56]] 4.5, see 4.101). It therefore takes its standard values from the same R 10 series of a.c. components (6.3, 8, 10, 12.5, 16, 20, 25, 31.5, 40, 50, 63, 80, 100 kA). The thermal stress it represents is associated with the [[rated-peak-withstand-current]] (4.6), which expresses the corresponding electrodynamic (peak) stress. Verified by short-time withstand current tests (6.5).
