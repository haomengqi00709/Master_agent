# Arrester identification (rating plate)

**Arrester identification** is the minimum set of information that must appear on the rating plate
(nameplate) of every lightning arrester ([[iec-99-1]] §50). The required markings are:

- **Rated voltage** — see [[rated-voltage-arrester]] (§51).
- **Rated frequency** — only if other than one of the standard frequencies (50/60 Hz, see
  [[rated-frequency-arrester]] §52).
- **Nominal discharge current** — see [[nominal-discharge-current]]; specifying for the 5 000 A
  arrester whether **Series A or Series B**, and for the 10 000 A arrester whether **light- or
  heavy-duty**.
- **Long-duration discharge class** — for 10 000 A heavy-duty arresters (the five classes of §63.3.2,
  Table III; see [[current-impulse-withstand-test]]).
- **Pressure-relief class** — for arresters fitted with a [[pressure-relief-device]] (classes A–E of
  §65.2, Table V).
- **Manufacturer's name or trademark, type and identification.**
- **Year of manufacture.**

The rating plate thus encodes the arrester's two classifying ratings (voltage and nominal discharge
current) plus the optional duty/relief classes that select which rows of Table I and which test
requirements apply. It is the field-readable summary of the
[[protective-characteristics-arrester|protective characteristics]] the type tests have verified.

## In the gapless metal-oxide standard [[iec-99-4]] (§3.1)
The gapless [[metal-oxide-surge-arrester]] nameplate (§3.1) adds and changes several items versus
99-1: it must also carry the **[[continuous-operating-voltage]] (U_c)** as a primary marking; the
pressure-relief item is a **pressure relief rated current in kA r.m.s.** (rather than 99-1's classes
A–E) for arresters with a [[pressure-relief-device]]; for multi-unit arresters the **assembling
position** of each [[arrester-unit|unit]]; and a **serial number** at least for arresters rated above
**60 kV**. As in 99-1, [[arrester-rated-frequency|rated frequency]] is marked only if non-standard,
and [[arrester-rated-voltage|rated voltage]] and [[nominal-discharge-current]] are required. If space
permits, the plate should also show the **[[line-discharge-class]] or [[high-lightning-duty-arrester|
High Lightning Duty]] type** (Annex C) and the contamination withstand level of the enclosure
(IEC 815). These quantities map to [[arrester-classification]] Table 1.
