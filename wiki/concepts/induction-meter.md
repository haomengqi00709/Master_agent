# Induction meter

An **energy meter which operates by the rotation of the disc of an induction
measuring element** (IEC 387 §3.1, after IEV 302-04-03). It is the classic
electromechanical a.c. energy meter: voltage and current circuits produce
fluxes that drive a rotating aluminium disc whose revolutions are proportional
to the energy, totalised by a [[meter-memory-display-register|register]].

IEC 387 treats induction and [[static-energy-meter|static]] meters in parallel —
the standard's symbols apply **independent of induction or static measurement
elements** (§1). The distinction surfaces in two places. The
[[meter-constant]] (§3.19, §7.2) for an induction meter is expressed in
**revolutions of the rotor per measuring unit** (e.g. 500 rev/kWh or 2 Wh/rev),
whereas a static meter uses pulses (imp/kWh). And for reactive-energy induction
meters adjusted for leading- or lagging-power-factor operation, the **direction
of normal rotor rotation viewed from the front** is defined (left to right) and
the register is marked with the leading/lagging arrow symbol — see
[[measured-quantity-marking]] (§6).

The "magnet for partial relief of the pressure of the rotor against the bottom
bearing" symbol ([[moving-element-suspension-symbol]], §11) is specific to such
rotating-disc meters. General graphical equivalent: the integrating-instrument
family in [[08-04-03]] / [[08-01-03]].
