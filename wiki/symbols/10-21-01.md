# 10-21-01 — Carrier frequency

![10-21-01](../assets/60617-10/p42.png)
<sub>This symbol appears on Publication No.617-10.pdf, printed p.44 (full page shown).</sub>

**Standard:** [[60617-10]] · **Section:** [[60617-10-21-symbol-elements]]

## Description
Carrier frequency. Notes: 1. – When this symbol is used to represent a carrier which is modulated in frequency or phase the f or φ is added. See, for example, symbol 10-22-02. 2. – The arrowhead on the vertical line representing the carrier (and the arrowhead on the frequency axis) may be omitted if no confusion will result.

## Names
- **EN:** Carrier frequency
- **FR:** Fréquence d'une onde porteuse

## Notes
- When this symbol is used to represent a carrier which is modulated in frequency or phase the f or φ is added. See, for example, symbol 10-22-02.
- The arrowhead on the vertical line representing the carrier (and the arrowhead on the frequency axis) may be omitted if no confusion will result.
