# Reclamation

A means of repair involving the **removal or addition of material** (e.g.
machining, metal spraying, welding, sleeving) to restore an apparatus to its
correct dimensions. Defined in [[iec-79-19]] (clause 2.2.6); type-specific
reclamation rules are in its clauses 3.3, 4.3, 7.3. Related: [[overhaul]].
