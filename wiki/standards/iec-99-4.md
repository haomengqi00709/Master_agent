# IEC 99-4 — Metal-oxide surge arresters without gaps for a.c. systems

*Surge arresters, Part 4: Metal-oxide surge arresters without gaps for a.c.
systems.* IEC Technical Committee 37 (Surge arresters), 1st edition, 1991-11.

Like [[iec-79-19]] and [[iec-56]], this is a **requirements / test standard**,
not a symbol dictionary. It sets the minimum criteria for the requirements and
testing of **gapless metal-oxide surge arresters** applied to a.c. power systems
(Introduction, §1.1). These arresters are commonly applied in place of the
non-linear-resistor **gapped** arresters of its sibling [[iec-99-1]]. Because the
arrester's resistor elements carry system power-frequency voltage continuously in
service, an **accelerated ageing procedure** (§7.5.2) is built into the standard
to simulate long-term voltage/temperature effects. Decomposed here per the
LLM-Wiki method — each definition, rating and test is its own concept page.

## Scope (§1.1)
Applies to non-linear [[metal-oxide-resistor|metal-oxide resistor]]-type surge
arresters **without spark gaps**, designed to limit voltage surges on a.c. power
circuits. Basically applies to all metal-oxide arresters, but polymeric-housed,
GIS, liquid-immersed and other special designs may need special consideration.
Protection of low-voltage circuits below 3 kV is under consideration.

## Normative references (§1.2)
IEC 60-1 (HV test techniques), IEC 71 / 71-2 (insulation co-ordination),
[[iec-99-1]] (gapped arresters), IEC 99-3 (artificial pollution testing of surge
arresters), IEC 270 (partial discharge measurements), IEC 815 (selection of
insulators for polluted conditions). Letter symbols per IEC 27; graphical symbols
per **IEC 617** (the 60617 family this wiki indexes).

## Requirements (Section 5) & tests (Sections 6–8)
The requirements clauses (Section 5) state *what* the arrester must achieve; the
test sections specify *how* it is verified.
- [[insulation-withstand-test]] (§5.1, §7.2)
- [[residual-voltage-test]] (§5.3, §7.3) — steep/lightning/switching impulse
- [[long-duration-current-impulse-withstand-test]] (§5.8, §7.4) including the
  [[line-discharge-class]] test (§7.4.2) and [[line-discharge-class]] selection (Annex E)
- [[operating-duty-test]] (§5.9, §7.5) — high-current & switching-surge duty,
  with the [[accelerated-ageing-procedure]] (§7.5.2) and
  [[thermal-stability-evaluation]] (§7.5.6)
- [[partial-discharge-test]] (§5.4), [[seal-leakage]] (§5.5),
  [[current-distribution-multi-column]] (§5.6)
- [[pressure-relief]] (§5.11) and [[arrester-disconnector]] tests (§5.12, §7.6)
- [[arrester-routine-test]] (§8.1) and [[arrester-acceptance-test]] (§8.2)
