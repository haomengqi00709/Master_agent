# Indicator light — BLUE (specific meaning)

**IEC 73 §4.2, Table I.** A **BLUE** [[indicator-light]] has a **specific meaning
assigned according to the need** in the case considered: blue *may be given any
specific meaning which is not covered by the three* reserved colours RED, YELLOW
and GREEN. Unlike those three, BLUE is **not reserved** — it is the user-assignable
colour for a definite, documented meaning that does not fit danger/caution/safety.

Typical applications listed in Table I, column 4:
- indication of remote control;
- selector switch in "set-up" position.

BLUE is frequently used in both the *indication* and *confirmation* modes
([[indicator-light-modes-of-use]], §4.1). In several Appendix A examples
([[indicator-light-colour-choice-guide]], Table IV lines 3, 6 and 13) BLUE *might
also* have been chosen instead of WHITE, showing the two non-reserved colours
overlap in use. Its push-button counterpart is the BLUE button for "any specific
meaning not covered by the above colours" ([[push-button-colour-table]]), and BLUE
is one of the permitted RESET-button colours ([[push-button-reset]], §5.4).
Contrast the reserved [[indicator-light-red]], [[indicator-light-yellow]],
[[indicator-light-green]] and the neutral [[indicator-light-white]]. The
general signal-lamp symbol is [[08-10-01]] (colour code **BU**).
