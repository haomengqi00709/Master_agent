# Non-linear resistor type (valve type) arrester

A **non-linear resistor type (valve type) arrester** is an arrester having a single or multiple
[[series-gap|spark-gap]] connected in series with one or more
[[non-linear-series-resistor|non-linear resistors]] ([[iec-99-1]] §4). It is the construction to which
the whole of IEC 99-1 applies (§1 scope): surge protective devices for repeated operation that limit
voltage surges on a.c. power circuits and interrupt power [[follow-current]]. The "valve" action
comes from the [[non-linear-series-resistor]], whose voltage–current characteristic is a low
resistance to high [[discharge-current]] (clamping the [[residual-voltage]] across the terminals) and
a high resistance at normal power-frequency voltage (limiting follow-current magnitude). The
[[series-gap]] isolates the resistor from continuous power-frequency voltage until a surge causes
[[power-frequency-sparkover-voltage|sparkover]], and then helps interrupt the follow current at a
current zero so the arrester reseals.

This type is distinguished from gapless and other constructions, and its repeated-operation,
self-reclosing behaviour is what the [[operating-duty-test]] (§64) verifies — twenty
[[nominal-discharge-current|nominal-current]] impulses applied while energized at rated power
frequency, with the arrester required to establish and then interrupt follow current each time. It is
the [[surge-arrester]] generically.
