# IEC 1082 — Preparation of documents used in electrotechnology

*Établissement des documents utilisés en électrotechnique.* Prepared by
**sub-committee 3B (Documentation)** of IEC technical committee 3 (Documentation
and graphical symbols). This is the **modern successor of [[iec-113-1|IEC 113]]**:
Part 3 states explicitly that *"IEC 1082 cancels and replaces IEC 113. Due to
restructuring and extensions of the material, there is no exact correspondence
between the parts of IEC 1082 and those of IEC 113."* It is a **rules / method
standard**, not a symbol dictionary — it governs *how* to assemble the graphical
symbols of the [[index#standards|60617 family]] into engineering documents, what
document *types* exist, and how items, terminals, signals and connections are
designated. The reference-designation prefixes it uses (`=` `+` `-` `:`) are
defined in **[[iec-750]]** (item designation in electrotechnology).

## Part 1 — General requirements (1991)
The foundation part. Section 2 defines the vocabulary: the **information carriers**
([[medium-and-document]]), the **forms of presentation** ([[forms-of-presentation]]
— pictorial, plan, diagram, map, chart/graph, table/list, textual) and a full
**classification of documents** ([[document-classification-iec1082]]). It then
defines the **methods of representing components** ([[representation-methods-iec1082]]
— attached / semi-attached / detached / grouped / dispersed / repeated) and of
representing conductors ([[single-line-representation]] / [[multi-line-representation]]).
The **general drawing rules** (Section 3, clause 4) cover diagram layout,
connecting lines, [[signal-flow-and-line-bundling|signal flow & line bundling]],
[[boundary-lines-and-simplified-connections|boundary frames & simplified
connections]], the title block, and Annex A (extracts from ISO drawing standards).
Section 3 also sets out the choice between [[function-oriented-vs-location-oriented-structure|function-oriented and location-oriented structure]].

## Part 2 — Function-oriented diagrams (1993)
Rules for diagrams that show **how a system works** (its function), independent of
physical layout. Builds on Part 1's symbol-selection rule (2.4: choose the [[60617-3|IEC 617]]
symbol depicting the *function actually performed*). It standardises the family of
[[function-oriented-diagrams]]: the **overview diagram**, the **function diagram**
(an equivalent-circuit diagram), the **circuit diagram** (positive-logic / direct
polarity), the **logic-function diagram**, and the **terminal-function diagram**
(with function charts). It also gives rules for [[simplified-representation-many-terminals|simplifying symbols with many terminals]].

## Part 3 — Connection diagrams, tables and lists (1993)
Rules for **connection documents** ([[connection-documents-iec1082]]) — the
"how it is wired" documents used when assembling, installing or maintaining
equipment. Common rules (Section 2) cover connection diagrams (topographical,
not to scale; matrix/grid form) and connection tables/lists (terminal-oriented vs
connection-oriented). Four document classes, one per section:
[[unit-connection-diagram]] (internal connections of one constructional unit),
[[interconnection-diagram-iec1082]] (connections *between* units),
[[terminal-connection-diagram]] (external connections to a unit), and
[[cable-diagram]] (cable laying & routing). Conductors and cables are identified
per [[iec-750]], coloured/numbered per **IEC 446**, colour-coded per **IEC 757**,
and terminals identified per **IEC 445**.

## Related standards
- **Supersedes [[iec-113-1]]** (Diagrams, charts, tables — 1971) and the rest of the IEC 113 family (113-3..113-8).
- **[[iec-750]]** — Item designation in electrotechnology: defines the `=`/`+`/`-`/`:` prefix system that IEC 1082 applies throughout.
- **IEC 445** — identification of equipment terminals & designated conductors (alphanumeric system).
- **IEC 446** — identification of conductors by colours or numerals; **IEC 757** — code for designation of colours.
- **[[60617-3|IEC 617 / 60617]]** — the graphical-symbol dictionary populated into IEC 1082 documents; Part 1 cross-refers to IEC 617-12 (binary logic) for logic-symbol rules.
- **IEC 375** — polarity / current-reference-direction conventions (cited by Part 2 for sources).
- **ISO 128 / 129 / 3098 / 3461-2 / 5455 / 5457 / 6428 / 7200** — general technical-drawing rules incorporated via Part 1 Annex A.
