# User

The user of the apparatus ([[iec-79-19]] 2.2.9). The user carries statutory responsibilities for ensuring that Ex apparatus in service remains in a [[serviceable-condition]] (2.3.3), and is the party that selects a competent [[repairer]] and issues the instructions to the repairer — covering the work required, the original [[certification]] and any special conditions of use (2.5). The user is responsible for holding and passing on the documentation needed to repair, overhaul, reclaim or modify the apparatus correctly, and for satisfying itself, on return, that the explosion-protection is intact. The repairer of an item may itself be the user, the [[manufacturer]], or a third-party repair agency ([[repairer]], 2.2.10).
