# Maintenance

Routine actions taken to preserve the fully serviceable condition of the installed apparatus ([[iec-79-19]] 2.2.4). Maintenance is preventive and is carried out on apparatus that is in service and not faulty — it keeps the apparatus in a [[serviceable-condition]] rather than restoring it. This distinguishes it from [[repair]] (2.2.2), which restores a faulty apparatus, and from [[overhaul]] (2.2.3), which restores to fully serviceable condition a non-faulty apparatus that has been in use or in storage. Because maintenance does not alter the design it is not a [[modification]]; but any maintenance that disturbs explosion-protection features — flameproof joints on [[enclosures]], [[cable-and-conduit-entries]], [[terminations]] — must still leave them compliant with the original certification. Responsibility for maintaining apparatus in service rests with the [[user]] (2.3.3).
