# IP second numeral 3 — spraying water

Second characteristic numeral **3** of the [[ip-code-structure|IP Code]]
([[iec-529]], 1st ed. Table II; 2nd ed. clause 6). Protection against **sprayed
water**: *"water falling as a spray at an angle up to 60° from the vertical shall
have no harmful effect."*

**Test ([[ip-second-numeral-tests]], 1st ed. §8.3):** either the **oscillating
tube** (Figure 4) or, where the enclosure is too large for the tube's ≤ 1 m
radius, the **hand-held spray nozzle** (Figure 5). With the oscillating tube the
spray holes cover an arc of **60° either side of the vertical**; water pressure
≈ **80 kN/m² (0,8 bar)**, supply ≥ 10 l/min; the enclosure sits on a rotating
turntable so all parts are wetted, **minimum 10 min** (or, if not rotated,
oscillate ±60° at ~60°/s for 5 min, rotate the enclosure 90° horizontally, and
continue 5 min). With the hand-held device the counterbalanced shield is in
place, delivery **10 ± 0,5 l/min** at ~0,8–1,0 bar, for **1 min per m²** of
calculated surface area (minimum 5 min). Inspection per §8.9.

Subsumes [[ip-second-numeral-2]]; next is [[ip-second-numeral-4]] (splashing).
