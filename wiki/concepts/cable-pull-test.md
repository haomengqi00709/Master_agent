# Cable pull test (encapsulation "m")

A mechanical type test verifying the integrity of the seal where a permanently
connected cable enters the [[encapsulation-compound|compound]] (IEC 79-18 §8.2.2). It
is the test required by [[external-connections-and-sealing|§5.5.2]] for apparatus and
parts with a permanently connected cable. It **shall not be carried out on
[[ex-component-encapsulation|Ex components]]**.

A tensile force is applied to the cable, **in the direction of the cable entrance into
the compound, for 1 h**. The force in newtons is the **smaller** of:

- **20 × the cable diameter** in millimetres, or
- **50 × the mass** in kilograms of the encapsulated apparatus,

but **at least 1 N**. **No visible displacement between the compound and the cable
shall be observed** — confirming the gas-tight seal of §5.5.1 (e.g. the ≥ 5 mm of bare
conductor bonded in the compound) holds under service pull loads.
