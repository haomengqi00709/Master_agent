# IP first numeral 5 — dust-protected

First characteristic numeral **5** of the [[ip-code-structure|IP Code]]
([[iec-529]], 1st ed. Table I; 2nd ed. clause 5). At this level the object
criterion becomes **dust** rather than a discrete object size:

- **Solid objects (equipment):** **dust-protected** — *"ingress of dust is not
  totally prevented, but dust shall not enter in sufficient quantity to interfere
  with satisfactory operation of the equipment"* or impair safety.
- **Access (persons):** complete protection against access to hazardous parts
  with the [[ip-access-probe|test wire]] (as numeral 4) — wire shall not enter
  and adequate clearance is kept.

**Test ([[ip-first-numeral-tests]], 1st ed. §7.5):** the **dust chamber** test
(Figure 2). Talcum powder, sieved through a 75 µm-aperture / 50 µm-wire mesh,
**2 kg per m³** of chamber volume (used for no more than 20 tests), is kept in
suspension. For **category 1 enclosures** (where normal duty cycles produce a
pressure lower than the surrounding air) the equipment interior is held below
atmospheric pressure by a vacuum pump: the test draws up to **80 times the
enclosure air volume**, not exceeding 60 volumes/hour or a 200 mm-water
depression; if the extraction rate is 40–60 vol/h the test stops after 2 h,
otherwise it continues until 80 volumes are drawn or **8 h** elapse. The
protection is satisfactory if talcum has not accumulated in a quantity or
location that, like any dust, would interfere with correct operation. Any
drain-hole is additionally wire-tested as for numeral 4.

Subsumes [[ip-first-numeral-4]]; the strictest level is [[ip-first-numeral-6]]
(dust-tight).
