# IEC 79-18 — Encapsulation "m"

*Electrical apparatus for explosive gas atmospheres, Part 18: Encapsulation "m".*
1st edition, 1992-10. Prepared by IEC sub-committee 31K (Encapsulation) of TC 31;
based extensively on CENELEC EN 50028.

A sibling of [[iec-79-19]] in the **IEC 79** family, but a different kind of
document: where 79-19 is a repair/overhaul procedure standard, **79-18 is a
construction-and-test requirements standard** for a single type of protection.
It prescribes the specific requirements for the construction and testing of
electrical apparatus, parts of electrical apparatus and [[ex-component-encapsulation|Ex components]]
with rated voltages **not exceeding 11 kV** that use the type of protection
**[[type-of-protection-m|encapsulation "m"]]**. Decomposed here per the LLM-Wiki
method — each defined term, each constructional requirement, and each test is its
own concept page.

## Scope (clause 1)
Specific requirements for the construction and testing of [[type-of-protection-m]]
apparatus, parts and [[ex-component-encapsulation|Ex components]] rated ≤ 11 kV.
These **supplement [[iec-79-19#related-standards|IEC 79-0]] "General Requirements"**
with explicit exceptions: a list of IEC 79-0 clauses is declared *not applicable*
to "m" (e.g. 4.2 max surface temperature for Ex components, 8 Fasteners,
9 Interlocking devices, 15 Cable/conduit entries, 17 Switchgear, 18 Fuses, and
several 22.4 tests — impact, IP, explosive-mixture and cable-clamping tests).
The standard does not address dust atmospheres, nor non-explosion risks
(live-part contact, radiation, etc.) (Introduction; clause 1).

## The protection principle
[[type-of-protection-m|Encapsulation "m"]] (3.1) works by enclosing the parts that
could ignite an explosive atmosphere by **sparking or heating** inside a solidified
[[encapsulation-compound|compound]] so the atmosphere cannot reach them and cannot
be ignited. Safety therefore depends on the compound's integrity, its thickness
over live parts, the absence of voids, the seal at conductor entries, and on
keeping the compound within its rated temperatures.

## Requirements for the compound (clause 4)
The manufacturer must precisely document each compound and the encapsulation
production method — see [[encapsulation-compound]] (4.1–4.3): name/address,
exact reference, colour, fillers and additives, surface treatments, plus the
[[temperature-range-of-the-compound]] and [[continuous-operating-temperature]].

## Marking (clause 10)
Beyond the minimum IEC 79-0 marking: the sign **"m"** (10.1.1/10.2.1), input/output
electrical data, external fuse data, permitted prospective short-circuit current if
not 4 000 A, and the mark **"U"** on Ex components where appropriate (10.2.5).
