# Rated single capacitor bank breaking current

Clause 4.107 of [[iec-56]] defines the rated single capacitor bank breaking current as the maximum capacitor current the circuit-breaker shall be capable of breaking at its rated voltage under the prescribed conditions of use and behaviour, without exceeding the maximum permissible switching overvoltages specified by the manufacturer (suggested values in Table IX, columns B). It refers explicitly to switching a shunt capacitor bank where no shunt capacitors are connected to the source side of the breaker — a single capacitor bank (3.101.11) whose inrush is limited only by supply inductance and the bank's own capacitance. This contrasts with the [[rated-back-to-back-capacitor-bank-breaking-current]], where parallel banks raise the inrush. Specification is not mandatory; values are selected from the R 10 series. The principal hazard on interruption is [[restrike]] producing voltage escalation; verified by the [[capacitive-current-switching-test]] ([[iec-56]] 4.107).
