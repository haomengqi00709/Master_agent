# Pressure relief (§5.11)

Pressure relief is the requirement that, **when an arrester is fitted with a
[[pressure-relief-device]], the failure of the arrester shall not cause explosive
shattering of the housing** ([[iec-99-4]] §5.11). If an internal fault forms an arc,
the resulting pressure must be vented so the housing fails benignly rather than
flinging porcelain fragments.

It is verified by the test described in **clause 8.7 of [[iec-99-1]]** (other test
conditions under consideration). The sample is deemed to pass if **the housing
remains intact, or breaks non-explosively with all parts of the sample contained
within the required enclosure** (§5.11). For arresters so fitted, the **pressure
relief rated current** in kA r.m.s. must appear on the nameplate
([[arrester-identification]] §3.1). Per [[arrester-classification]] Table 1 (row 7),
the pressure-relief test applies to 1 500 A–10 000 A and 20 000 A arresters when
fitted with a relief device (not required for the smallest classes in some rows).
It is type-test item 5 of the [[arrester-type-test]] programme (§7.1).
