# Evaluation of thermal stability in the operating duty tests (§7.5.6)

This clause is the **pass/fail rule** for [[thermal-stability]] in the
[[operating-duty-test]] ([[iec-99-4]] §7.5.6). An arrester section subjected to the
operating duty test is considered thermally stable — and passes — **if the peak of
the resistive component of leakage [[continuous-current|current]], or the power
dissipation, or the resistor temperature steadily decreases at least during the last
15 min of the elevated continuous-operating-voltage (U_c\*) application** in the
sequences of figures 1, 2 and C.1.

Because the resistive-current peak is strongly influenced by supply-voltage
stability and ambient-temperature change, the judgement may be unclear at the end of
the U_c\* application. In that case the U_c\* application is **extended** until a
steady decrease in current, power dissipation or temperature is clearly confirmed.
If no *increasing* trend is observed within **3 h** of voltage application, the
section is considered stable. A failure to stabilise indicates
[[thermal-runaway]]. This monitored quantity (resistive leakage current / power /
temperature) is the same one tracked in the [[high-current-impulse|high-current]]
and switching-surge duty tests (§7.5.4.2, §7.5.5.2) and in the optional special
thermal stability test ([[arrester-acceptance-test]] §8.2.2).
