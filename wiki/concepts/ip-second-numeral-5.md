# IP second numeral 5 — water jets

Second characteristic numeral **5** of the [[ip-code-structure|IP Code]]
([[iec-529]], 1st ed. Table II; 2nd ed. clause 6). Protection against **water
jets**: *"water projected by a nozzle against the enclosure from any direction
shall have no harmful effect."*

**Test ([[ip-second-numeral-tests]], 1st ed. §8.5):** the enclosure is sprayed
from **all practicable directions** with a jet from a **standard test nozzle**
(Figure 6) with **6,3 mm internal diameter**. Conditions: delivery rate
**12,5 l/min ± 5%**; water pressure ≈ **30 kN/m² (0,3 bar)** at the nozzle (set to
achieve the delivery rate); **test duration 1 min per m²** of enclosure surface
area, **minimum 3 min**; distance from nozzle to surface **≈ 3 m** (may be reduced
when spraying upwards to ensure proper wetting). Inspection per §8.9.

This is the lower of the two jet levels; the higher-energy jet is
[[ip-second-numeral-6]] (12,5 mm nozzle). Subsumes [[ip-second-numeral-4]]. The
"versatile" dual designation `IPX5/IPX7` lets one enclosure declare jet
protection *and* temporary-immersion protection, which are tested separately.
