# Rated out-of-phase breaking current

Clause 4.106 of [[iec-56]] defines the rated out-of-phase breaking current as the maximum out-of-phase current the circuit-breaker shall be capable of breaking, under the prescribed conditions, in a circuit having the recovery voltage specified below. Out-of-phase conditions (3.101.14) are abnormal states of lost or lacking synchronism between the system parts on either side of the breaker, where the phase angle between the generated-voltage vectors exceeds normal and may reach 180 degrees (phase opposition), so the [[recovery-voltage]] is much higher than for a normal fault. Its specification is not mandatory; if assigned: (a) power-frequency recovery voltage is 2.0/sqrt(3) x rated voltage (earthed-neutral) or 2.5/sqrt(3) (other systems); (b) TRV follows Tables VIA-VID; (c) the current is 25% of the [[rated-short-circuit-breaking-current]] unless otherwise specified. Verified by the [[out-of-phase-test]] (6.110). See also [[making-and-breaking-capacity]] ([[iec-56]] 4.106).
