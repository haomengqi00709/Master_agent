# Location diagram or table

The fourth purpose-class in [[iec-113-1]] (§6). A location diagram or table
**contains detailed information about the location of parts of the equipment** —
e.g. terminal blocks, plug-in units, sub-assemblies, modules, etc. It **shows the
item designations used in related diagrams and tables**, tying physical position
to the identifiers used elsewhere in the documentation (§6). A note adds that a
**location diagram need not necessarily be to scale** (§6).

It is distinguished from the other purpose classes by its concern with *where*
parts are, rather than how they function ([[explanatory-diagram]], §3) or how they
are connected ([[wiring-diagram]], §5). It is one of the two concepts newly
**inserted** in the 1971 revision, alongside the [[equivalent-circuit-diagram]]
(§Preface). Its information may be given as a [[diagram]] or as an equivalent
[[table-document]] (hence "diagram or table"). It is closely related to
[[topographical-representation]] (§9), the §7–9 method in which symbol positions
correspond to the physical layout of the items represented — though the location
*diagram* itself need not be to scale.
