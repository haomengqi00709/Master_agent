# Type of protection "e" — increased safety

Measures applied to give, with a higher degree of security, freedom from excessive temperatures and from arcs or sparks in the interior and on external parts of apparatus that does not produce them in normal service. Creepage/clearance distances and the temperature class are decisive.

Defined in [[iec-79-19]] 2.2.17. Repair, overhaul, reclamation and modification requirements are in **[[iec-79-19]] clause 6**.
