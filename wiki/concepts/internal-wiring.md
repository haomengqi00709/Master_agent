# Internal wiring and connections — Ex repair

Internal connections and their segregation. Renewed wiring must be no inferior electrically/thermally/mechanically and no smaller in cross-section; for type "i" critical inter-conductor distances must be kept in their original positions.

In [[iec-79-19]], repair/overhaul/reclamation of this component is specified per type of protection:

- **[[type-of-protection-d|"d" — flameproof enclosure]]** (clause 3): There are no particular requirements for this type of protection, but repairs to internal connections should be of a standard at least equivalent to the original design (3.2.5).
- **[[type-of-protection-i|"i" — intrinsic safety]]** (clause 4): Certain inter-conductor distances and their segregation are critical, so disturbed internal wiring shall be re-located in its original position; damaged insulation, screens, outer sheaths and/or double insulation or fixing methods shall be replaced by equivalent material and re-fixed in the same configuration (4.2.12).
- **[[type-of-protection-p|"p" — pressurization]]** (clause 5): Internal connections should not be electrically, thermally or mechanically inferior to those originally fitted and should be of a standard at least equivalent to the original design (5.2.5).
- **[[type-of-protection-e|"e" — increased safety]]** (clause 6): If internal connections are renewed, the insulation shall not be electrically, thermally or mechanically inferior to that originally supplied, and the cross-sectional area shall not be less than originally fitted (6.2.5).
- **[[type-of-protection-n|"n" — non-sparking]]** (clause 7): If internal connections are renewed, the insulation shall not be electrically, thermally or mechanically inferior to that originally supplied, and the cross-sectional area shall not be less than originally fitted (7.2.5).
