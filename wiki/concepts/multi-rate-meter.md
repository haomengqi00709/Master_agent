# Multi-rate meter

An **energy meter provided with a number of registers, each becoming operative
at specified time intervals corresponding to different tariffs** (IEC 387 §3.6,
after IEV 302-04-06). It is the meter type underlying time-of-use tariff
metering — day/night/peak rates.

IEC 387 gives multi-rate meters **no special graphical symbol**: under the
tariff-device rules (§9 a), the respective rates "shall be marked beside the
appropriate set of dials or register," with examples for **day, night and peak**
rates. The standard adds a NOTE that the marking of more than three-rate
registers should be the object of the purchase contract. A multi-rate meter
typically also carries an [[auxiliary-device-symbols|auxiliary-device]] marking
for the **nature and value of the auxiliary voltage of the rate-changing relay**
(§10.4, e.g. 60 V d.c.), shown on the connection diagram.

The register concept it depends on is defined in §3.13 — see
[[meter-memory-display-register]] (each rate has its own register, which for a
static meter may be a separate electronic memory sharing one display). See
[[tariff-device-symbols]] for the full clause-9 marking scheme.

Related TC 13 standards: IEC 1037 (ripple control receivers), IEC 1038 (time
switches for tariff and load control). General graphical equivalent:
[[08-04-08]] (multi-rate watt-hour meter).

See also: [[iec-387]] §3.6, §9.
