# Type tests (design tests) of an arrester (§2.43)

Type (design) tests are **tests made upon completion of the development of a new
arrester design to establish representative performance and demonstrate compliance
with the relevant standard** ([[iec-99-4]] §2.43). Once made they need not be
repeated unless the design is changed so as to modify its performance — and then
only the relevant tests are repeated. They are made on representative samples
(complete arresters, [[arrester-section|sections]] or resistor elements), as
distinct from the [[arrester-routine-test|routine tests]] made on every unit and
the negotiated [[arrester-acceptance-test|acceptance tests]]. (This mirrors the
circuit-breaker [[type-test]] of [[iec-56]], but for the arrester.)

Section 7 enumerates the type tests, applied as required by Table 1 ([[arrester-classification]])
and Table C.1 (§7.1):
1. [[insulation-withstand-test|Insulation withstand tests]] (§5.1, §7.2)
2. [[residual-voltage-test|Residual voltage tests]] (§5.3, §7.3)
3. [[long-duration-current-impulse-withstand-test|Long duration current impulse
   withstand test]] (§5.8, §7.4)
4. [[operating-duty-test|Operating duty tests]] (§5.9, §7.5)
5. [[pressure-relief|Pressure relief test]] (§5.11)
6. [[arrester-disconnector|Tests of arrester disconnectors]] (§5.12, §7.6)
7. Artificial pollution test (Annex F, not yet established)
8. [[partial-discharge-test|Partial discharge test]] (§5.4)
9. [[seal-leakage|Seal leakage test]] (not yet established)
10. [[current-distribution-multi-column|Current distribution test]] for
    multi-column arresters (§5.6, §8.1e)

Arresters differing only in mounting/support but otherwise of the same components
and construction (same performance, heat-dissipation and internal atmosphere) are
considered the **same design** (§7.1).
