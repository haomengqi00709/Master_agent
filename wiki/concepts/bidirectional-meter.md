# Bidirectional meter

A **meter intended to measure the energy in both directions** (IEC 387 §3.10) —
i.e. energy both received and supplied at the measuring point (import and
export). It is the metering concept behind net or two-way energy accounting.

Under the tariff-device rules (§9 d): if the meter registers energy received and
energy supplied by means of **two sets of dials or drums, each shall be marked
with an arrow pointing in the appropriate direction**. For reactive-energy meters
registering energy received or supplied, a supplementary plate shall be provided
for the symbols given in 9.5. Table 6 gives the marking pair (§9):
**energy received** at the measuring point (e.g. import) and **energy supplied**
(e.g. export), each a distinct directional symbol. Symbols 9.6–9.10 (the
instantaneous/actual average-demand value and the highest average demand value
for the present cumulation/billing period) are assigned for static maximum-demand
devices with a display and may accompany a bidirectional static meter.

See [[tariff-device-symbols]] for the full scheme and
[[maximum-demand-meter]] for the demand-display side. General graphical link:
[[08-04-04]] (watt-hour meter, one direction) contrasts with the two-direction
case.
