# Routine verification and routine tests (encapsulation "m")

The clause 9 checks applied to **every** unit of encapsulated apparatus, parts or
[[ex-component-encapsulation|Ex components]] in production (as opposed to the once-off
type tests of clause 8) (IEC 79-18). They supplement clause 22 of IEC 79-0.

- **9.1 Visual check.** Each unit is inspected; **no visible damage of the
  [[encapsulation-compound|compound]]** shall be evident — cracks, exposure of
  encapsulated parts, flaking, impermissible shrinkage, swelling, decomposition or
  softening (the same defect list as the type-test acceptance criteria,
  [[thermal-tests|§8.2.1.3]]).
- **9.2 Electric strength test.** The [[apparatus-electric-strength-test|§8.2.3 electric
  strength test]] conditions are applied: between separate circuits accessible from the
  outside; between all externally accessible circuits (commoned) and all external metal
  parts (commoned); and between every externally accessible circuit operating above
  **60 V** and every externally accessible part close to that circuit.
- **9.3 Checking the electrical data.** It is verified that the electrical
  characteristics (voltage, current, active power, etc.) comply with the specification
  submitted to the authority.
- **9.4 Voltage transformers.** For voltage transformers with voltages **above 1 kV**,
  the level of **partial discharges** shall comply with **IEC 44-4**.
