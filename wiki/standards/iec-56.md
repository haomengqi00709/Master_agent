# IEC 56 — High-voltage AC circuit-breakers

*Disjoncteurs à courant alternatif à haute tension.* 4th edition, 1987.

A **specification standard** (not a symbol dictionary): it defines the ratings,
design requirements and tests for high-voltage AC [[circuit-breaker]]s (rated
voltages above 1000 V). Distilled here into wiki form per the LLM-Wiki method —
the device and each rated characteristic / test concept is its own concept page,
cross-linked to the [[60617-7]] switchgear symbols.

## Scope (clause 1)
Requirements for high-voltage AC circuit-breakers designed for indoor/outdoor
installation, for operation at frequencies 50/60 Hz on systems with voltages
above 1000 V — making, carrying and breaking normal and short-circuit currents.

  tightness tests, and applies figure/notation corrections (E→U). No new ratings.
