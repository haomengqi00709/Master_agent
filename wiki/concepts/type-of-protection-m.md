# Type of protection "m" — encapsulation

A type of protection in which the parts that could ignite an explosive atmosphere
by **either sparking or heating are enclosed in a [[encapsulation-compound|compound]]**
in such a way that the explosive atmosphere cannot be ignited (IEC 79-18 §3.1).
Unlike a flameproof enclosure ([[type-of-protection-d|"d"]]), which contains an
internal explosion, "m" *excludes* the explosive gas from the ignition-capable
parts altogether by embedding them in a solid resin. It is the subject of the whole
of [[iec-79-18]] and is signified by the mark **"m"** (IEC 79-18 §10.1.1).

Protection is achieved and verified through several concrete requirements, each its
own page: a documented [[encapsulation-compound|compound]] kept within its
[[temperature-range-of-the-compound|temperature range]] and
[[continuous-operating-temperature]]; encapsulation made **without voids**
([[free-space-and-voids]], §5.1.2); a minimum [[thickness-of-compound|compound thickness]]
(3 mm, §5.2) and minimum [[distances-through-compound|distances through the compound]]
(Table 1, §5.3); a [[external-connections-and-sealing|seal at conductor entries]]
(§5.5) and [[temperature-limitation|temperature limitation]] (§5.4). Protection must
be maintained under recognised overloads and any single internal electrical fault
(§5.1.4), subject to the [[fault-not-considered-components|components and distances not considered faultable]].

The standard applies to apparatus, parts of apparatus and
[[ex-component-encapsulation|Ex components]] with rated voltages **≤ 11 kV**
(IEC 79-18 §1), and supplements IEC 79-0 with stated exceptions. The protection is
proven by the type tests of clause 8 ([[thermal-tests]], [[thermal-cycling-test]],
[[apparatus-electric-strength-test]], [[cable-pull-test]]) plus compound tests
([[compound-electric-strength-test]], [[water-absorption-test]]) and the
[[non-specific-tests-encapsulation|non-specific tests]] of annex C.
