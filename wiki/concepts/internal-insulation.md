# Internal insulation

Internal insulation consists of the internal solid, liquid or gaseous parts of the insulation of equipment, which are protected from the effects of atmospheric and other external conditions ([[iec-56]] 3.101.21). Being shielded from pollution, humidity and weather, its performance is governed by the intrinsic dielectric strength of the enclosed media rather than by ambient conditions. A disruptive discharge in a solid internal dielectric produces permanent loss of strength, making such insulation [[non-self-restoring-insulation]], whereas a discharge in an enclosed liquid or gaseous dielectric may recover, making it [[self-restoring-insulation]] ([[iec-56]] 3.101.24, Note 2). It complements the [[external-insulation]], which is exposed to open air; the combined withstand capability of both is expressed by the equipment's [[rated-insulation-level]].
