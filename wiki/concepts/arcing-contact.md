# Arcing contact

An arcing contact is a contact on which the arc is established ([[iec-56]] 3.103.8, IEV 441-15-08). It is provided in the [[main-circuit]] of each [[pole]] specifically to take the arc during making and breaking, thereby protecting the [[main-contact]] (3.103.7) from arc erosion. In operation the arcing contacts are arranged to part after the main contacts on opening and to touch before them on closing, so the current transfers to the arcing contacts before the gap that arcs is created. Because they bear the thermal and erosive stress of arcing, arcing contacts are typically made of arc-resistant material (such as a tungsten alloy) and are renewable. They work together with the breaker's arc-control device (3.103.17) and the arc-extinguishing medium, and are a defined contact type (3.103.5) of the [[circuit-breaker]].
