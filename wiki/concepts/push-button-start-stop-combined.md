# Push-button — combined START/STOP & inching (neutral)

**IEC 73 §5.3.** Two cases where a single [[push-button]] cannot be given a
single start-or-stop meaning, so a **neutral** colour is required:

- **§5.3.1 — Alternating buttons.** Push-buttons which, when pressed several times,
  act **alternately as START and STOP** (or ON and OFF) shall be **BLACK, WHITE or
  GREY**. **Neither RED nor GREEN shall be used** — because the button is sometimes
  a start and sometimes a stop, it must not carry either reserved/preferred colour
  ([[push-button-stop-emergency]] RED, [[push-button-start-on]] GREEN).

- **§5.3.2 — Inching / jogging buttons.** Push-buttons which cause a movement
  **while pressed** and stop the movement **when released** (inching or jogging
  buttons) shall be **BLACK, WHITE, GREY or GREEN**, with a **preference for
  BLACK**. Such buttons **shall not be RED** (they are not stop functions).

The common thread is that an ambiguous or momentary action must not borrow the
reserved RED stop-meaning, and the alternating case must also avoid GREEN to
prevent it being read as a dedicated start. These rules map onto the Table II
neutral row ([[push-button-colour-table]]): BLACK/GREY/WHITE = *no specific meaning
assigned*, usable for any function except a sole STOP/OFF function.
