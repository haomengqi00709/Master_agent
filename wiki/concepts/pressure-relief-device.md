# Pressure-relief device of an arrester

A **pressure-relief device of an arrester** is a means for relieving internal pressure in an arrester
and preventing explosive shattering of the housing following prolonged passage of [[follow-current]]
or internal flashover of the arrester ([[iec-99-1]] §9). Should an arrester fail internally — for
example if the [[series-gap|gaps]] or [[non-linear-series-resistor|resistors]] are bridged by an
internal arc — the fault current heats and pressurises the housing; without relief the housing could
explode and endanger personnel and adjacent equipment. The pressure-relief class for arresters so
fitted must appear on the [[arrester-identification|rating plate]] (§50) and is verified by the
[[pressure-relief-test]] (§65).

The five **pressure-relief classes (A–E)** are defined by minimum prospective symmetrical fault
current in [[iec-99-1]] Table V: class A = 40 000 A, B = 20 000 A, C = 10 000 A (all for 10 000 A
light- or heavy-duty arresters), D = 10 000 A and E = 5 000 A (for 5 000 A Series A or B arresters).
The test (§65) is made on a sample in a new housing — separately at high current (§65.2) and low
current (800 A r.m.s., §65.3) — with the series gaps and resistor elements bypassed by a fuse wire so
that internal arcing is initiated. The arrester passes if the housing remains intact, or breaks
sufficiently non-explosively that all parts are contained within a prescribed circular enclosure.
Note that an [[arrester-disconnector]] (§49) does *not* perform pressure relief and may not prevent
explosive shattering on high fault currents.

**In the gapless metal-oxide standard [[iec-99-4]]:** the same device is defined identically
(§2.7) and the requirement is **[[pressure-relief]]** (§5.11) — the arrester failure shall not cause
explosive shattering of the housing, verified by the test in clause 8.7 of [[iec-99-1]] (other
conditions under consideration); the sample passes if the housing stays intact or breaks
non-explosively with all parts contained. There the **pressure-relief rated current** (kA r.m.s.) is
the nameplate quantity rather than 99-1's A–E classes, and the test applies to the 1 500 A–20 000 A
classes per [[arrester-classification]] Table 1 (when fitted).
