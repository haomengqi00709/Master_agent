# Certificate references

A certificate reference number may refer to a single design or a range of similar apparatus. The suffix “X” added to the certificate number indicates special conditions of use — the certification documents must be studied before such apparatus is installed, repaired, overhauled, reclaimed or modified.

Defined in [[iec-79-19]] 2.2.12.
