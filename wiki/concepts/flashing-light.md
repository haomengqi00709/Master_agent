# Flashing light

**IEC 73 §3.4.** A **steady light** is normally used for [[indicator-light]]s and
for [[illuminated-push-button]]s. For further distinction or information, and
especially to give **additional emphasis**, flashing lights may be used for the
following purposes:

- a) to **attract further attention**;
- b) to **request immediate action**;
- c) to indicate a **discrepancy** between a commanded state and the actual state
  of the related equipment;
- d) to indicate a **change in progress** (flashing during a transition period).

Where flashing lights of **different frequencies** convey information of different
priority, the **rapidly flashing** light shall be used for the **higher-priority**
information. A note specifies the timing: in general the **ON/OFF time ratio
should lie between 1:1 and 4:1**, i.e. the ON time should be at least equal to the
OFF time.

Flashing interacts with the colour code rather than replacing it: a flashing alarm
button may, when pressed, change to a **steady** light while keeping its
clause-5 function ([[illuminated-push-button-modes-of-use]]). The WHITE
"double-confirmation" sequence — flashing to confirm a starting/transition
operation, then automatically steady to confirm normal running — is a §6.3
application of this clause. See also [[indicator-light-modes-of-use]].
