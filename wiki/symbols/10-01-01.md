# 10-01-01 — Telephony

**Standard:** [[60617-10]] · **Section:** [[60617-10-01-lines-and-circuit-usage]]

## Description
Telephony. Symbol 05-01-01 is used to represent a line or other telecommunication circuit. The usage of circuits may be indicated by letters, symbols 10-01-01 to 10-01-04.
