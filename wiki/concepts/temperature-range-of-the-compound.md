# Temperature range of the compound

The **range of temperatures within which the properties of the
[[encapsulation-compound|compound]], in either operation or storage, permit
compliance with the requirements of the standard** (IEC 79-18 §3.3). It is one of
the two thermal characteristics the compound manufacturer must declare (the other
being the [[continuous-operating-temperature]]), and §4.2 requires it to appear in
the material description.

The range is load-bearing for testing. The [[compound-electric-strength-test]]
(8.1.1) must be performed at **the highest temperature of the temperature range
defined in §3.3** — a 50 mm × 3 mm disc is held at 4 kV for at least 5 min at that
upper-bound temperature with no flashover or breakdown allowed. The range also
bounds normal service: §5.4 ([[temperature-limitation]]) forbids exceeding the
compound's rated temperatures, and the [[thermal-cycling-test]] (8.2.1.2) exercises
the sample between (T_amax + 10) °C and (T_amin − 5) °C around the specified ambient
limits while confirming the internal temperature stays within the
[[continuous-operating-temperature]].
