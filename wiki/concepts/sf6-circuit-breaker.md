# Sulphur hexafluoride (SF6) circuit-breaker

A sulphur hexafluoride (SF6) circuit-breaker is a [[circuit-breaker]] in which the arc is extinguished in sulphur hexafluoride gas ([[iec-56]] 3.102.10, IEV 441-14-31). SF6 is an electronegative gas with excellent arc-quenching and dielectric properties; it captures free electrons to give rapid dielectric recovery, allowing compact interrupters at the highest transmission voltages. It is a specific member of the [[gas-blast-circuit-breaker]] family (3.102.9), paralleling the [[air-blast-circuit-breaker]] which instead blasts compressed air. SF6 designs may be built as a [[dead-tank-circuit-breaker]] or [[live-tank-circuit-breaker]], the latter often a stacked [[module]] arrangement per [[pole]]. Each pole carries rated current through its [[main-contact]] and transfers the arc to the [[arcing-contact]], with the gas flow (puffer or self-blast) directed across the arc at separation.
