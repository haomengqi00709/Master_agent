# IP first numeral 4 — wire / objects ≥ 1,0 mm

First characteristic numeral **4** of the [[ip-code-structure|IP Code]]
([[iec-529]], 1st ed. Table I; 2nd ed. clause 5, Table V):

- **Access (persons):** protection against access with a **wire** — verified with
  the [[ip-access-probe|test wire]] of **1,0 mm diameter × 100 mm long**
  (corresponds to [[ip-additional-letter|additional letter D]]). 1976 wording:
  "wires or strips of thickness greater than 1,0 mm".
- **Solid objects (equipment):** excludes solid foreign objects of **1,0 mm
  diameter and greater**.

**Test ([[ip-first-numeral-tests]], 1st ed. §7.4; 2nd ed. Table V):** a straight
rigid steel wire of **1,0 mm diameter** (end free from burrs, at right angles to
its length) is applied with a force of **1 N ± 10%**. The protection is
satisfactory if the wire **cannot enter** the enclosure and adequate clearance is
kept. As for numeral 3, interpretation for equipment with ventilating openings or
drain holes is the relevant Technical Committee's responsibility (1st ed. §7.4).
A note in Table I records that numerals 1–4 exclude both regularly and
irregularly shaped objects provided three mutually perpendicular dimensions
exceed the stated figure.

Subsumes [[ip-first-numeral-3]]. The next level [[ip-first-numeral-5]] is the
**dust** regime, tested differently (a dust chamber, not a probe).
