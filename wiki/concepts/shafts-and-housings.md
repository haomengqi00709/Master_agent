# Shafts and housings — Ex reclamation

Shafts and bearing housings (including flameproof joints), reclaimed by metal spraying, sleeving or welding — any machining to the specified flamepath dimensions.

In [[iec-79-19]], repair/overhaul/reclamation of this component is specified per type of protection:

- **[[type-of-protection-d|"d" — flameproof enclosure]]** (clause 3): Shafts and bearing housings, including flameproof joints, may be reclaimed e.g. by metal spraying or sleeving, but any subsequent machining shall be to the flamepath dimensions specified in the apparatus standard/certification; welding may be appropriate with due regard to its limitations (3.3.2).
- **[[type-of-protection-p|"p" — pressurization]]** (clause 5): Reclaim shafts and bearing housings by metal spraying or sleeving; welding may be appropriate with due regard to its limitations (5.3.2).
- **[[type-of-protection-e|"e" — increased safety]]** (clause 6): Reclaim shafts and bearing housings by metal spraying or sleeving; welding may be appropriate with due regard to its limitations (6.3.1.3).
- **[[type-of-protection-n|"n" — non-sparking]]** (clause 7): Reclaim shafts and bearing housings, preferably by metal spraying or sleeving; welding may be appropriate with due regard to its limitations (7.3.3.1).
