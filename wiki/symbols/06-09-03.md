# 06-09-03 — Transformer with two windings, with instantaneous voltage polarity indicators

![06-09-03](../assets/60617-6/p16.png)
<sub>This symbol appears on Publication No.617-6.pdf, printed p.18 (full page shown).</sub>

**Standard:** [[60617-6]] · **Section:** [[60617-6-09-general-symbols-transformers-and]]

## Description
Transformer with two windings, shown with instantaneous voltage polarity indicators. Instantaneous currents entering the marked ends of the windings produce aiding fluxes.

## Names
- **EN:** Transformer with two windings, with instantaneous voltage polarity indicators
- **FR:** Transformateur à deux enroulements, figuré avec indicateurs de polarité instantanée des tensions

## Notes
- The instantaneous voltage polarities may be indicated in form 2 of the symbol.
