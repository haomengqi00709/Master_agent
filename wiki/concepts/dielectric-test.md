# Dielectric test

The dielectric test verifies that the circuit-breaker withstands the overvoltages defined by its [[rated-insulation-level]], proving the integrity of insulation to earth, between poles and across the open gap. Per [[iec-56]] 6.1 (with IEC 694 6.1), it comprises power-frequency withstand tests (6.1.7), lightning-impulse and switching-impulse voltage tests (6.1.6) at the rated withstand voltages, plus wet tests for outdoor breakers and artificial-pollution tests (6.1.8). Test voltages and conditions follow Tables IV/VIII-X. For breakers at or above 300 kV the open-gap power-frequency test applies 2.5 U/sqrt(3) across the break using two out-of-phase sources; impulse tests across the open break may add 0.7 U sqrt(2)/sqrt(3) to U_p. Self-restoring external insulation passing on repeat after a single disruptive wet-test discharge is acceptable ([[iec-56]] 6.1.7). Related: [[partial-discharge-test]], [[radio-interference-voltage-test]].
