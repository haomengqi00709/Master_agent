# Rated value

A rated value is a quantity value assigned, generally by a manufacturer, for a specified operating condition of a component, device or equipment ([[iec-56]] 3.105.1, IEV 151-04-03). It is the reference figure a circuit-breaker is designed and guaranteed to meet, so that the breaker in correct condition of maintenance and adjustment can withstand all service stresses provided they do not exceed its rated characteristics ([[iec-56]] clause 4). The standard enumerates the rated characteristics that define the rating, including rated voltage, [[rated-insulation-level]], rated frequency, rated normal current, rated short-time and peak withstand currents, rated short-circuit breaking and making currents, rated transient [[recovery-voltage]] for terminal faults, and rated operating sequence ([[iec-56]] clause 4). Each such rated characteristic is a specific instance of the general rated-value concept defined here.
