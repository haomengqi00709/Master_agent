# 08-09-02 — Synchro, general symbol

![08-09-02](../assets/60617-8/p17.png)
<sub>This symbol appears on Publication No.617-8.pdf, printed p.19 (full page shown).</sub>

**Standard:** [[60617-8]] · **Section:** [[60617-8-09-miscellaneous-measuring-elements-and]]

## Description
Synchro, general symbol. The asterisk must be replaced by letters for the particular synchro: first letter C=Control, T=Torque, R=Resolver; succeeding letter D=Differential, R=Receiver, T=Transformer, X=Transmitter, B=Rotatable stator winding. Inner circle = rotor, outer circle = stator.
