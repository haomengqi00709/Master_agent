# Time sequence chart or table

The second example of an [[explanatory-chart-or-table]] in [[iec-113-1]] (§4.2). It
is one which, in addition to giving the successive operations in a specified order,
**takes account of the time intervals between successive operations** (§4.2). It
thus extends the [[sequence-chart-or-table]] (§4.1) with a time dimension.

In terms of the [[chart-graph]] definition (§2.2), the plain sequence chart covers
the "different operations" case (§2.2(a)) while the time-sequence chart covers the
"operations and time" case (§2.2(b)) — the explicit representation of *when*, and
with what spacing, operations follow one another. Its explanatory purpose, shared
with the rest of the §4 family, is to facilitate the study of the related
[[explanatory-diagram]]s and to give additional information. Either form may be
rendered as a [[table-document]] (§2.2).
