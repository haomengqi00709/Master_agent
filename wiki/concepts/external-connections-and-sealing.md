# External connections and sealing (encapsulation "m")

How conductors enter the [[encapsulation-compound|compound]] and how bare live parts
leave it — the boundary where the encapsulation must stay gas-tight (IEC 79-18 §5.5,
§5.6).

- **§5.5.1 Seal at conductor entry:** the entrance of **all electrical conductors,
  including cables**, into the compound shall ensure a **seal against the possible
  entry of explosive atmosphere** into the encapsulated apparatus, parts or
  [[ex-component-encapsulation|Ex components]]. This can be obtained, for example, by
  having **at least 5 mm of bare conductor within the compound** (a sufficient bonded
  length so the gas cannot track along the conductor surface).
- **§5.5.2 Permanently connected cable:** where connection is by a cable permanently
  connected to the apparatus or its parts, the [[cable-pull-test]] of §8.2.2 shall be
  carried out (verifying no displacement between compound and cable under tension).
- **§5.6 Bare live parts through the surface:** bare live parts that **pass through the
  surface** of the compound shall be protected by **one of the types of protection
  listed in 1.1 of IEC 79-0** — i.e. the protection is handed off to another Ex type
  at the point the conductor emerges.

This couples to [[thickness-of-compound]] (the compound depth over those conductors)
and to [[adhesion]] (no moisture path between compound and partially-embedded parts).
