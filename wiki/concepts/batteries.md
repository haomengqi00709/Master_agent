# Batteries — Ex repair

Batteries in Ex apparatus — replace only with manufacturer-specified types; where encapsulated, replace the whole assembly.

In [[iec-79-19]], repair/overhaul/reclamation of this component is specified per type of protection:

- **[[type-of-protection-d|"d" — flameproof enclosure]]** (clause 3): Where batteries are used, the manufacturer's advice should be followed (3.2.9).
- **[[type-of-protection-i|"i" — intrinsic safety]]** (clause 4): Only types specified in the equipment manufacturer's instructions should be used as replacements, and where batteries are encapsulated the whole assembly should be replaced (4.2.11).
- **[[type-of-protection-p|"p" — pressurization]]** (clause 5): Where batteries are used, the manufacturer's advice should be followed (5.2.9).
- **[[type-of-protection-e|"e" — increased safety]]** (clause 6): Where batteries are used, reference shall be made to the manufacturer's instructions before any repair or replacement (6.2.9).
- **[[type-of-protection-n|"n" — non-sparking]]** (clause 7): Where batteries are used, reference shall be made to the manufacturer's instructions before any repair or replacement (7.2.9).
