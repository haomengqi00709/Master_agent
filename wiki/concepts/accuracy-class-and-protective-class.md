# Accuracy class, meter constant and insulating/protective class (clause 7)

IEC 387 §7 and **Table 4** bring together three name-plate/dial markings (given
as examples, items 7.1–7.4):

- **7.1 Accuracy class** — the metrological class, e.g. *class 1*, shown as the
  class number enclosed in a symbol or written "Cl. 1". The class corresponds to
  the meter's maximum permissible error and is the subject of the TC 13 product
  standards (IEC 514, IEC 521, IEC 687, IEC 1036).
- **7.2 Meter constant for induction meters** — expressed in **revolutions per
  measuring unit** (e.g. 500 rev/kWh) or measuring units per revolution (2
  Wh/rev). See [[meter-constant]] and [[induction-meter]].
- **7.3 Meter constant for static energy meters** — expressed in **pulses per
  measuring unit** (e.g. 500 imp/kWh) or measuring units per pulse (2 Wh/imp).
  See [[meter-constant]] and [[static-energy-meter]].
- **7.4 Insulating / protective class** — a meter of **protective class II**
  carries the double-square class II symbol, marking its insulation/protection
  class.

These markings, with the unit of the measured quantity ([[measured-quantity-marking]]),
are the principal metrological declarations on a meter's
[[meter-name-plate-and-dial|name-plate and dial]]. The [[meter-constant]] item is
expanded on its own page.
