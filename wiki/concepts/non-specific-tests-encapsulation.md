# Non-specific tests (annex C, encapsulation "m")

The **annex C** (normative) environmental-conditioning and resistance tests for
[[type-of-protection-m|encapsulation "m"]] (IEC 79-18). Most prepare the
[[encapsulation-compound|compound]] specimens for the evaluative tests that follow in
the **annex B** sequences; some carry their own pass criterion.

- **C.1.1 Thermal endurance to heat.** Continuous storage of the samples for **four
  weeks** at **90–95 % relative humidity** and a temperature **20–22 K above the maximum
  service temperature, but at least 80 °C**. If the maximum service temperature exceeds
  **75 °C**, the four weeks are replaced by **two weeks at 90–97 °C** (90–95 % RH)
  followed by **two weeks at 20–22 K above the maximum service temperature**.
- **C.1.2 Thermal endurance to cold.** Storage for **24 h** at the **minimum service
  temperature reduced by at least 5 K but at most 10 K**.
- **C.2.1 Resistance to chemical agents** (group I): two samples — one held **24 h in
  oil No. 2** (ISO 1817 reference immersion liquids) at **50 °C**, the other **24–26 h in
  a hydraulic liquid** (aqueous polymer solution in 35 % water); afterwards wiped and
  stored 24 h in laboratory atmosphere.
- **C.3.1 Resistance to light** (group II, if the surface is not protected from light):
  **six test bars (50 × 6 × 4 mm)** per ISO 179, exposed per **ISO 4892** in a
  xenon-lamp chamber with sunlight-simulating filter, **black-panel 55–58 °C**, for
  **1 000 h**. The criterion is impact bending strength (ISO 179): after exposure it
  shall be **at least 50 %** of the unexposed value; where no rupture occurs before
  exposure, **not more than three** of the exposed bars may break.

The thermal-endurance steps (C.1.1/C.1.2) include **no evaluation criteria** themselves —
they condition the samples ahead of the [[compound-electric-strength-test]],
[[water-absorption-test]] and the assembled-apparatus tests, per the
[[sampling-encapsulation|annex B sequences]].
