# Light transmitting parts — Ex repair

Viewing windows / luminaire lenses; not repairable or re-cemented — replace with complete manufacturer assemblies, and never clean plastics with solvent (household detergent only).

In [[iec-79-19]], repair/overhaul/reclamation of this component is specified per type of protection:

- **[[type-of-protection-d|"d" — flameproof enclosure]]** (clause 3): No attempt shall be made to re-cement or repair light transmitting parts; only complete replacement assemblies specified by the manufacturer shall be used, and plastics parts shall not be cleaned with solvents (household detergents recommended) (3.2.7).
- **[[type-of-protection-p|"p" — pressurization]]** (clause 5): Plastics light transmitting parts shall not be cleaned with solvent; household detergents are recommended (5.2.7).
- **[[type-of-protection-e|"e" — increased safety]]** (clause 6): No attempt shall be made to repair light transmitting parts and only manufacturer replacement components used; plastics parts shall not be cleaned with solvents (household detergents may be used) (6.2.7).
- **[[type-of-protection-n|"n" — non-sparking]]** (clause 7): Plastics light transmitting or other parts should not be cleaned with solvents; household detergents may be used (7.2.7).
