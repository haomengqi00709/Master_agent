# IEC 99-1 — Lightning arresters, Part 1: Non-linear resistor type arresters for a.c. systems

**Committee:** IEC Technical Committee No. 37, *Lightning Arresters* · **Edition:** Second edition, 1970 (supersedes the first edition of 1958).

This Recommendation specifies the requirements and test procedures for **surge protective
devices designed for repeated operation to limit voltage surges on a.c. power circuits and to
interrupt power follow current** — in particular [[surge-arrester|lightning arresters]] consisting of
single or multiple spark gaps in series with one or more non-linear resistors (IEC 99-1 §1, the
[[non-linear-resistor-type-arrester|valve / non-linear resistor type]]). It covers normal service
conditions, definitions, standard ratings, classification, the general testing procedure, the full
suite of [[type-test|type (design) tests]], and routine and acceptance tests. The graphical symbol
for the device is [[07-22-03]] (*lightning arrester / parafoudre*) in [[60617-7]] §22; the series gap
element is [[07-22-01]].

## Normal service conditions (§2)
Arresters conforming to this Recommendation are suitable for: ambient temperature −40 °C to
+40 °C; altitude not exceeding 1 000 m; supply frequency 48–62 Hz; and power-frequency voltage
between line and earth terminals not exceeding the [[rated-voltage-arrester|rated voltage]]. Other
conditions are *abnormal service conditions* requiring special consideration (Appendix B).
