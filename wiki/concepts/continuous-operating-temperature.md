# Continuous operating temperature of the compound

The **maximum temperature to which the [[encapsulation-compound|compound]] can be
subjected continuously, according to the data given by the manufacturer of the
compound** (IEC 79-18 §3.4). With the [[temperature-range-of-the-compound]] it is
one of the two compound thermal characteristics the manufacturer must declare and
include in the material description (§4.2).

It is a hard limit in service and in test. Under §5.4
([[temperature-limitation]]), none of the marked maximum surface temperature,
temperature class, or continuous operating temperature of the compound may be
exceeded in normal service. The [[thermal-cycling-test]] (8.2.1.2) explicitly
checks that **the internal temperature does not exceed the specified continuous
operating temperature of the compound** (cross-referencing §3.4 and §5.4) during
hot-side energization. One controlled exception exists: for
[[encapsulated-fuses|encapsulated fuses]] (§6.2), the momentary temperature caused
by the fuse rupturing **may exceed** the continuous operating temperature provided
the type of protection "m" is not affected (e.g. no cracks) and the marked surface
temperature / temperature class is still not exceeded.
