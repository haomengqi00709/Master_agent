# Lampholders — Ex repair

Lampholders are invariably specific types (single-pin for fluorescent, screw for others); use only manufacturer-specified replacements and do not rewire factory-made connections unless equipped to the same standard.

In [[iec-79-19]], repair/overhaul/reclamation of this component is specified per type of protection:

- **[[type-of-protection-d|"d" — flameproof enclosure]]** (clause 3): Replacement lampholders listed by the manufacturer should be used (3.2.11).
- **[[type-of-protection-p|"p" — pressurization]]** (clause 5): Replacements listed by the manufacturer should be used (5.2.11).
- **[[type-of-protection-e|"e" — increased safety]]** (clause 6): Only manufacturer-specified replacements shall be used; where wiring to the lampholder is factory-made (crimps, etc.) rewiring shall not be undertaken unless the repairer can make it to the same standard (lampholders are invariably specific types — single-pin for fluorescent, screw for others) (6.2.11).
- **[[type-of-protection-n|"n" — non-sparking]]** (clause 7): Replacements listed by the manufacturer shall be used (7.2.11).
