# Pressure-relief tests (Type test, §65)

The **pressure-relief tests** of [[iec-99-1]] §65 are made on arresters fitted with a
[[pressure-relief-device]], to show that an arrester failure will not cause explosive shattering of
the housing. Each test is made on a sample in a **new housing**: one sample at high current (§65.2)
and a separate sample at low current (§65.3). To initiate internal current flow, all
[[series-gap|series gaps]] and [[non-linear-series-resistor|resistor elements]] are bypassed by a fuse
wire that melts within the first 30 electrical degrees after current initiation. The sample is mounted
to simulate installation and surrounded by an approximately circular enclosure at least 30 cm high
whose diameter equals the sample diameter plus twice the sample height (minimum 1.8 m). The supply
frequency is 48–62 Hz. **The sample passes if the housing remains intact, or breaks sufficiently
non-explosively that all parts are contained within the enclosure.**

**High-current test (§65.2).** Made on the longest arrester of each design, demonstrating all ratings
of that design. The test is normally made on a single-phase circuit at **77 % (−0, +30 %) of rated
voltage** (the voltage seen by an arrester rated 75 % of system phase-to-phase voltage), short-circuit
power factor ≤ 0.1. The current must flow for at least 0.2 s. Compliance is demonstrated against one of
the **pressure-relief classes A–E** (Table V), defined by minimum prospective symmetrical fault
current: A = 40 000 A, B = 20 000 A, C = 10 000 A (10 000 A arresters), D = 10 000 A, E = 5 000 A
(5 000 A arresters). Sub-clauses §65.2.1/§65.2.2 give procedures at, and below, 77 % rated voltage.

**Low-current test (§65.3).** Any rating of the design may be used. At a test voltage of 77 %
(−0, +30 %) of sample rating, the circuit is adjusted to drive **800 A r.m.s. (±10 %)** through the
sample (measured ~0.1 s after start); current flows until venting occurs.

The pressure-relief class must appear on the [[arrester-identification|rating plate]]. (Note: an
[[arrester-disconnector]] does not provide pressure relief.)
