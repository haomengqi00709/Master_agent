# IEC 387 — Symbols for alternating-current electricity meters

*Symbols for alternating-current electricity meters / Symboles pour compteurs à
courant alternatif.* **Second edition, 1992-07**, prepared by **IEC Technical
Committee No. 13: Equipment for electrical energy measurement and load control.**
This edition cancels and replaces the first edition issued in 1972. Annex A
forms an integral part of the standard.

Unlike the [[index#standards|60617 parts]] (a general graphical-symbol
dictionary), IEC 387 is a **dedicated marking/symbol standard for one product
family — a.c. electricity meters**. The symbols may be **substituted for the
markings given in different national languages** and explain the meter's
technical characteristics irrespective of the country for which the meter is
intended. Decomposed here per the LLM-Wiki method: each definition and each
symbol-table clause is its own concept page.

## Scope (clause 1)
Applies to **letter and graphical symbols for a.c. electricity meters and their
auxiliary devices**, independent of induction or static measurement elements.
All the symbols may be marked on the **name-plate, dial plate, external labels
or accessories** of the meter. See [[meter-symbol-marking]].
