# IEC 56-2 — Rated characteristics (Part 2)

**IEC Publication 56-2 (3rd edition, 1971), "High-voltage alternating-current
circuit-breakers — Part 2: Rating"** is the standalone *ratings* part of the
earlier, six-part edition of [[iec-56]]. At that time Publication 56 was split
into separate booklets — Part 1 General and Definitions, **Part 2 Rating**,
Part 3 Design and Construction, Part 4 Type Tests and Routine Tests, Part 5
Selection for Service, Part 6 Information/Transport/Erection/Maintenance. The
ratings clauses of this Part 2 were later **consolidated into clause 4 of the
4th-edition [[iec-56]] (1987)**, so it is treated here as an earlier edition of
the same material, **not** a separate parallel standard. Prepared by IEC SC 17A
and approved 1969-1970; it carried Amendments 1 (1974), 2 (1975) and 3 (1977).

It enumerates the rated characteristics in three tiers that the consolidated
standard preserves: (1.1) those **always given** — [[rated-voltage]],
[[rated-insulation-level]], [[rated-frequency]], [[rated-normal-current]],
[[rated-short-circuit-breaking-current]], rated [[transient-recovery-voltage]]
for terminal faults, [[rated-short-circuit-making-current]] and
[[rated-operating-sequence]]; (1.2) those **given in specific cases** —
[[short-line-fault]] characteristics (three-pole breakers directly connected to
overhead lines, ≥52 kV and >12.5 kA), [[rated-duration-of-short-circuit]] (when
no direct over-current release is fitted), [[rated-line-charging-breaking-current]]
(overhead-line switching ≥72.5 kV), and rated supply voltage/frequency and
compressed-gas pressure where applicable; and (1.3) those **given on request** —
[[rated-out-of-phase-breaking-current]], [[rated-cable-charging-breaking-current]],
[[rated-single-capacitor-bank-breaking-current]], [[rated-small-inductive-breaking-current]],
and rated supply voltage/frequency of auxiliary circuits.

## Notable Part-2 detail folded into the 4th edition
- **Standard rated-voltage lists** (clause 2): Series I 3.6 … 72.5 kV and the
  above-72.5 kV list up to 765 kV — see [[rated-voltage]].
- **Insulation Tables I/II/III** (clause 3) for full vs reduced insulation; the
  British NC objected to the column-3 power-frequency values (List 2 of IEC 71),
  and Amendment 1 (1971) recast clause 3 into Lists 1/2 and Tables I-IV.
- **TRV representation** (clause 7): the two-parameter (uc, t3) and four-parameter
  (u1, t1, uc, t2) envelopes plus delay line ta originate here — see
  [[transient-recovery-voltage]]. Tables VA/VC give first-pole-to-clear factors
  1.5 (≤100 kV) and 1.3 (>100 kV).
- **Short-line-fault** line characteristics (clause 8, Table VII): rated surge
  impedance Z = 480/375/330 Ω and peak factor k by conductors-per-phase, with
  RRRV factor s; Appendix A gives the full SLF-TRV calculation. Amendment 1
  later replaced Table VII with a single Z = 450 Ω, k = 1.6 row.
- **Rated duration of short-circuit** standard value 1 s (3 s if longer needed) —
  see [[rated-duration-of-short-circuit]].
- **Co-ordination tables** (clause 21, Tables XIII-XV) pairing rated voltage,
  short-circuit breaking current and normal current as non-mandatory preferred
  combinations.
