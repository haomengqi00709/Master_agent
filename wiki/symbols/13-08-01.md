# 13-08-01 — Amplifier, general symbol

![13-08-01](../assets/60617-13/p15.png)
<sub>This symbol appears on Publication No.617-13.pdf, printed p.27 (full page shown).</sub>

**Standard:** [[60617-13]] · **Section:** [[60617-13-08-amplifiers]] · **Form 3**

## Description
Amplifier, general symbol (Form 3). f may be replaced by a qualifying symbol for an additional function: Σ summing, ∫ integration, d/dt differentiation, exp exponentiation, log logarithm, SH sample-and-hold. m is the common amplification factor. For forms 1 and 2 see symbols 10-15-01 and 10-15-02 of IEC 617-10.
