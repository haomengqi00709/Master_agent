# Electric strength test for the apparatus (encapsulation "m")

The §8.2.3 type test that proves the dielectric integrity of the **assembled**
encapsulated apparatus — between circuits, to earth, and to the surface of the
[[encapsulation-compound|compound]] (IEC 79-18). Distinct from the
[[compound-electric-strength-test|test on bare compound specimens]] (§8.1.1), this is
applied to the finished product. It is carried out between:

- **a)** galvanically separated circuits;
- **b)** each circuit and all earthed parts;
- **c)** each circuit and the surface of the compound (which may be clad with a
  conductive foil if necessary).

**Test voltage:** **500 V r.m.s.** for apparatus with supply voltages not exceeding
**90 V peak**; otherwise **2 U + 1 000 V** with a minimum of **1 500 V a.c.**, at
**48–62 Hz** (or a d.c. voltage equal to the a.c. peak where an a.c. test would damage
the electronics). Here **U** is the sum of the rated voltages of the two circuits for
(a), and the rated voltage of the circuit under test for (b) and (c). The voltage is
**raised steadily over not less than 10 s** and **held for at least 60 s**; **no
flashover or breakdown shall occur**. A note warns that high-voltage equipment may need
additional checks that partial discharge and corona within the compound do not affect
its insulating properties.

This test is reused as the **acceptance threshold** for the
[[fault-not-considered-components|§5.1.5(c) optocoupler/relay segregation]] (electric
strength ≥ 1.5 U), in the [[routine-tests-encapsulation|routine electric strength test]]
(§9.2), and conditionally for [[encapsulated-cells-and-batteries|cells/batteries]]
(§8.2.4.2).
