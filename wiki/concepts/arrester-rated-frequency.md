# Rated frequency of an arrester

The rated frequency of an arrester is **the frequency of the power system on which
the arrester is designed to be used** ([[iec-99-4]] §2.10). The **standard rated
frequencies are 50 Hz and 60 Hz** (§4.2). Frequency need not appear on the
nameplate if it is one of these standard values; it must be marked only if other
than standard ([[arrester-identification]] §3.1).

The rated frequency frames the conditions under which the arrester's
power-frequency quantities are defined. Under normal [[arrester-service-conditions]]
(§4.4.1d) the supply frequency must be **not less than 48 Hz and not exceeding
62 Hz**; frequencies outside 48–62 Hz are an abnormal service condition (Annex A,
item 10). All tests with power-frequency voltage are accordingly made with an a.c.
voltage of approximately sinusoidal waveshape between **48 Hz and 62 Hz** (§6.1).
This is the same 50/60 Hz pair used by the circuit-breaker
[[rated-frequency]] of [[iec-56]], though defined here for the arrester device.
