# Cable diagram, table and list (IEC 1082-3 §6)

The [[connection-documents-iec1082|connection document]] that provides **all the
information required for laying the cables** among constructional units of
equipment or installations (IEC 1082-3 Section 6, §6.1). Where the
[[interconnection-diagram-iec1082|interconnection diagram]] says *which units are
joined*, the cable diagram is concerned with the **cables themselves and their
routes** — *information about the cable routes shall be included if necessary.*
Groups of cables may be shown in **[[single-line-representation]] with cable item
designations** (§6.1, fig. 18 — e.g. `-W121…-W130`, `-W125…-W133` drawn as single
lines). Successor of the cable-documentation parts of IEC 113.

**Worked examples (§6.2).**
- **fig. 18** — part of a cable diagram where **groups of cables are represented by
  single lines**, each labelled by its cable [[reference-designation-system|item
  designation]] (`-W…`).
- **fig. 19** — a cable diagram based on fig. 8 (one cable per route between the
  units `-A1`, `-A2`, `-A3`).
- **fig. 20** — a cable diagram showing **prefabricated cables with connectors**:
  units `-A1`, `-A2` have socket outlets while `-A3` has non-removable cables `-W1`,
  `-W2` each ending in a plug (`-X1`, `-X2`); `-A1`/`-A2` are interconnected by the
  screened 5-core cable `-W1` with plugs `-X1`/`-X2`, and `-A1`/`-A3` by a 9-core
  screened cable that is part of `-A3`.
- **fig. 21** — a **cable table** for the same equipment as fig. 19, listing per
  cable its item designation, **cable type** (e.g. `H05VV-...`), end points and
  remarks (e.g. auxiliary-voltage supply, `~230V`).

Cable representations for the various physical cable kinds (screened/twisted pairs,
power cable with sealing end, coaxial cable with plug, a cable containing an optical
fibre) are catalogued in Part 3 fig. 4 and shared with the other connection
documents. Conductors/cores are identified per **IEC 446 / IEC 757** and cables by
their `-W…` designations.
