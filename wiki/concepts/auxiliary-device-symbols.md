# Symbols for auxiliary devices (clause 10)

IEC 387 §10 and **Table 7** define markings for devices attached to a meter,
given as examples (items 10.1–10.5):

- **10.1 Meter supplied with pulse transmitter** — the marking gives the number
  of **pulses per kWh** or the number of **Wh per pulse** (example: 10 imp/kWh or
  100 Wh/imp). This is the output used for remote reading / telemetering and ties
  to the [[meter-constant]] of a [[static-energy-meter]].
- **10.2 Meter supplied with rotor clamp** — a transport/locking device for the
  rotor of an [[induction-meter]].
- **10.3 Auxiliary supply voltage for a static energy meter** — used when the
  auxiliary supply is **separated from the measuring voltage** (example: U_a =
  100 V, 50 Hz a.c.). Specific to [[static-energy-meter]] designs.
- **10.4 Nature and value of the auxiliary voltage of the relay of a multi-rate
  meter** — shown on the connection diagram (example: 60 V d.c.). Supports the
  rate-changing relay of a [[multi-rate-meter]].
- **10.5 Reversal preventing device (mechanical or electronic)** — a ratchet or
  electronic block that stops the register running backwards.

The clause closes by noting that **a proposal for the marking of signal ports is
given in Annex A** — see [[signal-port-symbols]].
