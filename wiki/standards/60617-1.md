# IEC 60617-1 — General information, general index, cross-correspondence

*Informations générales, index général, correspondance*

- **Standard:** IEC 60617-1 (formerly IEC 617-1)
- **Edition:** 1985
- **Source:** `Publication No.617-1.pdf` (scanned, 83 pp)

Unlike the other parts, **Part 1 contains no symbols of its own** — it is the
navigation layer for the whole 60617 family:

1. **General information** (Section 1) — how the series is organised, item
   designation, the rules for replacing asterisks, etc.
2. **General index** (Section 2) — an alphabetical index (FR + EN) mapping every
   term to its symbol number and its **IEC 750 item-designation letter** (e.g.
   R resistor, K relay, G generator, P measuring instrument…).
3. **Cross-correspondence** (Section 3) — a table mapping every **new IEC 617
   number** to the **old IEC Publication 117 number** (and its part). See
   [[cross-correspondence-iec117]].

This part is the index/cross-reference for [[index#standards|the whole family]];
it has no per-symbol nodes.
