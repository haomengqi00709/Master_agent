# Power-frequency voltage sparkover test (Type test, §60)

The **power-frequency voltage sparkover test** is the first [[type-test|type test]] of [[iec-99-1]]
§60. Dry and wet tests are made (per §55–§57, §59) on **three samples** of complete arresters of each
voltage rating tested; performance for other ratings of the same design within ±25 % (or 6 kV,
whichever is greater) of a tested rating may be inferred by proportioning the voltage level. The
voltage is switched on low enough to avoid a switching-surge sparkover, then **raised rapidly at a
uniform rate until sparkover of the series gap occurs**, and switched off as fast as possible (within
0.5 s) after sparkover. For arresters with grading resistors that may overheat, the time above rated
voltage is kept to 2–5 s.

The voltage is applied **not less than five times**, ~10 s apart. The pass criterion is that, for all
classes except the 10 000 A heavy-duty class, **the lowest power-frequency sparkover voltage of each
sample is not less than 1.5 times the [[rated-voltage-arrester|rated voltage]]** (see
[[power-frequency-sparkover-voltage]]); for the 10 000 A heavy-duty class the minimum is by agreement.
The **average** of the five values is the reference [[power-frequency-sparkover-voltage]] used to
compare the arrester before and after other type tests. The dry version of this test is also the
minimum [[routine-and-acceptance-tests|routine test]] performed on every arrester (§67), and it is
made before and after the [[current-impulse-withstand-test]] and [[operating-duty-test]] as a
condition check (the average must not change by more than 10 %). Tests 1–4 of §59 (this test plus the
[[voltage-impulse-sparkover-test|impulse sparkover tests]]) are made on the same samples.
