# Transformer-operated meter marking (clause 8)

IEC 387 §8 and **Table 5** set out how a meter fed via **instrument transformers**
(voltage and current transformers) must be marked, depending on its register type
([[primary-half-primary-secondary-register]]). The governing rule splits ratios by
whether the register accounts for them:

- **Ratios taken into account by the register** are marked on the
  **name-plate or dial** (for primary registers, the ratios of *all* transformers;
  for half-primary registers, the one ratio the register accounts for).
- **Ratios not taken into account** are marked on a **supplementary plate on the
  cover** of meters fitted with half-primary or secondary registers (for secondary
  registers, the ratios of all transformers; for half-primary, the ratio not
  accounted for), together with the **multiplying factor** by which the register
  reading is multiplied to obtain the primary-side energy.

The **instrument-transformer symbol** (examples 8.1–8.3) is marked on the
name-plate or dial of meters fitted with half-primary or secondary registers; it
means the meter is constructed to operate in assembly with such transformer(s)
whose ratio(s) are not taken into account by the register, so the primary energy
is the register reading times an appropriate factor.

**Table 5** gives worked examples: a **secondary-register** meter (50/5 A,
10 000/100 V on supplementary plate, multiplying factor = 1 000); two
**half-primary** cases (variable primary current 500/5 A, factor 100; variable
primary voltage 10 000/100 V, factor 100); and a **primary-register** meter
(10 000/100 V, 50/5 A marked directly). A NOTE states that, given the lack of
space on the name-plate, only one symbol is given — that for an instrument
transformer in **IEC 617-6** (the [[60617-6]] hub).
