# Thermal tests (encapsulation "m")

The §8.2.1 type tests that verify the thermal behaviour of encapsulated apparatus,
parts and [[ex-component-encapsulation|Ex components]] in [[type-of-protection-m|"m"]]
(IEC 79-18). They confirm the [[temperature-limitation|§5.4 limits]] hold and that the
[[encapsulation-compound|compound]] survives thermal stress.

**8.2.1.1 Maximum temperatures.** A sample is type-tested to ensure (i) the temperature
limits of §5.4 are not exceeded in **normal service**, and (ii) the maximum surface
temperature is not exceeded under the **§5.1.4 fault conditions**. Without external
load, the test follows 22.4.6.1 of IEC 79-0 but with supply voltages of **U_n + 10 %**
and **U_n − 10 %**; with external load, the current is adjusted to the **highest value
that does not operate the protective device**. The final temperature is reached when its
rate of rise is **≤ 2 K/h**. (Note: the protective device may be a fuse to IEC 127, test
current **1.7 I_n**.)

**8.2.1.2** is the [[thermal-cycling-test]] (its own page).

**8.2.1.3 Acceptance criteria.** After the thermal tests the sample is visually
inspected: **no visible damage of the compound that could impair the protection** may be
evident — no cracks, exposure of encapsulated parts, flaking, impermissible shrinkage,
swelling, decomposition or softening, and **no evidence of overheating**. These same
criteria are reused by the discharge test for [[encapsulated-cells-and-batteries|cells/batteries]]
(8.2.4) and referenced by [[adhesion|§5.7 adhesion]] (no visible separation).
