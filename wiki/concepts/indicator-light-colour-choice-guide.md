# Guide to choosing indicator-light colours (Appendix A)

**IEC 73, Appendix A** (with Table IV) — guidance, not strict rules, for choosing
[[indicator-light]] colours. Historically, indicator-light colours were chosen in
relation to the **switchgear immediately associated** with them — the colour
showed the position of the switch. This standard **changes that principle**: the
colour shall be based on the **most important information to be given to the
personnel** using the equipment ([[choice-of-colours]], §3.2). Usually that
information relates to some **mechanical effect, often a movement**, and the state
of intermediate apparatus (switchgear, motors, etc.) can in most cases be
**disregarded**.

Table IV gives worked examples; columns 2 and 3 still show the switching device's
function and position only for **ease of comparison** with former practice. Key
points the appendix draws out:
- In the first example (lines 1–2) nothing changes: RED and GREEN match the old
  recommendations of **Part 2 of IEC 54**. Other examples cover cases that RED and
  GREEN alone could not handle.
- Lines 9–10 and 12–13 show that, for the **same conditions**, **different colours**
  may be needed for indicator lights connected **in parallel but mounted in
  different locations** (the §3.2 parallel-lights note).
- In lines 3, 6 and 13, **BLUE** might also have been chosen instead of **WHITE**
  ([[indicator-light-blue]] vs [[indicator-light-white]] overlap).

Strict rules cannot be given; the appendix offers only guidance to promote the
same basic principles across the different fields of application of [[iec-73]].
