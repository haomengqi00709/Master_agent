# Compound (encapsulation "m")

The **compound** is the solid material that does the protecting in
[[type-of-protection-m|encapsulation "m"]]. Per IEC 79-18 §3.2 it is defined as
**thermosetting, thermoplastic, epoxy resin (cold curing) and elastomeric
materials, with or without fillers and/or additives, considered — after their
solidification — to be compounds.** The compound is applied to the device by the
[[encapsulation-process]] (embedding or potting), and the type of protection
depends entirely on its post-cure properties.

Because the protection rests on the compound, IEC 79-18 clause 4 imposes strict
documentation. The manufacturer's documents, verified by the national or other
appropriate authority under 22.2 of IEC 79-0 (§4.1), shall precisely describe each
compound and the encapsulation production method. The description (§4.2) shall
include: the **name and address of the material manufacturer**; the **exact and
complete reference of the material**, its colour, and the kind and percentage of
**fillers and other additives** (an ISO designation should be used where possible);
**surface treatments** such as varnishes; the [[temperature-range-of-the-compound]];
and the [[continuous-operating-temperature]]. The authority is **not required to
verify** the material's compliance with its own description (§4.3) — it relies on
the manufacturer's declaration, which is why the construction and test clauses are
so prescriptive.

Only the properties of the compound on which the type of protection depends need be
taken into account when choosing it for an application (§5.1.1). The compound is
proven by dedicated specimen tests: the [[compound-electric-strength-test]] (8.1.1)
and, for moist-environment use, the [[water-absorption-test]] (8.1.2), plus the
[[non-specific-tests-encapsulation|thermal-endurance, chemical and light tests]] of
annex C.
