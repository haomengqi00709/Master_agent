# 10-09-14 — Circulator four-port shown with reversible direction of circulation

![10-09-14](../assets/60617-10/p23.png)
<sub>This symbol appears on Publication No.617-10.pdf, printed p.25 (full page shown).</sub>

**Standard:** [[60617-10]] · **Section:** [[60617-10-09-multi-port-devices]]

## Description
Circulator four-port shown with reversible direction of circulation. Current entering the coil at the end marked with the dot causes the energy to flow in the circulator in the direction of the arrowhead marked with a dot.

## Names
- **EN:** Circulator four-port shown with reversible direction of circulation
- **FR:** Circulateur à quatre accès figuré avec direction de circulation réversible
