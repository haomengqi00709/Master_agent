# 10-21-04 — Pilot frequency

![10-21-04](../assets/60617-10/p43.png)
<sub>This symbol appears on Publication No.617-10.pdf, printed p.45 (full page shown).</sub>

**Standard:** [[60617-10]] · **Section:** [[60617-10-21-symbol-elements]]

## Description
Pilot frequency. Note. – For FDM transmission systems the order of the group to which the pilot refers, i.e. group, supergroup, mastergroup or supermastergroup, may be indicated by adding the respective number 1, 2, 3, or 4 of oblique strokes.

## Names
- **EN:** Pilot frequency
- **FR:** Fréquence d'une onde pilote

## Notes
- For FDM transmission systems the order of the group to which the pilot refers, i.e. group, supergroup, mastergroup or supermastergroup, may be indicated by adding the respective number 1, 2, 3, or 4 of oblique strokes.
