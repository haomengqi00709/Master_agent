# Component part

An indivisible item; the assembly of such items may form an apparatus ([[iec-79-19]] 2.2.5). The component part is the unit at which repair decisions are taken: a replacement or reclaimed component part may be used only if it leaves the apparatus in a [[serviceable-condition]] without prejudice to its explosion-protection or certification requirements. [[reclamation]] (2.2.6) is specifically the restoration of damaged component parts — by removing or adding material — to a serviceable condition in accordance with the standard to which the part was originally manufactured. Throughout the per-component clauses ([[enclosures]], [[windings]], [[terminations]], etc.) the standard specifies, part by part, when a damaged component part may be repaired or reclaimed and when it must instead be replaced with a new part obtained from the [[manufacturer]].
