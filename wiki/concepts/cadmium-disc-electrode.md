# Cadmium disc electrode

One of the two electrodes of the [[spark-test-apparatus]] consists of a
**rotating cadmium disc with two slots in it**, as illustrated in the standard's
Figure 2 ([[iec-79-3]] §4.2). **Cadmium as supplied for electroplating may be used
for casting the cadmium discs.** Cadmium is chosen as a "soft" electrode material
whose erosion characteristics under sparking give the apparatus its required,
reproducible ignition sensitivity; the two slots interrupt the sliding contact of
the [[tungsten-wire-electrode|tungsten wires]] to force repeated make- and
break-sparks each revolution.

The disc is mounted on its own shaft, geared to the wire-holder shaft so that it
**turns in the opposite direction at 19,2 rev/min** while the wire holder turns at
80 rev/min (see [[contact-mechanism]] for the 50:12 gear ratio and 31 mm shaft
spacing). The distance between the wire holder and the disc is **10 mm**. A **new
cadmium disc must be conditioned before use** to reach the required sensitivity —
the procedure (running in air, then capacitive-circuit ignition trials) is given
in Annex A.2 (see [[cadmium-disc-conditioning]]); replacing the disc is also a
last-resort action when sensitivity cannot be restored
([[spark-test-sensitivity-actions]]).
