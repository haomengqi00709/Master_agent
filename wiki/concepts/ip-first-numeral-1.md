# IP first numeral 1 — back of hand / objects ≥ 50 mm

First characteristic numeral **1** of the [[ip-code-structure|IP Code]]
([[iec-529]], 1st ed. Table I; 2nd ed. clause 5, Table I). It combines two
simultaneous requirements:

- **Access (persons):** protection against access to hazardous parts with the
  **back of the hand** — verified with the [[ip-access-probe|sphere probe]] of
  **50 mm diameter** (corresponds to [[ip-additional-letter|additional letter A]]).
  The 1976 wording: "a large surface of the body, such as a hand (but no
  protection against deliberate access)".
- **Solid objects (equipment):** protection against solid foreign objects of
  **50 mm diameter and greater** — the enclosure excludes objects exceeding 50 mm.

**Test ([[ip-first-numeral-tests]], 1st ed. §7.1):** a rigid sphere of
**50 mm ± 5% diameter** is applied against the openings with a force of
**50 N ± 10%**. The protection is satisfactory if the sphere does not pass
through any opening and adequate clearance is maintained to parts that are
normally live, or to moving parts, inside the enclosure.

Compliance implies compliance with [[ip-first-numeral-0]]. The next, finer level
is [[ip-first-numeral-2]] (finger / 12,5 mm).
