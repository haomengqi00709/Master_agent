# Insulation withstand test on the arrester housing (§5.1, §7.2)

The insulation withstand test demonstrates the ability of the **external insulation
of the arrester housing** to withstand voltage stresses under dry and wet
conditions ([[iec-99-4]] §5.1, §7.2). The housing — with internal parts removed or
rendered inoperative — must withstand voltages keyed to the arrester's
[[protective-characteristics|protection levels]]:
- the **lightning impulse protection level × 1,3** (the 1,3 factor covering
  atmospheric variation and currents above nominal);
- for 10 000/20 000 A arresters rated 200 kV and above, the **switching impulse
  protection level × 1,25**;
- a power-frequency voltage (wet for outdoor housings, dry for indoor).

The component tests (§7.2):
- **§7.2.6 Lightning impulse voltage test** — fifteen impulses per polarity at the
  lightning level × 1,3; pass if no *internal* disruptive discharge and ≤2 external
  flashovers per series. Not required if the dry arcing distance exceeds test
  voltage ÷ 500 kV/m.
- **§7.2.7 Switching impulse voltage test** — for 10 000/20 000 A ≥200 kV;
  fifteen impulses per polarity at switching level × 1,25 (wet outdoor / dry
  indoor).
- **§7.2.8 Power frequency voltage test** — 1 500/2 500/5 000 A and
  [[high-lightning-duty-arrester|HLD]] arresters: peak = lightning level × **0,88**
  for 1 min; 10 000/20 000 A below 200 kV: peak = switching level × **1,06** for
  1 min.

Ambient density/humidity corrections per IEC 60-1 (no humidity correction for wet
tests). This is type-test item 1 of the [[arrester-type-test]] programme and row 2
of [[arrester-classification]] Table 1.
