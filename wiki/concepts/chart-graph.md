# Chart (graph)

One of the three primary document types of [[iec-113-1]] (the U.S.A. term is
**graph**). Per §2.2, a chart may show the **interrelation between**: (a) different
operations; (b) operations and time; (c) operations and physical quantities; and
(d) the states of several items. This contrasts it with a [[diagram]], which shows
how the parts of a network or apparatus are interrelated/interconnected — a chart
deals with operations, time, quantities and states rather than with the physical
or electrical interconnection of parts.

In the purpose-based classification (§4), the explanatory members of this family
are the [[explanatory-chart-or-table]] group: the [[sequence-chart-or-table]]
(§4.1), which gives successive operations in a specified order, and the
[[time-sequence-chart-or-table]] (§4.2), which additionally accounts for the time
intervals between successive operations — corresponding directly to the
"operations" and "operations and time" cases of the §2.2 definition. Like a
diagram, a chart may be replaced or supplemented by a [[table-document]] (§2.2),
and IEC 113-1 increasingly emphasises tables for this role.
