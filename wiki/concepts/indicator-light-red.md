# Indicator light — RED (danger or alarm)

**IEC 73 §4.2, Table I.** A **RED** [[indicator-light]] means **danger or alarm**:
the *warning of a potential danger or a situation which requires immediate
action*. RED is one of the three colours (with YELLOW and GREEN) whose meaning is
**reserved** — these three *shall not be used with any other meaning* — so a red
light is never neutral or arbitrary; it always signals the highest-urgency
condition.

Typical applications listed in Table I, column 4:
- failure of pressure in a lubricating system;
- temperature outside specified (safe) limits;
- essential equipment stopped by action of a protective device;
- danger from accessible live or moving parts.

RED is frequently used in the *indication* mode ([[indicator-light-modes-of-use]],
§4.1) and is a natural candidate for a [[flashing-light]] when immediate action is
required (§3.4 b). It is the light counterpart of the reserved push-button colour
RED for STOP/OFF/emergency ([[push-button-stop-emergency]], §5.1). Contrast with
[[indicator-light-yellow]] (caution), [[indicator-light-green]] (safety), and the
free/neutral [[indicator-light-blue]] and [[indicator-light-white]]. The
general signal-lamp symbol is [[08-10-01]] (colour code **RD**).
