# Main contact

A main contact is a contact included in the main circuit of a circuit-breaker, intended to carry, in the closed position, the current of the main circuit ([[iec-56]] 3.103.7, IEV 441-15-07). Its function is to provide the low-resistance, continuous current-carrying path through each [[pole]] of the [[main-circuit]]. The main contact is paired with and protected by the [[arcing-contact]] (3.103.8): on opening, the main contacts part first while the current commutates to the arcing contacts, so that arcing and the resulting erosion occur on the sacrificial arcing contact rather than on the main contact; on closing the sequence reverses. This division of duty preserves the low contact resistance and current rating of the main contact over many operations. It is one of the defined contact types (3.103.5) of a [[circuit-breaker]].
