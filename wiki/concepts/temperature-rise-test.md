# Temperature-rise test

The temperature-rise test verifies that, carrying its [[rated-normal-current]] continuously, the circuit-breaker's contacts, terminals and parts do not exceed the temperature-rise limits of Table V (IEC 694 4.4.2), protecting against accelerated ageing of materials. Per [[iec-56]] 6.3 (with IEC 694 6.3), a breaker without series accessories is tested at its rated normal current; where series-connected accessories with a range of rated currents are fitted, additional tests are run at the rated current of each accessory (6.3.2). Temperatures and rises are measured per 6.3.3 and referred to ambient air temperature per 6.3.4, with auxiliary equipment covered in 6.3.5 and interpretation per 6.3.6 ([[iec-56]] 6.3). After a [[short-circuit-test]], contacts must still carry rated normal current within 10 K of these limits (6.102.8.2). Related: [[type-test]], [[short-circuit-test]].
