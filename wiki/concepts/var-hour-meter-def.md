# Var-hour meter (reactive energy)

An **instrument intended to measure reactive energy by integrating reactive
power with respect to time** (IEC 387 §3.4, after IEV 301-04-18). Alongside the
[[watt-hour-meter-def|watt-hour meter]] it is one of the two arrangements named
throughout Table 1: most measuring-element symbols read "watt-hour **or
var-hour** meter" — see [[measuring-element-symbols]]. The dedicated
three-element var-hour symbols 4.8–4.10 (each measuring element's voltage circuit
fed by the voltage between the phase conductors that do **not** contain its
current circuit) are reactive-meter specific.

The registered unit is the **var-hour (varh)** / kvarh (§5.6,
[[meter-unit-symbols]]). Reactive meters drive the leading/lagging power-factor
marking rules of §6: an induction reactive-energy meter adjusted for leading- or
lagging-PF operation has a defined rotor rotation direction and its register is
marked with the leading or lagging arrow symbol; meters measuring under both
conditions carry both, close to each register — see
[[measured-quantity-marking]]. Table 3 also gives the "working range of reactive
energy meter" example.

Contrast: active [[watt-hour-meter-def]], apparent
[[volt-ampere-hour-meter-def]]. Related TC 13 standard: IEC 145 (var-hour
meters). General graphical equivalent: integrating instrument [[08-01-03]].

See also: [[iec-387]] §3.4.
