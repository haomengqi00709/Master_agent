# IEC 60617-10 (formerly IEC 617-10) — Telecommunications: Transmission

*Télécommunications: Transmission*

- **Standard:** IEC 60617-10 (formerly IEC 617-10)
- **Edition:** 1st edition, 1983
- **Source:** `Publication No.617-10.pdf` (scanned, 51 pp)
- **Symbols:** 232 across 24 sections

Telecommunications transmission symbols: telecommunication circuits and lines, antennas and radio/space stations, microwave components (waveguides, one-/two-/multi-port devices, couplers, masers/lasers), signal generators, amplifiers, networks, frequency/band and spectrum-diagram symbols, and fibre-optic transmission. Heavily cross-references [[60617-2]] qualifying symbols and other parts.
