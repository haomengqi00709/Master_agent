# Permanence of colours

**IEC 73 §3.3.** In order to **retain the significance** assigned to the colour of
[[indicator-light]]s and [[push-button]]s, the selection of materials shall be
such that the colours **do not fade or change in a significant manner** during
their useful life and under the conditions of operation for which they are
designed.

This is the durability requirement that protects the whole coding scheme of
[[iec-73]]: the safety value of a colour code (e.g. RED = danger/stop) only holds
if a yellow lens does not bleach toward white, or a green button does not dull to
a neutral grey, over the equipment's service life. It complements
[[use-of-colours]] (which fixes a distinguishable palette) and
[[choice-of-colours]] (which assigns meaning) by ensuring the chosen,
distinguishable colour stays distinguishable and recognisable in service. The
exact colorimetric targets that the materials must hold to are those of **ISO
3864** (referenced at §3.1).
