# IEC 79-3 — Spark-test apparatus for intrinsically-safe circuits

*Electrical apparatus for explosive gas atmospheres, Part 3: Spark-test
apparatus for intrinsically-safe circuits.* 3rd edition, 1990-12 (replaces the
2nd edition of 1972). Prepared by **IEC SC 31G** (Intrinsically-safe apparatus)
of **TC 31** (Electrical apparatus for explosive atmospheres).

A sibling of [[iec-79-19]] in the **IEC 79** explosive-atmospheres family, but a
narrower **test-apparatus specification**: it defines the physical spark-test
machine — an electromechanical contact mechanism in a gas-filled explosion
chamber — used to demonstrate that a circuit of [[type-of-protection-i]]
(intrinsic safety) cannot ignite an explosive gas atmosphere. The actual test
*procedure*, calibration regime and gas mixtures live in **IEC 79-11 clause 9**;
this part specifies the **apparatus** that procedure relies on. Decomposed here
per the LLM-Wiki method — each definition, each constructional element and each
annex procedure gets its own concept page.

## Scope (clause 1)
Requirements for the spark-test apparatus used to test electrical apparatus and
systems with [[type-of-protection-i|type of protection "i"]] for
**intrinsically-safe circuits** per IEC 79-11, where the **rated current does not
exceed 2 A** (see [[spark-test-apparatus-limitations]]). IEC 79-11 (clause 9)
governs the use of the apparatus for the spark-ignition test, its calibration and
the [[explosive-test-mixture|explosive test mixtures]] per gas group. There are
no IEC 79-0 requirements applicable to this part.

## Introduction & principle
The apparatus shows compliance with IEC 79-11 by establishing that, with circuit
parameters adjusted to a prescribed **safety factor**, no
[[safety-factor-and-ignition-criterion|ignition]] of the explosive test mixture
occurs within a defined number of operations of the contact mechanism — see
[[spark-ignition-test-principle]].
