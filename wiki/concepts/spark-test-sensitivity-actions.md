# Recommended actions when sensitivity is not as specified (Annex A.3)

A guide list of actions when the [[spark-test-apparatus]]'s sensitivity is found
to be out of specification ([[iec-79-3]] Annex A, clause A.3, informative):

- **a)** Check the parameters of the **calibration circuit** (§4.3, see
  [[spark-test-calibration]]) and the apparatus parameters in the last paragraph
  of §4.2 (the [[terminal-electrical-characteristics|terminal C/R/L limits]]).
- **b)** Check the **composition of the [[explosive-test-mixture|explosive test
  mixture]]** (§4.1).
- **c)** Remove the wire holder, inspect the
  [[tungsten-wire-electrode|tungsten wires]] and, **if the ends are not split,
  straighten and clean them** to remove deposits by manually rubbing the surface
  (including the ends) with **abrasive cloth**.
- **d)** Fit **new tungsten wires** (per [[tungsten-wire-preparation]]).
- **e)** Repeat step b) of [[cadmium-disc-conditioning|Annex A.2]] (the 20 000-rev
  run-in air).
- **f)** **Replace the [[cadmium-disc-electrode|cadmium disc]]** (last resort).

A note specifies the **abrasive grain particle-size distribution** for step c),
using nominal R40-series sieve apertures from **ISO 2194**: all grains pass
106 µm; not more than 24 % retained on 75 µm; at least 40 % retained on 53 µm; not
more than 10 % pass 45 µm.
