# Indicator light

Defined in **IEC 73 §2.1** as a *"device with (a) lamp(s), provided for the
purpose of giving information."* It is the central object of [[iec-73]] alongside
the [[push-button]]: a luminous signalling device whose **colour carries a coded
meaning** about the state of equipment or an instruction to the operator. The
standard treats the indicator light, the illuminated sign and the lighted
[[illuminated-push-button]] together for the purposes of the colour code.

For indicator lights the colours are seen as **transmitted light** through a lens
(clear or diffused): RED, YELLOW, GREEN, BLUE and WHITE (§3.1.1). A specific
significance is assigned to each, so the colours must be easily distinguishable
([[use-of-colours]]). The colour is chosen from the **information to be given to
the operator**, not from the position of any associated switchgear
([[choice-of-colours]], and the [[indicator-light-colour-choice-guide]] of
Appendix A). The two principal [[indicator-light-modes-of-use]] are *indication*
and *confirmation*. The meanings of RED/YELLOW/GREEN are reserved (Table I, §4.2);
see [[indicator-light-red]], [[indicator-light-yellow]], [[indicator-light-green]],
[[indicator-light-blue]], [[indicator-light-white]].

Where colour alone is insufficient, the light should be supplemented by symbols
(IEC 417) or written information (§1.3), and may be reinforced by an audible
signal or a [[flashing-light]]. The corresponding 60617 graphic is the
general signal lamp [[08-10-01]], which carries the same RD/YE/GN/BU/WH colour
abbreviations used in this standard.
