# Rated insulation level

The rated insulation level specifies the dielectric withstand capability of the circuit-breaker, expressed by rated power-frequency and impulse withstand voltages to earth, between poles, and across the open break. Sub-clause 4.2 of IEC Publication 694 applies, supplemented in [[iec-56]] 4.2. For [[rated-voltage]] up to and including 72.5 kV, Table I applies; Series II (60 Hz, USA/Canada) is added, e.g. 72.5 kV gives 350 kV rated lightning impulse withstand (peak) and 160 kV power-frequency withstand to earth (4.2.1). For 100-245 kV, Table III applies (4.2.2); for 300 kV and above, switching-impulse withstand voltages from Table IV apply (4.2.3), with an increased open-breaker value (column 6) for special circuit-breakers used in synchronizing with substantial switching surges. Coordinated with [[rated-voltage]].
