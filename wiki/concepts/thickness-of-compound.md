# Thickness of the layer of compound (encapsulation "m")

The depth of [[encapsulation-compound|compound]] over live parts is one of the
decisive dimensions of [[type-of-protection-m|encapsulation "m"]] (IEC 79-18 §5.2).

- **§5.2.1 — free surface:** between the **free surface** of the compound and the
  components/conductors, the thickness shall be **at least 3 mm**. For small parts
  with no free surface exceeding **2 cm²**, **1 mm** is allowed; if such a part then
  cannot pass the IEC 79-0 impact test or the §8.2.3
  [[apparatus-electric-strength-test|electric strength test]], the authority issues a
  certificate marked **"X"** requiring additional means of protection (e.g. extra
  mechanical protection).
- **§5.2.2 — metal protective housing (potting):** between a metal housing and any
  component/conductor, **at least 1 mm**. For rotating machines with windings in
  slots, the slot insulation shall be **≥ 0.2 mm**, elongated **≥ 5 mm** beyond the
  slot end, and protected by the §5.2.1 thickness of compound.
- **§5.2.3 — insulating protective housing (potting):** **no** minimum compound layer
  is required behind an insulating housing **≥ 1 mm** thick; if the housing is thinner,
  housing + compound together must meet §5.2.1. The insulating housing must pass the
  [[non-specific-tests-encapsulation|annex C tests]]; where it directly supports bare
  live parts, its tracking resistance and creepage distances must meet **4.4 of
  IEC 79-7**.

Thickness interacts with [[free-space-and-voids|void rules]] (3 mm between
encapsulated components, 1 mm where a void < 1 cm³) and is distinct from
[[distances-through-compound|distances through the compound]] (§5.3).
