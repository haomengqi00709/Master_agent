# 13-05-22 — Mm-output

![13-05-22](../assets/60617-13/p12.png)
<sub>This symbol appears on Publication No.617-13.pdf, printed p.21 (full page shown).</sub>

**Standard:** [[60617-13]] · **Section:** [[60617-13-05-qualifying-symbols-indicating-the]]

## Description
If an Mm-input/output stands at its internal 0-state, affected inputs have no effect and affected output label-sets containing its identifying number are ignored (disabling multi-function inputs/outputs).

## Names
- **EN:** Mm-output
- **FR:** Sortie Mm

## Notes
- Note 1 with symbols 13-05-14 and 13-05-15 applies.
