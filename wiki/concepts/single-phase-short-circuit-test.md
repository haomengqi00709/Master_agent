# Single-phase short-circuit test

Single-phase short-circuit tests are additional to the basic short-circuit duties of [[iec-56]] 6.106 and apply only to three-pole breakers for earthed-neutral systems having the three poles in one enclosure, or three separately-enclosed poles coupled mechanically with a common opening release ([[iec-56]] 6.108.1). The test demonstrates that operation is not adversely affected by the unbalanced electromagnetic forces produced when only one pole carries current. It must be shown that the breaker can break its rated short-circuit breaking current — d.c. component not exceeding 20% — through one pole only, with the [[transient-recovery-voltage]] from Tables IIA-IIX divided by the first-pole-to-clear factor and a power-frequency recovery voltage of U/sqrt(3) ([[iec-56]] 6.108.2). The [[test-duty]] is a single breaking operation, applied through an outer pole, or the pole giving maximum stress on the inter-pole coupling ([[iec-56]] 6.108.3).
