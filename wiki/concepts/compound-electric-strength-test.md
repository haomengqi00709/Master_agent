# Electric strength test for the compound (encapsulation "m")

A type test on the [[encapsulation-compound|compound]] material itself, run on
standard specimens rather than the finished apparatus (IEC 79-18 §8.1.1). It proves
that the cured compound has the dielectric integrity on which
[[type-of-protection-m|encapsulation "m"]] relies.

A **disc of compound, 50 mm ± 2 mm in diameter and 3 mm ± 0.2 mm thick**, is tested
with a voltage of **4 kV** at a frequency between **48 Hz and 62 Hz**, applied between
**electrodes of 30 mm ± 2 mm diameter** placed at the centre of the disc. The voltage
shall be applied for **at least 5 min** at the **highest temperature of the
[[temperature-range-of-the-compound|temperature range]] defined in §3.3**. **No
flashover or breakdown shall occur.** Testing at the top of the temperature range is
the worst case, since dielectric strength typically falls as temperature rises.

This is distinct from the [[apparatus-electric-strength-test|electric strength test on
the assembled apparatus]] (§8.2.3), which checks insulation between circuits and to
earth in the actual product. Within the annex B sequences the compound discs are
prepared by the [[non-specific-tests-encapsulation|thermal-endurance conditioning of
annex C]] before this test.
