# Furnace (hot-air furnace for the test flask)

The heater that brings the [[erlenmeyer-test-flask|200 ml test flask]] to the
desired uniform temperature ([[iec-79-4]] §4.2, detailed in **Appendix A**). The
flask **shall be heated in an adequately uniform manner by a hot-air furnace**.

**Adequacy criterion (§4.2).** A furnace and its temperature-measurement
position(s) are deemed satisfactory **if the measured ignition temperatures of
the Table I reference products agree with the specified values within the
tolerances of §7**, when this standard's procedure is followed. The Table I
calibration substances (purity ≥ 99.9 %) are **n-heptane 220 °C, ethylene
435 °C, benzene 560 °C** — a built-in three-point check spanning the range.

**Appendix A — two qualifying designs:**

- **Furnace A1 (Figs 1–6).** A refractory cylinder **127 mm (5 in) internal
  diameter × 127 mm long**, circumferentially wound with a uniformly spaced
  **1200 W** main heater, in refractory insulation and a retaining shell, with a
  compressed asbestos-cement cover ring and flask guide ring, plus a **300 W neck
  heater** and a **300 W base heater**. **Three thermocouples** are positioned
  25 mm (1 in) and 50 mm (2 in) below the bottom of the neck heater and under the
  flask base near its centre. Each thermocouple can be held to **within ±1 °C** of
  the desired temperature using independently variable controls for the three
  heaters.
- **Furnace A2 (Figs 7–9).** A resistance-heated furnace of **~1300 W**, max
  heating current **6 A**. Heating wire Ø1.2 mm, length 35.8 m of Cr/Al 30/5
  alloy wound round a ceramic cylinder (1.2 mm turn spacing), fixed with
  high-temperature mastic and enclosed in a 20 mm aluminium-oxide-powder
  insulating layer; a stainless-steel cylinder is inserted in the ceramic body and
  a stainless-steel lid holds the flask. Operated a.c. or d.c.; if automatic
  control is used the heating/cooling periods should be of equal length.
  Measurement thermocouples sit on the flask wall 25 ± 2 mm from its base and at
  the centre of the under-surface of the base.

Three independently controlled heating zones (main/neck/base, A1) provide the
uniform field the [[ignition-test-procedure|procedure]] relies on; non-uniform
heating is a [[factors-affecting-ignition-temperature|known error source]].
