# IP second numeral 8 — continuous submersion

The highest second characteristic numeral, **8**, of the
[[ip-code-structure|IP Code]] ([[iec-529]], 1st ed. Table II; 2nd ed. clause 6).
Protection against **continuous submersion**: *"the equipment is suitable for
continuous submersion in water under conditions which shall be specified by the
manufacturer."* A table note records that this normally means the equipment is
hermetically sealed, though for some equipment water may enter provided it
produces no harmful effect.

**Test ([[ip-second-numeral-tests]], 1st ed. §8.8):** the test conditions are
**subject to agreement between manufacturer and user**, but **shall not be less
severe** than those for [[ip-second-numeral-7]] (i.e. at least the §8.7 immersion
depth/time). Because submersion is application-specific, depth and duration are
fixed by the manufacturer's specification rather than a single universal figure.
Inspection per §8.9.

`IPX8` is the top water rating; combined with a dust-tight first numeral it gives
the most-sealed designation `IP68`. Subsumes all lower water degrees
([[ip-second-numeral-0]]–[[ip-second-numeral-7]]).
