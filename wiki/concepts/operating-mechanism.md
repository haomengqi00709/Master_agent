# Operating mechanism

The assembly that stores and delivers the energy needed to close and open the [[circuit-breaker]] and that carries the releases and interlocks acting on the contacts. In [[iec-56]] it is treated under "operating devices," which fall within the standard's scope together with their auxiliary equipment (scope, [[iec-56]] clause 1); the complete breaker including its operating devices must be capable of completing the rated operating sequence (3.102). According to its energy source the mechanism realises [[stored-energy-operation]], [[independent-manual-operation]] or [[dependent-manual-operation]] (the last excluded from scope). It houses the trip-free linkage and is acted upon by the [[over-current-release]], [[making-current-release]], [[instantaneous-release]] and the [[interlocking-device]]; recharging time of the operating mechanism is a tested characteristic.
