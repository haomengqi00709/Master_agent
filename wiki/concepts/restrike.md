# Restrike

A restrike is a resumption of current between the contacts of a switching device during a breaking operation occurring a quarter of a cycle of power frequency or more after the most recent current zero ([[iec-56]] 3.105.47, IEV 441-17-46). The quarter-cycle timing is the defining threshold that distinguishes it from a [[reignition]], which occurs less than a quarter-cycle after current zero ([[iec-56]] 3.105.46). A restrike therefore represents a dielectric failure of the gap relatively late, after the [[recovery-voltage]] (and its transient component, the TRV) has built up to a substantial value, making restrikes particularly significant in capacitive switching where they can generate escalating overvoltages. A breaker designed to avoid them is termed restrike-free ([[iec-56]] 3.102.12). Restrike behaviour is closely tied to the rate of rise of the [[recovery-voltage]] versus the dielectric recovery of the gap.
