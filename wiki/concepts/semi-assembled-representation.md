# Semi-assembled representation

The second method of **arrangement of symbols** in [[iec-113-1]] §8.2. Here **the
symbols for the different parts of an item of apparatus or of an installation are
separated and arranged in such a way that the symbols for the mechanical linkages
between the parts which work together may be drawn easily** (§8.2). The parts are no
longer drawn in close proximity, but they are kept positioned so that the
mechanical-coupling symbols joining co-operating parts remain simple to draw.

It sits between the other two §8 arrangements: more separated than
[[assembled-representation]] (§8.1), where parts are kept in close proximity, but
organised around *mechanical linkages* rather than circuit-following like
[[detached-representation]] (§8.3). As with the other arrangement methods it is
independent of the §7 conductor-count methods ([[single-line-representation]],
[[multi-line-representation]]) and of [[topographical-representation]] (§9), and
several methods may be combined on one diagram (Note to §7–9; see
[[mixed-diagram]]). The 1971 revision renamed the French term from *représentation
semi-développée* to *représentation rangée* (§Preface).
