# Explanatory chart or table

The second purpose-class in [[iec-113-1]] (§4), and the chart/table counterpart of
the [[explanatory-diagram]]. **Explanatory charts or tables are intended to
facilitate the study of diagrams and to give additional information** (§4). They
are a [[chart-graph]] (or its equivalent [[table-document]]) used in an explanatory
role — supporting the reading of [[circuit-diagram]]s and [[block-diagram]]s rather
than showing connections or location.

IEC 113-1 gives **two examples** (§4):

- [[sequence-chart-or-table]] (§4.1) — gives the successive operations in a
  specified order.
- [[time-sequence-chart-or-table]] (§4.2) — does the same but additionally takes
  account of the time intervals between successive operations.

These map onto the [[chart-graph]] definition (§2.2), whose cases include the
interrelation between *different operations* and between *operations and time* —
exactly the distinction between the plain sequence chart and the time-sequence
chart. As with all the chart classes, either example may be rendered as a table
(§2.2), and IEC 113-1's 1971 revision emphasises that tabular forms increasingly
serve this purpose (§Preface).
