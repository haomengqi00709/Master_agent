# High Lightning Duty (HLD) arrester (Annex C)

A High Lightning Duty arrester is a **20 000 A arrester especially applicable to
high-lightning-intensity areas**, for highest system voltage in the range
**1 kV to 52 kV** ([[iec-99-4]] Annex C, normative). It is a special category beyond
the five standard [[nominal-discharge-current]] classes, with its own test
requirements in **Table C.1** (rated voltage 3 ≤ Uᵣ ≤ 60 kV).

Its distinguishing feature is the [[operating-duty-test|operating duty test]]: per
§5.9 and §7.5.4, each sample is subjected to **three 30/80 current impulses with a
peak value of 40 kA**, at intervals of 50–60 s, rather than the standard
[[high-current-impulse|4/10 high-current]] regime. The 30/80 impulse tolerances:
peak 90–110 %, virtual front time 25–35 µs, time to half value 70–90 µs, opposite
polarity <20 %, small oscillations <5 % (Annex C). The complete sequence is figure
C.1; the special [[thermal-stability]] test (§8.2.2) follows figure C.2. Other
requirements track the 20 000 A class — [[insulation-withstand-test]] (§5.1, §7.2.6,
§7.2.8), [[residual-voltage-test]] (§7.3.1, §7.3.2; switching impulse *not
required*), [[long-duration-current-impulse-withstand-test]] (§7.4),
[[power-frequency-withstand-voltage-vs-time]] (§5.10), [[pressure-relief]] (§5.11),
[[arrester-disconnector]] (§5.12) and the polluted-housing test (Annex F). The HLD
type may be marked on the nameplate ([[arrester-identification]]).
