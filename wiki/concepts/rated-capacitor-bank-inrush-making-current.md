# Rated capacitor bank inrush making current

Clause 4.111 of [[iec-56]] defines the rated capacitor bank inrush making current as the peak value of current the circuit-breaker shall be capable of making at its rated voltage, with a frequency of the inrush current appropriate to the service conditions (see Appendix BB). Unlike the breaking quantities, this is a making duty: when a capacitor bank is energised — especially in a back-to-back arrangement — the parallel banks discharge into the bank being switched, producing a brief high-amplitude, high-frequency current surge that stresses the closing contacts and pre-arc. Specifying it is mandatory for any breaker assigned a [[rated-back-to-back-capacitor-bank-breaking-current]]. Values are selected from the R 10 series. Notes state the service inrush frequency is normally 2-5 kHz, and that a breaker tested at one frequency is deemed suitable for any lower inrush frequency. It is the making counterpart to the capacitive breaking duties; see [[making-and-breaking-capacity]] and [[capacitive-current-switching-test]] ([[iec-56]] 4.111).
