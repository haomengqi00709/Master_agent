# Encapsulated fuses (encapsulation "m")

Supplementary requirements for fuses encapsulated to [[type-of-protection-m|"m"]]
(IEC 79-18 §6.2), supplementing clause 5. The **fusing element shall be of the
enclosed type before encapsulation** — e.g. in glass or ceramic — so that the arc and
hot particles of rupture are contained before the [[encapsulation-compound|compound]]
is applied.

For voltages above **60 V**, fuses shall have a **breaking capacity in accordance with
IEC 127** (miniature fuses) **or IEC 269** (low-voltage fuses). A deliberate exception
to the thermal rules applies: the **maximum temperature caused by the rupturing of the
fuse may exceed the [[continuous-operating-temperature|continuous operating temperature
of the compound]]**, on condition that the type of protection "m" is **not affected**
(e.g. no cracks) — however, the **surface temperature of the apparatus or its parts
shall not exceed the marked temperature or temperature class**. This is the single
sanctioned departure from [[temperature-limitation|§5.4]], justified because fuse
rupture is momentary and the contained element prevents direct ignition.

A fuse complying with IEC 127 is also cited as a typical protective device in the
[[thermal-tests|maximum-temperature test]] (8.2.1.1 note), where the test current may
be **1.7 I_n**.
