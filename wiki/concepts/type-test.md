# Type test

A type test is one of the type tests of clause 6 ([[iec-56]] 6 / IEC 694 clause 6), made on a representative circuit-breaker to verify that the design meets its rated characteristics — as distinct from a routine test made on every unit. For IEC 56, the type tests comprise [[dielectric-test]]s (6.1), [[radio-interference-voltage-test]]s (6.2), [[temperature-rise-test]]s (6.3), main-circuit resistance (6.4), short-time/peak withstand current tests (6.5), [[mechanical-and-environmental-test]]s (6.101), and short-circuit making and breaking tests including terminal-fault, short-line-fault and out-of-phase tests (6.102-6.110), plus capacitive and small-inductive switching. In principle each is performed on a new, clean circuit-breaker; tests may be done at different times and locations, and the manufacturer's responsibility is limited to the specified values ([[iec-56]] 6 intro). See [[short-circuit-test]] and [[test-duty]].

**Amended (1992, Amd. 1):** [[iec-56-amendment-1]] corrects the clause-6 intro cross-reference (second dash) from "6.103 to 6.110" to "6.102 to 6.110", so the making/breaking duty 6.102 is included in the type-test list.
