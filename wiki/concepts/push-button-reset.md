# Push-button — RESET (BLUE / neutral)

**IEC 73 §5.4.** RESET push-buttons — used, for example, with **protective relays**
— shall be **BLUE, BLACK, WHITE or GREY**. The **exception** is a RESET
push-button which **also acts as a STOP or OFF** push-button: such a combined
button **shall be RED**, because the stop/off function takes priority under the
reservation rule of [[push-button-stop-emergency]] (§5.1). So a pure reset is a
non-reserved colour; a reset-plus-stop is forced to RED.

This is why Table II ([[push-button-colour-table]]) lists **"reset combined with
STOP"** among the RED applications rather than the blue/neutral ones. The
permitted plain-reset colours overlap with the assignable BLUE
([[indicator-light-blue]] is the light analogue of "specific meaning") and with
the neutral BLACK/WHITE/GREY group used for
[[push-button-start-stop-combined]] (§5.3). RESET is one of the four push-button
function clauses (§5.1 STOP/emergency, §5.2 START/ON, §5.3 combined/inching, §5.4
RESET) whose recommended colours are consolidated in Table II (§5.5).
