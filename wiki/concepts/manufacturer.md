# Manufacturer

Maker of the apparatus (who may also be the supplier, importer or agent), in whose name the certification of the apparatus was usually originally registered. Much of the standard turns on “reference to the manufacturer” for winding data, spare parts and any rating change.

Defined in [[iec-79-19]] 2.2.8.
