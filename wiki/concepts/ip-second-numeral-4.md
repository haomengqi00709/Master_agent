# IP second numeral 4 — splashing water

Second characteristic numeral **4** of the [[ip-code-structure|IP Code]]
([[iec-529]], 1st ed. Table II; 2nd ed. clause 6). Protection against **splashing
water from any direction**: *"water splashed against the enclosure from any
direction shall have no harmful effect."*

**Test ([[ip-second-numeral-tests]], 1st ed. §8.4):** the **same apparatus** as
numeral 3 (oscillating tube Figure 4, or hand-held spray Figure 5; the choice
rule is the same as §8.3), but the spray covers **all directions**. With the
oscillating tube, holes are drilled over the **whole 180° of the semicircle** and
the support is perforated to avoid acting as a baffle, so the enclosure is sprayed
from every direction by oscillating the tube to the limit of travel each way;
rate of oscillation, water pressure and duration are as in §8.3. With the
hand-held device the counterbalanced shield is **removed** and the enclosure
sprayed from all practicable directions at the §8.3 delivery rate and time per
unit area. Inspection per §8.9.

Splashing (4) is the boundary between the gentler drip/spray levels and the
**jet** levels: numerals 1–4 may sometimes be accepted on visual inspection in
obvious cases, whereas 5–8 always require the physical test. Subsumes
[[ip-second-numeral-3]]; next is [[ip-second-numeral-5]] (water jets).
