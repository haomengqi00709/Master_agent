# Unit of an arrester

A unit of an arrester is **a completely housed part of an arrester which may be
connected in series and/or in parallel with other units to construct an arrester
of higher voltage and/or current rating** ([[iec-99-4]] §2.6). A unit is *not
necessarily* an [[arrester-section]]: the unit is the physical, separately-housed
building block sold and stacked to reach the required rating, whereas a section is
a test concept representing the arrester's behaviour for a particular test.

Units appear throughout the standard's measurement rules. The [[reference-voltage]]
of a multi-unit arrester is the **sum** of the reference voltages of the
individual units (§2.35); likewise the [[residual-voltage]] of a complete arrester
is taken as the sum of the residual voltages of the individual units or resistor
elements when not directly measured (§8.1b, §8.2.1b). [[arrester-identification]]
requires multi-unit arresters to mark the **assembling position** of each unit on
the nameplate (§3.1). In the [[accelerated-ageing-procedure]], if the voltage
distribution of each unit in a multi-unit arrester is known by measurement or
calculation, the corrected-voltage formula is applied to the **maximum-stressed
unit** (§7.5.2.1).
