# Grading ring of an arrester

A grading ring is **a metal part, usually circular in shape, mounted to modify
electrostatically the voltage distribution along the arrester** ([[iec-99-4]]
§2.4). Fitted near the line end of tall arresters, it reshapes the electric field
so that the power-frequency voltage grades more evenly down the column of
[[metal-oxide-resistor|metal-oxide resistors]], reducing over-stress on the discs
closest to the energized terminal. It performs externally and electrostatically
the same field-grading job that the [[internal-grading-system]] performs internally
with grading capacitors.

The grading ring is treated as part of the arrester for testing: §6.3 requires
test samples to be "completely assembled (e.g. with grading rings if applicable)
and arranged as nearly as possible to simulate the conditions in service." It is a
form of auxiliary/grading equipment, for which §5.13 currently sets no requirement.
Its electrostatic grading complements the unbalance allowance built into the
[[accelerated-ageing-procedure]] corrected-voltage formula (§7.5.2.1).
