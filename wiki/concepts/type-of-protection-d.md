# Type of protection "d" — flameproof enclosure

Protection in which parts that can ignite an explosive gas atmosphere are placed in an **enclosure that withstands the pressure of an internal explosion** and prevents transmission of the explosion to the surrounding atmosphere. The flame paths/gaps are critical and must not be reclaimed beyond spec.

Defined in [[iec-79-19]] 2.2.14. Repair, overhaul, reclamation and modification requirements are in **[[iec-79-19]] clause 3**.
