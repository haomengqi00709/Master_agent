# Repairer

Repairer of the apparatus, who may be the manufacturer, the user or a third party (repair agency). Carries statutory responsibilities (2.3.4) and must hold the training, documentation and facilities required by clause 2.6.

Defined in [[iec-79-19]] 2.2.10.
