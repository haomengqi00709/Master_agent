# Push-button — STOP / OFF / emergency (RED)

**IEC 73 §5.1.** The colour **RED shall be used for STOP, OFF or emergency buttons
only**, and conversely STOP, OFF and emergency buttons **shall be RED**. Thus RED
is **reserved** for the function STOP or to cause the **opening of a main
circuit**: when a red [[push-button]] is actuated, this shall cause the equipment
to stop, to be de-energized, to initiate a fire alarm, etc. This is the strongest
rule in [[iec-73]] — the reservation runs both ways, so no non-stop function may
be coloured red and no stop/emergency function may be coloured anything else.

In Table II ([[push-button-colour-table]]) RED carries two meaning rows:
**"action in case of emergency"** (typical applications: emergency stop,
fire-fighting) and **"STOP or OFF"** (general stop; stop one or more motors; stop
a part of a machine; open a switching device; reset combined with STOP — see
[[push-button-reset]] §5.4 exception). Because of this reservation, RED is
explicitly **excluded** from the alternating and inching buttons of
[[push-button-start-stop-combined]] (§5.3). A critical safety constraint:
**emergency-stop buttons shall never be illuminated** ([[illuminated-push-button]]
§6.2 note), since a failed lamp could mislead. The light counterpart is
[[indicator-light-red]] (danger/alarm). Contrast the START/ON button
[[push-button-start-on]] (§5.2, GREEN preferred).
