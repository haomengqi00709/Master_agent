# Radio interference voltage (RIV) test

The radio-interference-voltage (RIV) test checks that corona and surface discharges from the high-voltage parts do not generate excessive radio-frequency noise. Per [[iec-56]] 6.2 (and IEC 694 6.2), RIV tests are performed only by agreement between manufacturer and user. The standard adds that tests may be carried out on one pole of the circuit-breaker, in both the closed and the open positions, the RIV being measured at a specified test voltage against the limit set in the base standard ([[iec-56]] 6.2). It is one of the dielectric-family [[type-test]]s and complements the [[dielectric-test]] and the [[partial-discharge-test]] in characterising the insulation and external corona behaviour of the apparatus.
