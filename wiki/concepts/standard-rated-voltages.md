# Standard rated voltages (§4.1)

[[iec-99-4]] §4.1 specifies the standard values of [[arrester-rated-voltage]]
(in kV r.m.s.) as **equal voltage steps within specified ranges** (Table 2):

| Range of rated voltage (kV r.m.s.) | Step (kV r.m.s.) |
|------------------------------------|------------------|
| < 3 | under consideration |
| 3 – 30 | 1 |
| 30 – 54 | 3 |
| 54 – 96 | 6 |
| 96 – 288 | 12 |
| 288 – 396 | 18 |
| 396 – 756 | 24 |

Other rated-voltage values may be accepted provided they are **multiples of 6**
(note to Table 2). The step size widens with voltage so that the relative
granularity stays roughly constant across the range. These standard rated voltages
dovetail with [[arrester-classification]] (Table 1), which assigns each
[[nominal-discharge-current]] class a rated-voltage band (e.g. 3 ≤ Uᵣ ≤ 360 kV for
10 000 A, 360 < Uᵣ ≤ 756 kV for 20 000 A). They are distinct from the
[[continuous-operating-voltage]] U_c, which is the continuous permissible voltage
rather than the temporary-overvoltage rating Uᵣ that this table standardises.
