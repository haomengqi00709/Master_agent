# Conditioning of a new cadmium disc (Annex A.2)

It is generally necessary to **condition a new
[[cadmium-disc-electrode|cadmium disc]]** before use to achieve the required
sensitivity of the [[spark-test-apparatus]] ([[iec-79-3]] Annex A, clause A.2,
informative). The recommended procedure:

- **a)** Fit the new disc into the apparatus.
- **b)** Connect the terminals to a **95 mH / 24 V d.c. / 100 mA** circuit and run
  the apparatus with the contact mechanism **in air for 20 000 revolutions** of
  the wire holder.
- **c)** Fit **new tungsten wires** (prepared per [[tungsten-wire-preparation]])
  and connect the terminals to a **2 µF non-electrolytic capacitor charged through
  a 2 kΩ resistor**.
- **d)** Using the **Group IIA (or Group I)** [[explosive-test-mixture|test
  mixture]], apply **70 V d.c. (95 V for Group I)** to the capacitive circuit and
  operate for **400 revolutions** or until ignition. If ignition occurs, **reduce
  the voltage by 5 %** and repeat until no ignition occurs in 400 revolutions.
- **e)** Repeat d) but starting at **60 V (80 V for Group I)**.
- **f)** Repeat e) but starting at **50 V (70 V for Group I)**.
- **g)** The **lowest voltage at which ignition occurs should not be greater than
  40 V d.c. (55 V for Group I)** — this is the sensitivity acceptance criterion.
- **h)** If no ignition occurs at the step-e, step-f or step-g voltages, go back to
  step d).

The procedure establishes the disc's sensitivity against a known capacitive
ignition energy; it is referenced by the calibration regime (see
[[spark-test-calibration]]) and reused as a step in the trouble-shooting list (see
[[spark-test-sensitivity-actions]]).
