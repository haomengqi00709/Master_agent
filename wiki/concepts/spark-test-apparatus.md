# Spark-test apparatus

The apparatus specified by IEC 79-3: a **contact mechanism in an explosion
chamber** that produces make-sparks and break-sparks in the prescribed
[[explosive-test-mixture|explosive test mixture]] ([[iec-79-3]] §4.2). The chamber
has a **volume of at least 250 cm³** (see [[explosion-chamber]]). An example of a
practical design is shown in the standard's Figure 1, with the contact mechanism
detailed in Figures 2–4.

Its two electrodes are deliberately asymmetric to generate energetic, repeatable
sparks: one is a **rotating slotted [[cadmium-disc-electrode|cadmium disc]]**
(Fig. 2), the other a **[[tungsten-wire-electrode|holder carrying four 0,2 mm
tungsten wires]]** (Fig. 3) that slide over the disc as both rotate (Fig. 4) — see
[[contact-mechanism]] for the geometry, drive, gear ratio and revolutions. A
**counting device** records the number of revolutions of the motor-driven wire
holder; alternatively a timing device fixes the test duration, from which the
revolution count is calculated. It is advantageous to stop the motor (or at least
the counter) automatically after an ignition, e.g. via a photocell or an
explosion-pressure switch (Figs 5–6). The chamber must withstand at least
1 500 kPa, and the connection terminals must meet tight residual-reactance limits
(see [[terminal-electrical-characteristics]]). Calibration is per IEC 79-11 9.1.3
(see [[spark-test-calibration]]); applicability limits are in
[[spark-test-apparatus-limitations]].
