# Illuminated push-button modes of use

**IEC 73 §6.3.** For [[illuminated-push-button]]s of types **a** and **b**
([[illuminated-push-button-types]]) two principal modes are distinguished — the
same indication/confirmation pair as for indicator lights
([[indicator-light-modes-of-use]]), but defined by the **lighting-then-pressing
order**:

1. **Indication (§6.3.1).** A button is **lighted to show** the operator that he
   may or should press it (or should perform a task and then press it). Reception
   or execution of the order is confirmed by the **extinction** of the light.
   *Sequence: first the button lights up, then push the button.* Colours YELLOW,
   GREEN and BLUE are frequently used. A [[flashing-light]] may attract attention
   (e.g. an alarm); pressing may then change the flash to a steady light, and the
   button may additionally carry a Clause-5 function (but no other) — the steady
   light stays on until the cause of the alarm is eliminated by a separate action.

2. **Confirmation (§6.3.1, item 2).** An **unlighted** button is pressed and then
   **lights up** to confirm the order has been received or executed; it stays lit
   until a contrary order follows. *Sequence: first push the button, then it lights
   up.* Colour WHITE is frequently used. A WHITE button may give a
   **"double-confirmation"** with a flashing light: pressing starts the flash
   (confirming a starting operation/sequence/transition has begun); when complete,
   it automatically goes **steady** to confirm normal running conditions
   ([[indicator-light-white]]).

**§6.3.2 — type c** buttons may be used in **both** modes, but each application
must be carefully examined to prevent confusion.
