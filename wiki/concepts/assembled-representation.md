# Assembled representation

One of three methods of **arrangement of symbols** in [[iec-113-1]] §8, the part of
the *classification according to method of representation* (Section Three) that
distinguishes diagrams by how the symbols for the parts of an item of apparatus are
arranged on the diagram. In assembled representation, **the symbols for the
different parts of an item of apparatus, or of an installation or equipment, are
drawn in close proximity on the diagram** (§8.1).

It is the most compact of the three arrangements. It contrasts with
[[semi-assembled-representation]] (§8.2), where the parts are separated so that
mechanical-linkage symbols between co-operating parts can be drawn easily, and with
[[detached-representation]] (§8.3), where the parts are separated and arranged so
that the circuits may easily be followed. These arrangement methods are independent
of the conductor-count methods of §7 ([[single-line-representation]],
[[multi-line-representation]]) and of [[topographical-representation]] (§9), and per
the Note to §7–9 several methods may be combined on the same diagram (see
[[mixed-diagram]]). The 1971 revision renamed the French term from *représentation
rassemblée* to *représentation assemblée* (§Preface).
