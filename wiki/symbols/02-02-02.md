**Standard:** [[60617-2]] · **Chapter II — Qualifying symbols** · **Section:** [[60617-2-02-current-voltage]]

## Description
Worked example: direct current, three conductors including mid-wire, 220 V (110 V between each outer conductor and mid-wire), shown as “2M — 220/110 V”. 2M may be replaced by 2+M.
