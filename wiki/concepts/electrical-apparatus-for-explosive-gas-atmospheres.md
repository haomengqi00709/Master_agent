# Electrical apparatus for explosive gas atmospheres

**Electrical apparatus in conformity with one or more parts of IEC 79**
([[iec-79-3]] §3.5). The note to the definition states that the parts of IEC 79
containing requirements or recommendations for electrical apparatus are listed in
Annex B (the bibliography) — i.e. the type-of-protection parts such as IEC 79-1
("d"), 79-2 ("p"), 79-7 ("e"), 79-11 ("i") and 79-15 ("n").

The term frames the subject matter of the whole family: apparatus intended for
use where an [[explosive-gas-atmosphere]] may occur, each part of IEC 79
specifying a recognised method of protection. IEC 79-3 sits one level down — it
does not protect apparatus directly but provides the **spark-test apparatus** by
which circuits claiming [[type-of-protection-i|intrinsic safety "i"]] are verified
against IEC 79-11. The same umbrella term governs the repair-and-overhaul scope of
the sibling standard [[iec-79-19]].
