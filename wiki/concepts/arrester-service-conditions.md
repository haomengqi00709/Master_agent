# Service conditions (§4.4)

[[iec-99-4]] §4.4 defines the conditions under which a conforming arrester must
operate. **Normal service conditions (§4.4.1):**
- a) Ambient air temperature within **−40 °C to +40 °C**.
- b) Solar radiation — its maximum effect (1,1 kW/m²) is taken into account in the
  type tests by preheating the test specimen; other nearby heat sources are
  subject to manufacturer/purchaser agreement.
- c) Altitude **not exceeding 1 000 m**.
- d) Power-supply frequency **not less than 48 Hz and not exceeding 62 Hz** (see
  [[arrester-rated-frequency]]).
- e) Power-frequency voltage applied continuously **not exceeding the
  [[continuous-operating-voltage]]** (U_c).
- f) Mechanical conditions — under consideration.
- g) Pollution conditions — no requirement at this time.

**Abnormal service conditions (§4.4.2, Annex A):** arresters subject to other
application/service conditions may need special design, manufacture or application,
subject to manufacturer/purchaser agreement. Annex A (normative) lists typical
abnormal conditions: temperatures beyond ±40 °C; altitude above 1 000 m;
corrosive fumes/vapours; excessive contamination (smoke, dirt, salt spray);
excessive moisture/humidity/steam; live washing; explosive dust/gas mixtures;
abnormal mechanical conditions (earthquakes, vibration, high wind, ice, cantilever
stress); unusual transport/storage; nominal frequencies below 48 Hz or above
62 Hz; and nearby heat sources. These map to the system/service data a purchaser
supplies at enquiry (Annex G).
