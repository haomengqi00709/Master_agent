# 13-05-15 — Zm-output

![13-05-15](../assets/60617-13/p11.png)
<sub>This symbol appears on Publication No.617-13.pdf, printed p.19 (full page shown).</sub>

**Standard:** [[60617-13]] · **Section:** [[60617-13-05-qualifying-symbols-indicating-the]]

## Description
Zm-output. Affecting analogue Zm-inputs/outputs impose their signal levels on the analogue inputs/outputs affected by them.

## Names
- **EN:** Zm-output
- **FR:** Sortie Zm

## Notes
- These symbols imply dependency notation (replace m by the identifying number); see IEC 617-12, Sections 11, 12, 25.
- For affecting digital Zm-inputs/outputs, see IEC 617-12, Section 17.
