# Rated normal current (I_r)

The rated normal current is the r.m.s. value of current the circuit-breaker can carry continuously at rated frequency without its temperature rise exceeding the limits of Table V of IEC Publication 694. Sub-clauses 4.4.1 and 4.4.2 of IEC 694 apply, with additions in [[iec-56]] 4.4. Values shall be selected from the standard R 10 series:
- 400 A; 630 A; 800 A; 1250 A; 1600 A; 2000 A; 2500 A; 3150 A; 4000 A; 5000 A; 6300 A.
Higher values, if required, are also taken from the R 10 series. Where a series-connected accessory such as a direct over-current release is fitted, its rated normal current is the r.m.s. current it carries continuously without deterioration. Carrying capability is verified against the [[rated-short-time-withstand-current]] (4.5) and temperature-rise limits.
