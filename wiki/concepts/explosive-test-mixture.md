# Explosive test mixture

The **explosive mixture used in the testing of intrinsically-safe circuits**
([[iec-79-3]] §3.4) — a controlled, reproducible representation of an
[[explosive-gas-atmosphere]] with which the [[explosion-chamber]] is filled
before a spark test. IEC 79-3 itself defines the term; the **specific mixtures
for each apparatus group are prescribed in IEC 79-11, sub-clause 9.1.2**, and the
test mixture appropriate to the apparatus group under test is chosen accordingly
(see [[spark-ignition-test-principle]], §4.1).

The mixture's sensitivity is what the apparatus's calibration is referenced to.
The Annex A conditioning procedure uses the **Group IIA (or Group I)** mixture and
records the lowest ignition voltage as the sensitivity benchmark (see
[[cadmium-disc-conditioning]]). When the apparatus's sensitivity drifts, checking
the **composition of the explosive test mixture** is one of the first recommended
actions (Annex A.3, see [[spark-test-sensitivity-actions]]). Group I corresponds
to mining (methane) applications; Group IIA to a representative industrial gas.
