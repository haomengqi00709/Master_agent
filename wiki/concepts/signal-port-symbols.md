# Symbols for signal ports (Annex A, normative)

**Annex A forms an integral part of IEC 387.** It defines example symbols for the
**signal ports** of a meter — the communication interfaces used for reading and
programming, especially on [[static-energy-meter|static meters]] (a proposal for
their marking is announced in §10). Four port types are illustrated (A.1–A.4):

- **A.1 Optical port, bidirectional** — an optical (infrared) read/program
  interface.
- **A.2 Inductive port, bidirectional** — an inductively coupled interface.
- **A.3 Galvanical port, unidirectional** — a direct electrical-contact interface.
- **A.4 Port in accordance with a specific standard** — e.g. TEMEX, ISDN, etc.

A NOTE defines the **directions of communication** shown by the accompanying
arrows: **output** (e.g. reading), **input** (e.g. programming), a **continuous
connection**, and a **connection on demand only** (e.g. password- or
switch-activated). These complement the data-exchange product standard **IEC 1107**
(direct local data exchange for meter reading, tariff and load control) listed in
the TC 13 back-matter.
