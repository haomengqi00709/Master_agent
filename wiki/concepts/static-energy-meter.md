# Static energy meter

An **energy meter in which current and voltage act on solid-state (electronic)
elements to produce an output pulse frequency proportional to the energy**
(IEC 387 §3.2). It is the electronic counterpart of the [[induction-meter]];
both are covered by IEC 387, whose symbols apply independent of the measurement
element used (§1).

Several IEC 387 provisions are specific to static meters. The
[[meter-constant]] (§7.3) is given in **pulses per measuring unit** — e.g.
500 imp/kWh or 2 Wh/imp — rather than rotor revolutions. Static meters rely on
the digital-information chain defined in §3.11–3.13: a
[[meter-memory-display-register|memory, display and register]] (a single display
may serve multiple electronic memories to form multiple registers). Auxiliary
provisions also single them out: §10.3 covers the **auxiliary supply voltage for
a static energy meter** when separated from the measuring voltage (e.g.
U_a = 100 V, 50 Hz) — see [[auxiliary-device-symbols]]. The static maximum-demand
display symbols 9.6–9.10 ([[tariff-device-symbols]]) are likewise "assigned for
static maximum demand devices with a display."

Related TC 13 product standards: IEC 687 (classes 0,2 S / 0,5 S) and IEC 1036
(classes 1 and 2). General graphical equivalent: [[08-04-03]] / [[08-01-03]].

See also: [[induction-meter]] · [[meter-constant]] · [[iec-387]] §3.2.
