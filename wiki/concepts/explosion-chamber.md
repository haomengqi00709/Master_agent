# Explosion chamber

The gas-filled enclosure that houses the [[contact-mechanism]] of the
[[spark-test-apparatus]]. It has a **volume of at least 250 cm³** ([[iec-79-3]]
§4.2) and is filled with the appropriate [[explosive-test-mixture|explosive test
mixture]] so that the make- and break-sparks of the electrodes act on a known
explosive atmosphere ([[explosive-gas-atmosphere]]).

Because an actual ignition produces a pressure rise, the **chamber shall be
designed for an explosion pressure of at least 1 500 kPa (15 bar)**. The base-plate
carries **gas-tight bearings** (unless a gas-flow system is used) and the
insulated shaft supports; gas inlet and outlet ports admit and (if flowing) renew
the mixture. To capture an ignition event the apparatus may use an
**explosion-pressure switch** (standard's Figure 5) wired into an automatic-stop
circuit (Figure 6), or a photocell, so the drive or counter halts the moment the
mixture ignites — fixing the revolution count at which ignition occurred.
