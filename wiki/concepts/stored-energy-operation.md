# Stored-energy operation

An operation by means of energy stored in the mechanism itself prior to the switching operation and sufficient to complete the specified operating cycle under predetermined conditions ([[iec-56]] 3.104.11, IEV 441-16-15). The energy (commonly a charged spring, or compressed gas or hydraulic fluid in the [[operating-mechanism]]) is accumulated in advance, then released on command so the closing or opening speed is independent of the operator. This guarantees a defined making speed, allowing a rated short-circuit making current to be assigned to the [[circuit-breaker]]. It underlies fast auto-reclosing duty, and contrasts with [[dependent-manual-operation]], where speed depends on the operator. Compare [[independent-manual-operation]], a stored-energy variant in which a manual action charges and then releases the store.
