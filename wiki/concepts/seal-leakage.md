# Seal leakage (§5.5, §8.1d)

Seal leakage addresses the integrity of the arrester's sealed housing. The
requirement (§5.5) is simply that **arrester units with sealed housings shall
exhibit no measurable leakage** ([[iec-99-4]] §5.5). A sealed housing keeps moisture
out of the [[metal-oxide-resistor|metal-oxide resistors]] and their internal
medium; ingress would alter the [[continuous-current]] and risk premature ageing or
[[thermal-runaway]].

It is verified as a **[[arrester-routine-test|routine test]]** (§8.1d): for each
sealed-housing [[arrester-unit|unit]], a leakage check is made by any sensitive
method adopted by the manufacturer. It also appears in the [[arrester-type-test]]
list as item 9 — the *seal leakage test* "not yet established" (§7.1) — i.e. a
formal type-test procedure was still to be defined when the standard was issued. It
is one of the "no measurable / manufacturer's-method" requirements alongside
[[partial-discharge-test|partial discharge]] and contact-noise checks.
