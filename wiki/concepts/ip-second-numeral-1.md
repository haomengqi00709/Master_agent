# IP second numeral 1 — vertically dripping water

Second characteristic numeral **1** of the [[ip-code-structure|IP Code]]
([[iec-529]], 1st ed. Table II; 2nd ed. clause 6). Protection against
**vertically falling drops**: *"dripping water (vertically falling drops) shall
have no harmful effect"* on the enclosure in its normal position.

**Test ([[ip-second-numeral-tests]], 1st ed. §8.1):** the **drip box / drip
apparatus** (Figure 3) produces a uniform rainfall of **3 mm to 5 mm of water per
minute** over the whole area. The equipment under test is placed in its **normal
operating position** under the drip apparatus (whose base is larger than the test
item; wall/ceiling-mounted equipment is fixed to a board of equal contact-surface
dimensions). The **test duration is 10 min**. After the test the enclosure is
inspected per §8.9: in general any water that entered must not impair operation,
reach unprotected live parts/windings, or accumulate at/enter cable ends.

This is the gentlest water level. Subsumes [[ip-second-numeral-0]]; next is
[[ip-second-numeral-2]] (dripping when tilted 15°).
