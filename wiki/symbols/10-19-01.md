# 10-19-01 — Modulator, demodulator or discriminator, general symbol

![10-19-01](../assets/60617-10/p39.png)

**Standard:** [[60617-10]] · **Section:** [[60617-10-19-modulators-demodulators-discriminators]]

## Description
Modulator, demodulator or discriminator, general symbol. Note. – This symbol is used as follows. Letters and input and output lines have been added in the figure for the purpose of explanation: a and b represent the modulating or modulated signal input and the modulated or demodulated signal output; c represents the input of the carrier-wave if required. Qualifying symbols may be placed inside or outside the symbol as shown below.
