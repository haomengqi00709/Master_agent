# Current impulse withstand tests (Type test, §63)

The **current impulse withstand tests** of [[iec-99-1]] §63 prove that the arrester survives the
high-magnitude and long-duration [[discharge-current|discharge currents]] it may carry, without
puncture or flashover of the [[non-linear-series-resistor|non-linear resistors]] or significant damage
to the [[series-gap|gaps]] or grading circuit. They are made (per §55, §59) on **three new samples**
of complete arresters, sections, or (for §63.3.3) resistor elements only; sample rating at least 3 kV,
need not exceed 6 kV. There are two sub-tests:

**§63.2 High-current impulse test.** Two **4/10** current impulses are applied to each sample at a peak
fixed by class: **100 kA** for 10 000 A light/heavy-duty, **65 kA** for 5 000 A Series A/B, **25 kA**
for 2 500 A, **10 kA** for 1 500 A. Tolerances: 90–110 % of peak, 3.5–4.5 µs front, 9–11 µs tail. The
average dry [[power-frequency-sparkover-voltage|power-frequency sparkover]] is measured before and
after; it must not change by more than 10 %, and the resistors must show no puncture or flashover.

**§63.3 Long-duration current impulse test.** Twenty discharge operations (four groups of five;
50–60 s between operations, 25–30 min between groups) from a **distributed-constant generator**
(Appendix E) deliver substantially rectangular current impulses. For **heavy-duty 10 000 A** arresters
(§63.3.2) the generator is first calibrated to one of **five long-duration discharge classes** (Table
III), each defined by a load-resistor value Rₛ, virtual duration of peak (2 000–3 200 µs) and charging
voltage Uₛ; the long-duration discharge class is marked on the rating plate. For **10 000 A light-duty
and 5 000 A / 2 500 A** arresters (§63.3.3) no generator demonstration is required and the test is made
on the resistor elements only, at the peak current and virtual duration of Table IV (e.g. 150 A /
2 000 µs for 10 000 A light-duty). Before/after [[power-frequency-sparkover-voltage|sparkover]] and
[[residual-voltage]] at nominal current must not change by more than 10 %.
