# Short-circuit (making and breaking) test

The short-circuit making and breaking tests prove the circuit-breaker can make and break all symmetrical and asymmetrical currents up to its [[rated-short-circuit-breaking-current]] at rated voltage. Per [[iec-56]] 6.102, this is deemed proved if the breaker makes and breaks the specified three-phase currents between 10% and 100% of rated, with single-phase duty at U/sqrt(3) also required for earthed-neutral or single-pole use. Tests may be direct or synthetic (6.102.4), and three-phase performance is preferably proved in three-phase circuits; single-pole, unit and multi-part testing (6.102.1, 6.102.3) are permitted within facility limits. Operation uses minimum operating voltage/pressure (6.102.2). After each [[test-duty]] the breaker must still carry and switch rated normal current (6.102.8). The detailed quantities, procedure and standardised duties are in [[short-circuit-test-quantities]], [[short-circuit-test-procedure]] and [[test-duty]] ([[iec-56]] 6.102-6.106).
