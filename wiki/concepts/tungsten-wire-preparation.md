# Preparation of tungsten wires (Annex A.1)

Practical advice for preparing the [[tungsten-wire-electrode|tungsten wires]]
([[iec-79-3]] Annex A, clause A.1, informative). Tungsten is **very brittle** and
the wires often tend to **split at the ends** after a relatively short period of
operation; two procedures are recommended to resolve this:

- **a) Fusion.** Divide the tungsten wire by fusion in a simple device (standard's
  Figure 7): a small **sphere** forms on each end, which is easily removed by
  slight pressure with tweezers. Prepared this way, experience shows the **life of
  the tungsten wires generally exceeds 25 000 revolutions of the wire holder**.
- **b) Shearing.** Cut the tungsten wire with a **shearing action** — for example
  using heavy-duty scissors in good condition.

Wire preparation feeds directly into the conditioning of a new
[[cadmium-disc-electrode|cadmium disc]] (Annex A.2 step c fits freshly prepared
wires — see [[cadmium-disc-conditioning]]) and into the sensitivity
trouble-shooting list (Annex A.3 inspects the wire ends, straightens and cleans
them with abrasive cloth, or fits new wires — see
[[spark-test-sensitivity-actions]]).
