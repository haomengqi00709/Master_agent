# Marking of the measured quantity (clause 6)

IEC 387 §6 governs how the measured-quantity symbol is placed on a meter. The
symbol of clause 5 ([[meter-unit-symbols]]) **indicating the nominal quantity
measured shall be marked conspicuously on the dial** of the meter
([[meter-name-plate-and-dial]]). Other appropriate symbols may be marked on the
name-plate or dial provided they do not prevent clear reading of the latter. When
the meter is intended for special conditions and/or a different power-factor
range, the appropriate symbol should be used.

Two power-factor rules are specific to reactive and apparent meters:

- **Reactive (induction) meters and leading/lagging PF** — if an
  [[induction-meter]] for reactive energy is adjusted to measure under **leading**
  PF only, or **lagging** PF only, the normal rotor rotation (viewed from the
  front) is **left to right**, and the register is marked with the leading or
  lagging arrow symbol as appropriate. If adjusted for **both** conditions, the
  rotation under lagging conditions is left to right, and the two registers are
  marked with the lagging/leading arrows respectively, close to each register.
- **Apparent energy with limited cos φ** — if the meter measures apparent energy
  with determined **limiting values of power factor**, these values shall be
  marked **in brackets after the unit symbol** (see [[volt-ampere-hour-meter-def]]).

two registers (kvarh); an apparent-energy meter (kVAh); an apparent-energy meter
for a limited cos φ range, e.g. kVAh (0,5 … 0,9 ind) with cos φ = 0,5 … 0,9
inductive; and the working range of a reactive-energy meter.
