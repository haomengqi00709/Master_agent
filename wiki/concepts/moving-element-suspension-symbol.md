# Symbols for details of the suspension of the moving element (clause 11)

IEC 387 §11 and **Table 8** cover markings describing how the moving element (the
rotating disc/rotor) of a meter is suspended or bearing-relieved. The example
given (item 8.1 of Table 8) is the symbol for a **magnet for partial relief of the
pressure of the rotor against the bottom bearing** — a magnetic bearing-relief
device that reduces friction on the lower jewel bearing of an
[[induction-meter]], improving low-load accuracy and bearing life.

This clause is specific to rotating-disc meters: it concerns the mechanical
suspension of the moving element, unlike the purely electronic
[[static-energy-meter]] which has no moving rotor. It complements the rotor-related
auxiliary devices in clause 10 (the rotor clamp, 10.2) — see
[[auxiliary-device-symbols]].
