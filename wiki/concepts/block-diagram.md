# Block diagram

The first type of [[explanatory-diagram]] in [[iec-113-1]] (§3.1). A **relatively
simple diagram to facilitate the understanding of the principle of operation**. It
is a diagram in which an installation or equipment, together with its functional
interrelationships, is represented by symbols, **block symbols or pictures**,
**without necessarily showing all the connections** (§3.1).

The block diagram is the least detailed of the three explanatory types: it conveys
the *principle* of operation and the functional relationships at a glance, leaving
out the full connection detail. That detail is the province of the
[[circuit-diagram]] (§3.2), which shows the electrical connections and other links
by symbols and is intended to facilitate understanding of the functioning *in
detail*; and of the [[equivalent-circuit-diagram]] (§3.3) for analysis and
calculation. Because a block diagram need not show all connections, it is well
suited to early-stage explanation and overview, complemented by an
[[explanatory-chart-or-table]] (§4) where operational sequence or additional
information is needed. The block symbols it uses are drawn from the [[60617-1]]
symbol family.
