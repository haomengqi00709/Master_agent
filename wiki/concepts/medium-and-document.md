# Medium, document and documentation (IEC 1082 basic terms)

The base vocabulary on which the whole of [[iec-1082]] rests, defined in
**IEC 1082-1 §2.1.1** (basic terms; their interrelation is shown in Part 1's
figure 1).

- **Medium (§2.1.1.1)** — the *material on which information is recorded*, for
  example paper, microform, or magnetic / optical disk. The medium is the
  physical carrier, distinct from the information it bears.
- **Document (§2.1.1.2)** — *information on a data medium*. A document is normally
  **designated by the type of information and the form of presentation** — e.g.
  "overview diagram", "connection table", "function chart". Part 1 notes that the
  information may appear **statically** (paper, microform) or **dynamically** on a
  (video) display device, so a document is not tied to paper.
- **Drawing, technical (§2.1.1.3)** — a *document presenting information in a
  graphical manner, which may include text*. A drawing is therefore a kind of
  document; the [[diagram]], plan and map are particular graphical drawings.
- **Documentation (§2.1.1.4)** — has **two meanings**: (1) a *collection of
  documents related to a given subject*, and (2) the *processing of documents*.

This naming rule — type-of-information × [[forms-of-presentation|form-of-presentation]]
— is what generates the document names used across the rest of the standard
(overview diagram, circuit diagram, connection table, parts list, etc.). The full
catalogue of document types built on these terms is the
[[document-classification-iec1082]].
