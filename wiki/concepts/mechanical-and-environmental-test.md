# Mechanical and environmental test

Mechanical and environmental tests prove that the circuit-breaker operates reliably over its rated supply-voltage, pressure and climatic range. Per [[iec-56]] 6.101, the core mechanical operation test (6.101.2) is 2000 no-load operating cycles per Table XII at minimum, rated and maximum control voltage/operating pressure, with no maintenance or adjustment allowed (lubrication excepted). Operating characteristics — closing/opening times, speeds, time spreads, recharging time, tightness, main-circuit resistance — are recorded before and after (6.101.1.2). Environmental tests, made by agreement, include low/high-temperature tests at T1 (minus 25 C or minus 40 C) and 40 C with 24 h soaks plus 50 close/50 open operations (6.101.3), a humidity test of 350 cycles between 25 C and 40 C above 95% RH (6.101.4), severe-ice operation (6.101.5) and static terminal-load tests for outdoor breakers at or above 52 kV (6.101.6). See [[type-test]], [[short-circuit-test]].

**Amended (1992, Amd. 1):** [[iec-56-amendment-1]] adds a sixth indent to 6.101.1.3 (open-position insulating properties verified by visual inspection, condition checking test per IEC 694 6.1.11 mandatory for sealed-for-life interrupters); revises the low-temperature test 6.101.3.3 (record at (20 ± 5) °C with tightness per EE1.4; heater-disconnect duration t_h verifying alarms/lock-out, defaulting to 2 h) and high-temperature test 6.101.3.4 (item m mirrors low-temp item b); adds accumulated-leakage limits over both temperature sequences (must not reach lock-out pressure without gas replenishment); and amends Appendix EE EE1.4.1.1 tightness-test acceptance (temporary leakage ≤ 3× F_p, must reset, no lock-out over the mechanical endurance test).
