# Main circuit

The main circuit is all the conductive parts of a circuit-breaker included in the circuit which it is designed to close and open ([[iec-56]] 3.103.2, IEV 441-15-02). It is the principal current-carrying path through each [[pole]], comprising the terminals, conductors and contacts that make and break the load and fault current, as distinct from the auxiliary and control circuits (3.103.3, 3.103.4) which serve signalling and operating functions and do not normally carry the main current. Within the main circuit the [[main-contact]] (3.103.7) carries the continuous current in the closed position, while the [[arcing-contact]] (3.103.8) is the part on which the arc is established during interruption. The main circuit thus defines the power-frequency conducting path whose making, carrying and breaking capacity is the essential duty of the [[circuit-breaker]].
