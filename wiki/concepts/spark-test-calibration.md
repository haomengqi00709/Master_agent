# Calibration of the spark-test apparatus

The [[spark-test-apparatus]]'s **sensitivity must be checked using a calibration
circuit, before and after each series of tests** ([[iec-79-3]] §4.3). IEC 79-3
defers the method to the normative reference: **IEC 79-11, sub-clause 9.1.3,
specifies how the sensitivity is to be verified**. Bracketing every test series
with a calibration guards against the apparatus drifting out of specification (for
example through electrode wear) and silently passing a circuit that should fail.

Calibration is distinct from, but related to, the **Annex A conditioning** of a
new [[cadmium-disc-electrode|cadmium disc]] (see [[cadmium-disc-conditioning]]),
which brings a fresh disc up to the required sensitivity, and from the
**trouble-shooting list** in Annex A.3 used when sensitivity is found to be out of
specification (see [[spark-test-sensitivity-actions]]). Those Annex procedures
also direct the user back to the apparatus parameters in §4.2 — the
[[terminal-electrical-characteristics|terminal C/R/L limits]] — and to the
[[explosive-test-mixture|test-mixture]] composition as checks.
