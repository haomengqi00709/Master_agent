# Sequence chart or table

The first example of an [[explanatory-chart-or-table]] in [[iec-113-1]] (§4.1). It
**gives the successive operations in a specified order** (§4.1). It is therefore a
[[chart-graph]] of the "different operations" kind (§2.2(a)): it records *which*
operations occur and in *what order*, without reference to how long each takes or
the intervals between them.

It is the simpler of the two §4 examples. Adding the time dimension — the intervals
between successive operations — yields the [[time-sequence-chart-or-table]] (§4.2),
which corresponds to the "operations and time" case of the chart definition
(§2.2(b)). Like any chart in IEC 113-1 it may be presented as a
[[table-document]] (§2.2), and its explanatory purpose is to facilitate the study
of the related [[explanatory-diagram]]s and to give additional information (§4).
