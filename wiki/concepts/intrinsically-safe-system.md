# Intrinsically-safe system

An **assembly of interconnected items of apparatus** — which may comprise
intrinsically-safe apparatus, associated apparatus and other apparatus, together
with the interconnecting cables — in which the circuits within those parts of the
system that may be exposed to an explosive gas atmosphere are
[[intrinsically-safe-circuit|intrinsically-safe circuits]] ([[iec-79-3]] §3.2).

The distinction matters for the spark test because intrinsic safety is a
**system property**, not merely a component one: associated apparatus (e.g.
barriers, isolators located in the safe area) bound the energy that can reach the
hazardous-area circuits, and the test applies to the circuit *as configured in the
system*. Cable parameters (capacitance, inductance) and fault combinations across
the interconnection are part of what IEC 79-11 prescribes when the circuit
parameters are set for testing on the [[spark-test-apparatus]].
