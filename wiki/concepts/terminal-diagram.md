# Terminal diagram

The third subtype of [[wiring-diagram]] in [[iec-113-1]] (§5.3). A **diagram showing
the terminals and the internal and/or external conductors connected to them**
(§5.3). It focuses on the connection points themselves — typically the terminal
blocks of an equipment — and the conductors landing on each terminal.

It complements the other two wiring-diagram subtypes by scope: the
[[unit-wiring-diagram]] (§5.1) covers all connections within a unit, the
[[interconnection-diagram]] (§5.2) the connections between units, and the terminal
diagram the terminals and their conductors. As a [[wiring-diagram]] it guides the
making and checking of connections (§5), and the §5 note allows it to be replaced
or supplemented by a [[table-document]] (a terminal/wiring table). The 1971
revision dropped the older alternative term *terminal connection diagram* and
renamed the French *schéma de raccordement aux bornes* to *schéma des bornes*
(§Preface).
