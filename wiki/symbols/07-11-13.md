# 07-11-13 — One pole of a six-position multi-pole switch

## Description
One pole of a six-position multi-pole switch; the pole shown makes earlier than the other poles from position 2 to 3 and breaks later than the other poles from position 5 to 6.

- **EN:** One pole of a six-position multi-pole switch
