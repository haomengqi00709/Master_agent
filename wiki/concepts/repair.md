# Repair

Action to restore a **damaged or worn** apparatus to a serviceable condition.
Defined in [[iec-79-19]] (clause 2.2). For Ex apparatus, a repair must preserve
the type of protection — see the per-type requirements [[type-of-protection-d]],
[[type-of-protection-e]], [[type-of-protection-i]], [[type-of-protection-p]],
[[type-of-protection-n]]. Related: [[overhaul]] (restore to *fully* serviceable),
[[maintenance]] (routine, preventive), [[reclamation]] (restore dimensions by
adding/removing material), [[modification]] (design change).
