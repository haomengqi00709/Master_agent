# Table

The third primary document type of [[iec-113-1]]. Per §2.2, a **table replaces or
supplements a [[diagram]] or a [[chart-graph]]**. Rather than being a distinct
subject matter, the table is a *form* in which the same information can be
presented — so most purpose classes have a table counterpart: an
[[explanatory-chart-or-table]] (§4), a wiring **table** (any of the
[[wiring-diagram]] forms can be replaced or supplemented by a table, Note to §5),
and a [[location-diagram-or-table]] (§6).

Tables carry special weight in this 2nd edition (1971). The Preface records that,
relative to Publication 113 (1959), *"tables will in future replace diagrams and
charts to an increasing extent"* — they were therefore given more emphasis in the
text and added to the title (*Diagrams, charts, tables*). This explains why so
many of the purpose classes are named "... diagram **or table**" or "... chart
**or table**": IEC 113-1 treats the tabular form as a first-class equivalent to the
graphical one for conveying connection, sequence and location information.
