# Making and breaking capacity

Making and breaking capacity is the short-circuit performance the [[short-circuit-test]] verifies: the ability to make and break all symmetrical and asymmetrical currents up to the [[rated-short-circuit-breaking-current]] at rated voltage. Per [[iec-56]] 6.102.1, this is demonstrated by making and breaking the specified three-phase currents between 10% (or lower critical currents per 6.107) and 100% of rated at rated voltage, plus single-phase duty at U/sqrt(3) for earthed-neutral or single-pole application. Proof is provided through the standardised [[test-duty]] series of 6.106 under the quantities of [[short-circuit-test-quantities]]. After a duty the breaker must still make and break its rated normal current though its short-circuit capacity may be impaired (6.102.8.2) ([[iec-56]] 6.102).
