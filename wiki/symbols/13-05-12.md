# 13-05-12 — Adjustment terminal, shown on the left-hand side

![13-05-12](../assets/60617-13/p10.png)

**Standard:** [[60617-13]] · **Section:** [[60617-13-05-qualifying-symbols-indicating-the]]

## Description
Adjustment terminal (A*). Replace A* by ADJ or the asterisk by a property/quantity indication, e.g. B bias, CL current limit, f frequency, H hysteresis, OFS offset, P power, SR slew rate, SYM symmetry, T temperature, U/V voltage, WF waveform, Z impedance, φ phase.
