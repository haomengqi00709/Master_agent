# Routine and acceptance tests (§67–§68)

IEC 99-1 distinguishes three test categories. **[[type-test|Type tests (design tests)]]** (§45) are
made once upon completion of a new design to establish representative performance and demonstrate
compliance; they need not be repeated unless the design is changed enough to modify performance.
**Routine tests** (§46) are made on each arrester (or its parts/materials) to ensure the product meets
the design specification. **Acceptance tests** (§47) are selected tests made when agreed between
manufacturer and purchaser on the arresters, or representative samples, of an order.

**Routine tests (§67).** The minimum requirement for routine tests by the manufacturer is the **dry
[[power-frequency-sparkover-test|power-frequency voltage sparkover test]]** (per §60). If the arrester
is built from a number of self-contained units, the routine test may be made on the units.

**Standard acceptance tests (§68).** When the purchaser specifies acceptance tests in the purchase
agreement, the following are made on the **nearest higher whole number to the cube root of the number
of arresters** to be supplied:
- (a) dry [[power-frequency-sparkover-test|power-frequency voltage sparkover test]] on the complete
  arrester (§60);
- (b) standard lightning-voltage [[voltage-impulse-sparkover-test|impulse sparkover test]] on the
  complete arrester (§61.2);
- (c) **only when specifically agreed**, the [[residual-voltage-test|residual voltage test]] at the
  nominal discharge current on the complete arrester or sections (§62).

Any change in sample number or test type is specifically negotiated between manufacturer and purchaser.
