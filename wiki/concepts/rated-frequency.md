# Rated frequency

The rated frequency is the power-system frequency for which the circuit-breaker is designed and at which all rated quantities apply. Sub-clause 4.3 of IEC Publication 694 is applicable ([[iec-56]] 4.3). The standard rated frequencies are 50 Hz and 60 Hz. Rated frequency is load-bearing for several other ratings: the d.c. component of the [[rated-short-circuit-breaking-current]] is referenced to a half-cycle of rated frequency for auxiliary-power tripping (4.101.2); the [[rated-short-circuit-making-current]] multiplier is 2.5x at 50 Hz versus 2.6x at 60 Hz (4.103); and the standard [[transient-recovery-voltage]] values apply to service frequencies of 50 Hz or 60 Hz (4.102). The standard [[rated-operating-sequence]] time intervals are likewise tied to rated frequency.
