# Illuminated push-button

Defined in **IEC 73 §2.3** as *"a push-button with an actuator illuminated by (a)
lamp(s) for the purpose of giving information."* It combines the two objects of
[[iec-73]] in one device: it is both a [[push-button]] (an operation initiated by
pressing) and an [[indicator-light]] (information given by its illumination), so
**both colour codes apply at once**.

The **basic requirement** (§6.2) is that clauses 4 and 5 both govern it: the
meaning of the colour used must accord with **Table I for the lighted button** and
**Table II for the function of the button** ([[push-button-colour-table]]). Where
it is difficult to assign an appropriate colour, neutral **WHITE** shall be used.
A critical safety rule: an illuminated push-button **shall never be used as an
emergency-stop button**, because failure of the lamp could lead to erroneous
conclusions (so [[push-button-stop-emergency]] emergency-stops stay as plain RED
buttons). A lamp designed to be pushed merely for lamp-testing is *not* an
illuminated push-button (§6.1 note).

Three button types are distinguished by the colours shown when lit
([[illuminated-push-button-types]], Table III, §6.1): (a) neutral when unlit /
one colour when lit; (b) neutral / different colours by separate lamps; (c)
different colours not-lighted and lighted. Their operation follows two
[[illuminated-push-button-modes-of-use]] — indication and confirmation (§6.3),
including the WHITE "double-confirmation" flashing-then-steady sequence. See
[[flashing-light]] for the flashing conventions.
