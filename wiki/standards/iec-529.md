# IEC 529 — Degrees of protection provided by enclosures (IP Code)

*Prepared by IEC Technical Committee No. 70, "Degrees of Protection by
Enclosures", set up in 1970 to merge the requirements of IEC 34-5 (rotating
machines) and IEC 144 (LV switchgear) into one document.* This standard defines
the **IP Code** — the universal `IP` two-numeral classification of how well an
enclosure keeps **people and solid objects out** (first numeral) and **water
out** (second numeral), plus optional letters — and the **type tests** that
verify each degree. It is the standard cited whenever another publication writes
"IP54" (e.g. [[iec-79-19]] requires a minimum IP54 for cable entries in
[[type-of-protection-e]] / [[type-of-protection-n]] apparatus).

## The two source files — what 529-1 vs 529-2 are
Both OCR files are the **same standard, different editions** (not parts and not
an amendment):

| File | Publication | Title | Role |
|------|-------------|-------|------|
| `iec-529-2.txt` | **Publication 529, 1st edition, 1976** | "Classification of degrees of protection provided by enclosures" | the **original** standard |
| `iec-529-1.txt` | **IEC 529, 2nd edition, 1989-11** (2nd impression 1992) | "Degrees of protection provided by enclosures (**IP Code**)" | the **revision that supersedes** the 1st ed. |

The 2nd edition is the major rewrite that **named the system "the IP Code"**,
added the formal code-arrangement diagram, the **[[ip-additional-letter|additional
letters A–D]]**, the [[ip-access-probe|access-probe / object-probe]] vocabulary,
and rebuilt the tables and tests. The 1st edition is the cleaner OCR for the
**water tests** (clauses 8.1–8.8) and the **dust test** (clause 7.5), so those
concept pages cite it where the 1989 OCR is garbled. Where the editions differ in
wording (e.g. the 1st ed. short descriptions "protected against solid objects
greater than 12 mm" vs the 2nd ed. "protected against access… / against solid
foreign objects ≥ 12,5 mm"), the **2nd edition (IP Code) governs**.

## Scope (1st ed. clause 1)
A system for specifying enclosures of electrical equipment by the degree of
protection provided, applicable where the rated voltage does not exceed 72,5 kV.
It also specifies **type tests** to check performance. It does **not** cover
protection against mechanical damage, risk of explosion, or conditions such as
moisture/condensation, corrosive vapours, fungus or vermin; nor fences/guards
provided solely for personnel safety. Tests use **fresh water**.

## The IP Code structure
The designation is the letters **IP** followed by two characteristic numerals and
optional letters: see **[[ip-code-structure]]** for the full arrangement,
the "X" placeholder rule, and worked examples.

```
IP  2  3  C  H
    │  │  │  └─ supplementary letter (optional): H M S W
    │  │  └──── additional letter (optional): A B C D
    │  └─────── second numeral (0–8 or X): water ingress
    └────────── first numeral (0–6 or X): access to hazardous parts + solid objects
```

## First characteristic numeral — access & solid objects (clause 5)
Protection of **persons against access to hazardous parts** AND of **equipment
against ingress of solid foreign objects**. One page per level:
- [[ip-first-numeral-0]] — non-protected
- [[ip-first-numeral-1]] — back of hand / ≥ 50 mm objects
- [[ip-first-numeral-2]] — finger / ≥ 12,5 mm objects
- [[ip-first-numeral-3]] — tool / ≥ 2,5 mm objects
- [[ip-first-numeral-4]] — wire / ≥ 1,0 mm objects
- [[ip-first-numeral-5]] — dust-protected
- [[ip-first-numeral-6]] — dust-tight

## Second characteristic numeral — water (clause 6)
Protection against **harmful ingress of water**. One page per level:
- [[ip-second-numeral-0]] — non-protected
- [[ip-second-numeral-1]] — vertical dripping
- [[ip-second-numeral-2]] — dripping when tilted 15°
- [[ip-second-numeral-3]] — spraying (up to 60° from vertical)
- [[ip-second-numeral-4]] — splashing (any direction)
- [[ip-second-numeral-5]] — water jets
- [[ip-second-numeral-6]] — powerful water jets / heavy seas
- [[ip-second-numeral-7]] — temporary immersion
- [[ip-second-numeral-8]] — continuous submersion

## Optional letters
- [[ip-additional-letter]] — A/B/C/D: actual protection of persons against access
  to hazardous parts, when better than the first numeral implies.
- [[ip-supplementary-letter]] — H/M/S/W: supplementary information (high-voltage,
  motion/stationary during the water test, weather).

## Verification tests
- [[ip-access-probe]] — access probes & object probes (the test fingers, rods,
  wires, spheres) used for the first numeral.
- [[ip-first-numeral-tests]] — clause-12 / clause-7 tests for the first numeral
  (incl. the dust chamber for IP5X/IP6X).
- [[ip-second-numeral-tests]] — clause-14 / clause-8 water tests for the second
  numeral (drip box, oscillating tube, spray nozzle, hose, immersion).

## Related standards
- Supersedes/merges **IEC 34-5** (degrees of protection for rotating machines)
  and **IEC 144** (LV switchgear and controlgear). References **IEC 50** (IEV)
  and **IEC 27** (letter symbols).
- Referenced by **[[iec-79-19]]** (minimum IP54 for [[cable-and-conduit-entries]]
  in [[type-of-protection-e]] / [[type-of-protection-n]]; the [[enclosures]]
  "degree of protection" that Ex repair must preserve).
- Later renumbered **IEC 60529**; treat that as the successor edition.
