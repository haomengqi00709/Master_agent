# Volt-ampere-hour meter (apparent energy)

An **instrument intended to measure apparent energy by integrating apparent
power with respect to time** (IEC 387 §3.5, after IEV 301-04-19). It is the third
energy-type meter defined by IEC 387, complementing the active
[[watt-hour-meter-def|watt-hour]] and reactive [[var-hour-meter-def|var-hour]]
meters.

The registered unit is the **volt-ampere-hour (VAh)** / kVAh (§5.8,
[[meter-unit-symbols]]). Apparent-energy metering has a marking rule of its own:
under §6, **if the meter is intended to measure apparent energy with determined
limiting values of power factor, these values shall be marked in brackets after
the symbol for the measuring unit.** Table 3 illustrates this with an "apparent
energy meter for limited cos φ range" — e.g. kVAh (0,5 … 0,9 ind), with
cos φ = 0,5 … 0,9 inductive. See [[measured-quantity-marking]].

Because apparent power combines active and reactive components, VAh metering is
often paired with the dual active/reactive registers; the unit symbols VA (§5.7)
and the squared-quantity units V²h and A²h (§5.10–5.11) also appear in the unit
table. General graphical equivalent: integrating instrument [[08-01-03]].

See also: [[iec-387]] §3.5.
