# Circuit diagram

The second, most detailed type of [[explanatory-diagram]] in [[iec-113-1]] (§3.2);
the U.S.A. term is **schematic diagram**. It is an explanatory diagram **intended
to facilitate the understanding of the functioning in detail**. It shows, by
symbols, an installation or part of an installation and **the electrical
connections and other links concerned with its operation** (§3.2).

The circuit diagram is distinguished from the [[block-diagram]] (§3.1) by detail:
where the block diagram gives only the principle of operation and need not show all
connections, the circuit diagram shows the electrical connections and operating
links explicitly through graphical symbols. A specialised form of it, the
[[equivalent-circuit-diagram]] (§3.3), exists for the analysis and calculation of
circuit characteristics. A circuit diagram may be drawn using any of the §7–9
methods of representation — [[single-line-representation]] or
[[multi-line-representation]] for conductors (§7), and
[[assembled-representation]], [[semi-assembled-representation]] or
[[detached-representation]] for the arrangement of symbols (§8). The same drawing
may serve as both an explanatory and a [[wiring-diagram]], forming a
[[mixed-diagram]] (Note to §3–6). Note that the 1959 edition's alternative term
*elementary diagram* was dropped in this revision (§Preface).
