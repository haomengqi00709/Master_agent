# Equivalent circuit diagram

The third type of [[explanatory-diagram]] in [[iec-113-1]] (§3.3). A **special type
of [[circuit-diagram]] for the analysis and calculation of circuit
characteristics** (§3.3). Whereas the ordinary circuit diagram (§3.2) is meant to
help a reader *understand* the functioning of an installation in detail, the
equivalent circuit diagram serves a computational purpose: it represents the
circuit in a form whose characteristics can be analysed and calculated (for
example, by replacing real components with idealised equivalents).

It is one of two concepts newly **inserted** in this 1971 revision relative to
Publication 113 (1959), the other being the [[location-diagram-or-table]]
(§Preface). As a member of the [[explanatory-diagram]] family it is concerned with
explaining/analysing function, in contrast to the connection-oriented
[[wiring-diagram]] (§5) and the position-oriented [[location-diagram-or-table]]
(§6). Like other circuit diagrams it is built from [[60617-1]] symbols and may use
any of the §7–9 methods of representation.
