# Sleeve bearings — Ex reclamation

Plain bearing surfaces, reclaimed by electroplating or metal spraying.

In [[iec-79-19]], repair/overhaul/reclamation of this component is specified per type of protection:

- **[[type-of-protection-d|"d" — flameproof enclosure]]** (clause 3): Sleeve-bearing surfaces may be reclaimed by electroplating or metal spraying (3.3.3).
- **[[type-of-protection-p|"p" — pressurization]]** (clause 5): Sleeve-bearing surfaces may be built up by electroplating or metal spraying (5.3.3).
- **[[type-of-protection-e|"e" — increased safety]]** (clause 6): Sleeve-bearing surfaces may be reclaimed by electroplating or metal spraying (6.3.2).
- **[[type-of-protection-n|"n" — non-sparking]]** (clause 7): Sleeve-bearing surfaces may be built up by electroplating or metal spraying (7.3.3.2).
