# Detached representation

The third method of **arrangement of symbols** in [[iec-113-1]] §8.3. Here **the
symbols for the different parts of an item of apparatus or of an installation are
separated and arranged in such a way that the circuits may easily be followed**
(§8.3). Unlike [[semi-assembled-representation]] (§8.2), which separates the parts
so that the *mechanical-linkage* symbols can be drawn easily, detached
representation separates them to optimise *circuit legibility* — the symbols of one
functional part may be distributed across the diagram wherever that makes the
current paths clearest.

It is the most dispersed of the three §8 arrangements, the opposite extreme from
[[assembled-representation]] (§8.1), where the parts are kept in close proximity. As
with the other arrangement methods it is independent of the §7 conductor-count
methods ([[single-line-representation]], [[multi-line-representation]]) and of
[[topographical-representation]] (§9), and several methods may be combined on a
single diagram (Note to §7–9; see [[mixed-diagram]]). The 1971 revision adopted the
term *detached representation* in place of the earlier *separated representation*
(§Preface).
