# Copy winding

Process by which a winding is **totally or partially replaced by copying the
original** ([[iec-79-19]] 2.2.13) — central to repairing Ex motors without
altering their characteristics. Particularly relevant to [[type-of-protection-e]]
(increased safety) and [[type-of-protection-d]] machines. The winding itself is
drawn with symbols such as [[06-03-01]] (commutating/compensating winding) and
[[06-02-07]] (three-phase star winding). Related: [[reclamation]].
