# Tests of arrester disconnectors (Type test, §66)

The **tests of arrester disconnectors** of [[iec-99-1]] §66 verify a fitted or accessory
[[arrester-disconnector]]. They are made on arresters fitted with disconnectors, or on the
disconnector assembly alone if its design is unaffected by heating of adjacent arrester parts
(§66.1). The mounting uses the manufacturer's recommended lead, or, in its absence, hard-drawn bare
copper ~5 mm diameter and 30 cm long arranged to allow free movement when the disconnector operates.

**Withstand tests (§66.2).** The disconnector must withstand, **without operating**, three tests
(three new samples each), at the peak current/duration corresponding to the highest arrester class it
is designed for: (A) the high-current impulse test (per §63.1, §63.2; see
[[current-impulse-withstand-test]]); (B) the long-duration current impulse test (per §63.1, §63.3.1,
§63.3.3); and (C) the [[operating-duty-test]] (§64) in series with a test-sample arrester section
having the highest follow current. For built-in disconnectors these are made together with the
arrester §63/§64 tests.

**Time/current curve test (§66.3).** Data are obtained at three symmetrically initiated current levels
— **20 A, 200 A and 800 A r.m.s. (±10 %)** — flowing through sample disconnectors. The non-linear
resistors and series gaps are bypassed (with fine copper wire, 0.08–0.13 mm) to start the internal
arcing where heating matters. At least five new samples are tested at each level; the r.m.s. current
and the time to first movement are plotted and the time/current characteristic drawn as a smooth curve
through the maximum durations.

**Evaluation (§66.3.1).** There must be clear evidence of effective and permanent disconnection. If in
doubt, a power-frequency voltage of **1.2 times the rated voltage of the highest-rated associated
arrester** is applied for one minute, during which current must not exceed **1 mA r.m.s.**
