# Rated frequency of an arrester

The **rated frequency of an arrester** is the frequency of the power system on which the arrester is
designed to be used ([[iec-99-1]] §11). The **standard rated frequencies are 50 Hz and 60 Hz**
(§52). The rated frequency need appear on the [[arrester-identification|rating plate]] only if it is
other than one of these standard frequencies (§50). Frequency is bounded more widely for service and
test purposes: arresters must be suitable for supply frequencies from **48 Hz to 62 Hz** under normal
service conditions (§2 c), and all power-frequency voltage tests are made with an approximately
sinusoidal alternating voltage whose frequency lies between 48 Hz and 62 Hz (§56). The
[[operating-duty-test]] (§64) likewise connects the arrester across a power supply of 48–62 Hz.

Operation outside the 48–62 Hz band is an abnormal service condition requiring special consideration
and discussion with the manufacturer (§2, Appendix B; see also [[arrester-application-guide]]).
Frequency also enters timing of the [[operating-duty-test]]: the initiating
[[nominal-discharge-current]] impulse is timed approximately 60 electrical degrees before the power-
frequency voltage peak, retarded in ~10° steps if follow current is not established consistently.
