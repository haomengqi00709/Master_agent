# Tariff device symbols (clause 9)

IEC 387 §9 and **Table 6** cover the markings for the four tariff-related meter
types, given as examples:

- **a) Multi-rate meters** — no special symbol; the respective rates are marked
  beside the appropriate set of dials or register (examples: day, night, peak). A
  NOTE: marking more than three-rate registers is a matter for the purchase
  contract. See [[multi-rate-meter]].
- **b) Excess energy meters** — the register recording excess metering is marked
  beside it with the **triangle symbol Δ**, and the power threshold over which
  excess-load energy is registered is marked beside the symbol in a suitable unit
  (e.g. 800 W), permanently or preferably on a changeable supplementary plate.
  Table 6 shows a fixed-threshold form and an adjustable-level form. See
  [[excess-energy-meter]].
- **c) Maximum demand meters** — a single-pointer indicator needs no symbol
  (markings per **IEC 211**); cumulative indicators mark the cumulative register
  with the unit of power; indicators are marked beside the register with the
  maximum average power and the appropriate symbol. Table 6 examples: drum-type
  MDI (0,2 kW/div, integration 15 min, detent 9 s) and a pointer/drum type with a
  warning device. See [[maximum-demand-meter]].
- **d) Bidirectional meters** — each of the two sets of dials/drums is marked with
  an **arrow** in the appropriate direction (energy received = import; energy
  supplied = export); reactive bidirectional meters use a supplementary plate for
  the 9.5 symbols. See [[bidirectional-meter]].

Table 6 also assigns **symbols 9.6–9.10 for static maximum-demand devices with a
display**: 9.6 the instantaneous (actual) value of the average demand, 9.7 the
highest average demand value for the present cumulation (billing) period, and the
import/export directional symbols.
