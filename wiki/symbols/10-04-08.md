# 10-04-08 — Radar antenna rotating four times per minute in azimuth and reciprocating in elevation

## Description
Example: radar antenna rotating four times per minute (4 min⁻¹) in azimuth and reciprocating in elevation between 0°…57°…0° in 1 s.
